Sacsri (Lee Yah Hoon) is a retired South Korean Zerg player born on 18 February 1992. He joined [WeMade FOX](@/wiki/teams/wemade-fox.md) in August 2008 and played for the team in the [2009–10 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2009-2010-shinhan-bank-proleague.md). When WeMade folded in November 2011 he moved to [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md), where he stayed until April 2014.

He became a regular in the AfreecaTV-era leagues. His best result there was the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 8](@/tournaments/asl-s8.md), and he also played in [ASL Season 6](@/tournaments/asl-s6.md), [KSL Season 4](@/tournaments/ksl-s4.md), [ASL Season 10](@/tournaments/asl-s10.md), [ASL Season 13](@/tournaments/asl-s13.md), [ASL Season 14](@/tournaments/asl-s14.md), [ASL Season 15](@/tournaments/asl-s15.md) and [ASL Season 18](@/tournaments/asl-s18.md).
