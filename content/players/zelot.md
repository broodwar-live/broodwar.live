ZeLoT (Seo Mun Ji Hoon) is a South Korean Zerg born in 1993. He never played professionally in the KeSPA era and made his name instead on the amateur circuit that kept Korean Brood War alive in the early 2010s, playing under IDs such as Minus)ZeLoT.

He competed in the SOSPA Ranking Tournaments and the SonicTV Starleagues; his best run there came in the 8th SonicTV Starleague, where he made the playoff stage before losing to [Killer](@/players/killer.md). He joined SouLGaming, a team made up mostly of non-Korean players, in December 2018.

In the Afreeca era he has qualified for the main stage of the ASL twice, in [ASL Season 8](@/tournaments/asl-s8.md) in 2019 and [ASL Season 17](@/tournaments/asl-s17.md) in 2024.
