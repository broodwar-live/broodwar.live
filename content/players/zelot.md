ZeLoT (Seo Mun Ji Hoon) is a South Korean amateur Zerg player, born in 1993, who never played professionally during the KeSPA era. He has competed in SOSPA Ranking Tournaments and SonicTV Starleagues, with his best run coming in the 8th SonicTV Starleague, where he lost to Killer in the elimination stage. He joined SouLGaming in December 2018.
