RuBy (Min Chan Ki) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1989, who played for MBCGame HERO and Air Force ACE. He played for MBCGame HERO and became the ace of Air Force ACE in the 2009–10 Proleague season, qualifying for both the MSL and the OSL in 2009.

He appears in 9 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2007 and 2011.
