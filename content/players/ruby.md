RuBy (Min Chan Ki) is a retired South Korean Terran born in 1989. He played for [MBCGame HERO](@/wiki/teams/mbcgame-hero.md) from 2006 and moved to [Air Force ACE](@/wiki/teams/air-force-ace.md) for his military service in 2009, quickly becoming ACE's ace in the 2009–10 Proleague season.

He was a frequent qualifier for the individual leagues, from the [2007 GOMTV MSL Season 3](@/tournaments/2007-gomtv-msl-season-3.md) to the [2011 ABC Mart MSL](@/tournaments/2011-abc-mart-msl.md), but never advanced deep into a tournament. In 2009 he made both the [2009–2010 NATE MSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-nate-msl.md), going out in the round of 32, and the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md), going out in the round of 36.

After his discharge in 2011 he could not return to MBCGame HERO, which had disbanded. Contract talks with another team fell through, KeSPA listed him as a player who had refused a contract, and he retired to pursue acting.
