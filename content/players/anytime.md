Anytime (Oh Yeong Jong) is a South Korean former professional Protoss player, born in 1986. He played for Plus, Lecaf OZ, Air Force ACE and Hwaseung OZ between 2004 and 2011. He won the 2005 So1 OnGameNet Starleague, beating BoxeR 3–2 in the final, and returned to the OSL final in Shinhan Bank Starleague Season 2 (2006), where he finished runner-up to NaDa.

He became known for Dark Templar play, which earned him the nickname "Sasin Toss" (Death God Toss), and was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.
