Last (Kim Sung Hyun) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1992. He joined [STX SouL](@/wiki/teams/stx-soul.md) in 2008, debuted in the 2008–09 Proleague and stayed with the team into StarCraft II until it disbanded in late 2013.

He became one of the leading Terrans of the Afreeca era. He reached the semifinals of [ASL Season 1](@/tournaments/asl-s1.md), then won [KSL Season 1](@/tournaments/ksl-s1.md) in 2018 with a 4–0 final sweep of [Jaedong](@/players/jaedong.md). He followed it by winning [ASL Season 7](@/tournaments/asl-s7.md) in 2019, beating [Mini](@/players/mini.md) 3–1 in the final. He was also a semifinalist in [ASL Season 6](@/tournaments/asl-s6.md) and [KSL Season 2](@/tournaments/ksl-s2.md), and reached the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 3](@/tournaments/asl-s3.md) and [ASL Season 8](@/tournaments/asl-s8.md). Fans nicknamed him "AlphaGo".
