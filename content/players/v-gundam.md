V-Gundam (Jo Jung Hyun) is a retired South Korean Terran born in 1981, nicknamed "Bamboo Terran". In an era when Terrans walled in against Protoss as a matter of course, he did not, relying on micro instead, and he often skipped expanding in favour of more production. His two-Factory TvP build, the "Gundam rush", was the most popular TvP build on Battle.net in 2001–02.

As a teamless amateur he finished third in the [2001 Coca-Cola OSL](@/tournaments/2001-coca-cola-osl.md), losing to [YellOw](@/players/yellow.md) in the semifinals and then beating isaac. That run earned him a place at AMD Dream Team, and in 2002 he was runner-up at the second King of Kings event, again losing to YellOw.

He fell out in the group stages of the [2002 NATE OSL](@/tournaments/2002-nate-osl.md) after tiebreakers, and a broken little finger left him winless in the [2003-2004 NHN OSL](@/tournaments/2003-2004-nhn-osl.md). He left the team, by then called Hexatron, shortly afterwards.
