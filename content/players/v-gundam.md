V-Gundam (Jo Jung Hyun) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1981, who played for AMD Dream Team and Hexatron. Known for a one-base Terran style that skipped the usual wall-in against Protoss, he gave his name to the "Gundam rush" build in Terran versus Protoss.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2001 Coca-Cola OSL](/tournaments/2001-coca-cola-osl/).
