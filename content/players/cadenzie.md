CadenZie (Zoe Summers) is a Zerg player from Scotland, United Kingdom, born in 1986. She moved her career to South Korea, where she competed and streamed full-time from 2018 and was regarded as one of the strongest female Zerg players.

Her Korean career began when Rain invited her onto his team for AfreecaTV BJ Destruction Season 1 in 2018. That team won the league, and her team won Season 2 later that year as well. In September 2018 she won Lady AfreecaTV StarLeague 6. She also played in [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md), [BSL Season 10](@/tournaments/bsl-s10.md) and [BSL Season 14](@/tournaments/bsl-s14.md).

Away from her own matches she coached in seasons 3 to 5 of the Coach Pupil League and worked as a caster, including the KSL 3 qualifiers alongside Artosis. She announced her retirement on 10 December 2023.
