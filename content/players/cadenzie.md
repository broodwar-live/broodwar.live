CadenZie (Zoe Summers) is a Zerg player from Scotland, United Kingdom, who competed and streamed full-time in South Korea. She was part of the winning teams in both 2018 seasons of AfreecaTV BJ Destruction and won Lady AfreecaTV StarLeague 6 in September 2018. She has also coached in the Coach Pupil League and done commentary work. She announced her retirement on 10 December 2023.
