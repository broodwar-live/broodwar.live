Soma (Park Sang Hyun) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1995, who never played professionally in the KeSPA era. He came up through the SonicTV events, reaching the round of 16 of the 7th SonicTV Starleague and playing for the Sonic All-Stars in the 2014 Gabangpop Sonic Proleague. He joined the amateur team SouLGaming in January 2020.

He has become one of the top Zergs of the ASL. After a semifinal in [ASL Season 9](@/tournaments/asl-s9.md), he reached the final of [ASL Season 10](@/tournaments/asl-s10.md) in 2020 and lost 3–4 to [ZerO](@/players/zero.md). He was also a semifinalist in [KSL Season 4](@/tournaments/ksl-s4.md) and [ASL Season 13](@/tournaments/asl-s13.md). His breakthrough came in [ASL Season 20](@/tournaments/asl-s20.md) in 2025, which he won 4–2 over [Snow](@/players/snow.md). He then defended the title in [ASL Season 21](@/tournaments/asl-s21.md) in 2026, beating [Flash](@/players/flash.md) 4–3 in the final.
