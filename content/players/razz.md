Razz (Michael Razzoli) is an American Protoss player born in 1989, who has competed since 2023 and plays StarCraft: Remastered for team RED. He has also used the IDs Razz[light], Razz[gtb] and Ilove[pain].

He is a regular in the Bombastic StarLeague, the leading league for players outside Korea. Since debuting in [BSL Season 16](@/tournaments/bsl-s16.md) in 2023 he has played in [BSL Season 18](@/tournaments/bsl-s18.md) and every season after it: [BSL Season 19](@/tournaments/bsl-s19.md), [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md), [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md) and [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md).
