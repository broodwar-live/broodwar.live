IntoTheRain (Im Sung Choon) is a retired South Korean Protoss player born in 1979 and one of the first generation of professionals, nicknamed "Han Bang Toss" and the "God of High Templar". He turned pro in spring 2000 through the Game-Q Star League and made his name in Game-Q events, beating [BoxeR](@/players/boxer.md) 3–2 in one final and 3–0 in the GeMBC Race Wars.

Nervous in front of live crowds, he never matched that form on OnGameNet: in the [2001 Hanbitsoft OSL](@/tournaments/2001-hanbitsoft-osl.md) and the [2001 Coca-Cola OSL](@/tournaments/2001-coca-cola-osl.md) he got no further than the second round. His best premier result on this site is a semifinal in the [2002 KPGA 1st Tour](@/tournaments/2002-kpga-1st-tour.md). Regarded as a strong PvZ player for his storm use, he last played for Greatest One, then became a commentator and, in 2012, a StarCraft II coach for AZUBU.
