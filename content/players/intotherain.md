IntoTheRain (Im Sung Choon) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1979, who played for Greatest One. One of the first generation of professional Protoss players, he began his career in 2000 and mentored [RainBOw](/players/rainbow/).

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2002 KPGA 1st Tour](/tournaments/2002-kpga-1st-tour/).
