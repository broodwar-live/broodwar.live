KwarK (David Milton) is a British Protoss player who represented the United Kingdom at the World Cyber Games in 2005 and 2006. He came back to Brood War around 2019, when the [Bombastic StarLeague](@/tournaments/bsl.md) launched, and in 2020 joined the German-founded, now international team ash. (aeterna societas honoris), with which he has played in the BWCL team league.

In the BSL he has played in [BSL Season 11](@/tournaments/bsl-s11.md), [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md), [BSL Season 14](@/tournaments/bsl-s14.md), [BSL Season 16](@/tournaments/bsl-s16.md) and [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md), and made the playoffs of [BSL Season 15](@/tournaments/bsl-s15.md). He streams on Twitch and is known for well-executed strategies and a low-APM style.
