Larva (Lim Hong Gyu) is a South Korean Zerg player born in 1994. He began on [eSTRO](@/wiki/teams/estro.md), which he left when the team folded in 2010, and joined [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md) in March 2011.

His profile rose after StarCraft: Remastered launched in 2017, when his brash showing in a ZOTAC Cup Masters showmatch drew attention. That year he cleared the ASL group stage for the first time and reached the semifinals of [ASL Season 4](@/tournaments/asl-s4.md). He returned to the last four in [ASL Season 10](@/tournaments/asl-s10.md) and won [ASL Season 11](@/tournaments/asl-s11.md) in 2021, beating [Mini](@/players/mini.md) 4–3 in the final. He has also made the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 5](@/tournaments/asl-s5.md), [KSL Season 2](@/tournaments/ksl-s2.md), [ASL Season 9](@/tournaments/asl-s9.md) and [ASL Season 20](@/tournaments/asl-s20.md). One of Afreeca's most popular streamers, he cites Conor McGregor as an inspiration for his confident persona.
