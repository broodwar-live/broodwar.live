Leta (Shin Sang Moon) is a South Korean Terran born in 1989. He debuted in 2006 and spent most of his career with the OnGameNet SPARKYZ franchise, which became Hite SPARKYZ and then [Hite Entus](@/wiki/teams/hite-entus.md), before finishing with [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md) in 2013.

He made his name in Proleague, with strong TvT, a two-Starport Wraith opening against Zerg and a good record in ace matches. In the playoffs of the [2008 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2008-shinhan-bank-proleague.md) he won all three of his sets, including the only win for his team in the final, as it finished runner-up to Samsung KHAN. He went 11–2 in the second round of the 2009–10 season and passed 100 career Proleague wins in May 2010.

In individual leagues he reached the quarterfinals of the [2009 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2009-bacchus-osl.md), the [2009 Lost Saga MSL](@/tournaments/2009-lost-saga-msl.md), the [2010 Korean Air OSL Season 2](@/tournaments/2010-korean-air-osl-season-2.md) and the [2011 ABC Mart MSL](@/tournaments/2011-abc-mart-msl.md). Returning in 2018, he also made the quarterfinals of [KSL Season 2](@/tournaments/ksl-s2.md) and, in 2026, the semifinals of [ASL Season 21](@/tournaments/asl-s21.md).
