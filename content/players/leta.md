Leta (Shin Sang Moon) is a South Korean Terran player who played for OnGameNet SPARKYZ (later Hite SPARKYZ and Hite Entus) and finished his career with CJ Entus in 2013. He was a strong Proleague performer, known for his two-Starport Wraith play against Zerg and for his record in ace matches, and joined the 100-win Proleague club in May 2010. His best individual results were Ro8 finishes in the Lost Saga MSL and Bacchus OSL 2009.
