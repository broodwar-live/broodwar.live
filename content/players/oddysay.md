Oddysay (Han Woong Ryul) is a retired South Korean Terran player, born in 1981, from the early televised era. He played for KOR from 2001 and joined KTF MagicNs in May 2002, staying until 2005.

His best premier-league result is a semifinal in the [2002 NATE OSL](@/tournaments/2002-nate-osl.md). He also reached the quarterfinals of the [2002 KPGA 1st Tour](@/tournaments/2002-kpga-1st-tour.md) and qualified for the [2001 Hanbitsoft OSL](@/tournaments/2001-hanbitsoft-osl.md), the [2002 SKY OSL](@/tournaments/2002-sky-osl.md) and the [2002-2003 Panasonic OSL](@/tournaments/2002-2003-panasonic-osl.md). In team play he went 2–0 in sets for KTF in the upper-bracket decider of the [2003–04 LG IBM MBC Team League](@/tournaments/2003-2004-lg-ibm-mbc-team-league.md).
