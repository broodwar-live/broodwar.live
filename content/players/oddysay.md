Oddysay (Han Woong Ryul) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1981, who played for KOR and KTF MagicNs. A Terran of the early televised era, he competed in the OSL and the KPGA Tour in 2001–2002.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2002 NATE OSL](/tournaments/2002-nate-osl/).
