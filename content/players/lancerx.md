LancerX (Pavel Maksimov) is a Russian Protoss player born in 1982 and a member of the clan SouLGaming; earlier he played under the tags RoX.KIS and iFU. From December 2010 he played an unusually heavy schedule of ladder and tournament games, and the volume paid off: by mid-2012 he was placing high in the bigger foreign events.

His form peaked in December 2012, when he beat Djem5 at the Moscow New Year LAN and again in the Russian King of the Hill group finals. In 2013 he won the next two LAN tournaments held in Russia and qualified for the TeamLiquid Legacy Starleague 2, where Michael eliminated him in the round of 16.

In the StarCraft: Remastered era he has played in the Bombastic StarLeague, appearing in [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md), [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md), [BSL Season 17](@/tournaments/bsl-s17.md), [BSL Season 19](@/tournaments/bsl-s19.md) and [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md).
