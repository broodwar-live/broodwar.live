LancerX (Pavel Maksimov) is a Russian Protoss player, born in 1982. He improved steadily through heavy ladder and tournament play in the early 2010s, won several Russian LAN events in 2013, and later became a regular in the Bombastic StarLeague.

He appears in 5 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2019 and 2026.
