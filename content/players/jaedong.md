Jaedong (Lee Jae Dong) is a South Korean Zerg who played for Lecaf OZ, Hwaseung OZ and Team 8, and is counted among the four players known as TaekBangLeeSsang. After debuting in 2006 and winning Rookie of the Year, he won three OSL titles (EVER 2007, BATOO 2008, Bacchus 2009) and two MSL titles (GomTV MSL Season 4 in 2008, NATE MSL in 2009). He was also runner-up in the 2008 Arena MSL, the Hana Daetoo Securities and BigFile MSLs of 2010, and the 2010 Korean Air OSL Season 2.

He moved to StarCraft II with Evil Geniuses, retired from it in November 2016, and then began streaming Brood War on Afreeca; he finished second in Korea StarCraft League 1 in 2018. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.
