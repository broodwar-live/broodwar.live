Speed (Lee Young Woong), previously known as 10minFlash and Sexy, is a South Korean Terran player who was on the rosters of WeMade FOX and Hwaseung OZ. In the post-KeSPA scene he twice reached the final of the SOSPA Ranking Tournament, losing to Mong and Killer. He has played for StarCastTV Stars since 2021.
