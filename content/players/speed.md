Speed (Lee Young Woong) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1994, who has also competed as 10minFlash and Sexy. He was on the rosters of [WeMade FOX](@/wiki/teams/wemade-fox.md) and, from March 2010, [Hwaseung OZ](@/wiki/teams/hwaseung-oz.md) during the KeSPA era.

In the streaming era he twice reached the deciding match of the SOSPA Ranking Tournament, losing to [Mong](@/players/mong.md) and to [Killer](@/players/killer.md). He has played for StarCastTV Stars since October 2021. He has qualified for the ASL six times, starting with [ASL Season 11](@/tournaments/asl-s11.md) and then in every season from [ASL Season 17](@/tournaments/asl-s17.md) to [ASL Season 21](@/tournaments/asl-s21.md).
