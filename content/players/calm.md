Calm (Kim Yoon Hwan) is a South Korean Zerg player who played his whole career for STX SouL, from 2005 to 2013. Initially used largely in 2v2 matches, he became one of STX's leading 1v1 players after 2v2 was dropped from Proleague in 2008. He won the 2009 Avalon MSL, his only individual title. He later played StarCraft II for STX SouL before retiring.
