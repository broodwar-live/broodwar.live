Calm (Kim Yoon Hwan) is a South Korean Zerg player who spent his entire progaming career, from 2005 to 2013, with [STX SouL](@/wiki/teams/stx-soul.md). For his first years he was used mainly as the Zerg half of STX's 2v2 pairs in Proleague. When 2v2 was dropped after the 2008 season he adapted quickly to 1v1 and became one of STX's aces, finishing the 2008–09 Proleague with 41 wins and 20 losses.

His breakthrough came in the [2009 Avalon MSL](@/tournaments/2009-avalon-msl.md), which he won by beating [Kwanro](@/players/kwanro.md) 3–1 in the final. He also reached the semifinals of the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md), the [2010 Hana Daetoo Securities MSL](@/tournaments/2010-hana-daetoo-securities-msl.md) and the [2010-2011 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2010-2011-bacchus-osl.md). After a spell in StarCraft II with STX he retired, and he has played in the ASL since 2018, reaching the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 7](@/tournaments/asl-s7.md).
