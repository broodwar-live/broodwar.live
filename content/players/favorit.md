Favorit (Ilja Hülsmann) is a Protoss player born in 1987. Of Russian origin and holding German nationality according to Liquipedia, he lives in Sweden. Before StarCraft: Remastered he played European clan leagues as Zarrr, under tags such as faculty.Zarrr and ash.Zarrr, and he now plays for iRk.

He was a regular in the early seasons of the Bombastic StarLeague. In [BSL Season 3](@/tournaments/bsl-s3.md) he reached Lower Round 3 of the playoff bracket, and he also played in [BSL Season 5](@/tournaments/bsl-s5.md), [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md), [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md) and [BSL Season 10](@/tournaments/bsl-s10.md).
