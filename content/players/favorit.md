Favorit (Ilja Hülsmann) is a Sweden-based Protoss player, born in 1987. He played European clan leagues under the name Zarrr before StarCraft: Remastered and has since competed for iRk.

He appears in 5 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2017 and 2020.
