Kal (Kim Ku Hyun) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1990, who played for STX SouL, Air Force ACE and Prime. Drafted by STX SouL in 2006, he became the team's ace and broke through by reaching the final of the 2008 GOMTV MSL Season 4, which he lost to [Jaedong](/players/jaedong/). He retired from professional StarCraft II in April 2014.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he finished runner-up in the [2008 GOMTV MSL Season 4](/tournaments/2008-gomtv-msl-season-4/) and reached the semifinals of the [2008 Club Day Online MSL](/tournaments/2008-club-day-online-msl/), [2009-2010 NATE MSL](/tournaments/2009-2010-nate-msl/) and [2010 Korean Air OSL Season 1](/tournaments/2010-korean-air-osl-season-1/).
