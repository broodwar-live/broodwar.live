Grrrr... (Guillaume Patry) is a Canadian Protoss player, born in 1982, who played for AMD Dream Team and Hexatron. The most successful foreigner of the first generation in Korea, he moved to South Korea in 2000 and competed at the top level while playing mainly Protoss. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he won the [2000 Hanaro OSL](/tournaments/2000-hanaro-osl/) and reached the semifinals of the [2001 Hanbitsoft OSL](/tournaments/2001-hanbitsoft-osl/).
