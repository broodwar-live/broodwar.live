Grrrr... (Guillaume Patry) is a retired Canadian Protoss player, born in 1982. After topping the early Blizzard and AMD online leagues he moved to South Korea in 2000, becoming the most successful foreigner of the first generation to compete there. He later played for AMD Dream Team and Hexatron before retiring in 2004.

He won the [2000 Hanaro OSL](@/tournaments/2000-hanaro-osl.md), beating [H.O.T-Forever](@/players/hot-forever.md) 3–2 in the final, and remains the only foreign player to have won an OSL. He also reached the semifinals of the [2001 Hanbitsoft OSL](@/tournaments/2001-hanbitsoft-osl.md). Though mainly a Protoss, he could play all three races at a high level; reaver drops and gas rushes were trademarks, and his habit of taking a fast expansion behind early templar tech against Zerg became a standard approach. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012 and played in OGN's Brood War Legends Match in 2015.
