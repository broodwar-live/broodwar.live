Rush (Yoo Young Jin) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1991, who played for [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md) from 2009 to 2012 and qualified for the [2012 Tving OSL](@/tournaments/2012-tving-osl.md). After a spell in StarCraft II he returned to Brood War on the Fish server in mid-2015 and began streaming on Afreeca that September.

He has become one of the most consistent Terrans of the ASL era. After quarterfinal runs in [ASL Season 10](@/tournaments/asl-s10.md) and [ASL Season 11](@/tournaments/asl-s11.md), he reached the final of [ASL Season 12](@/tournaments/asl-s12.md) in 2021, losing 3–4 to [Mini](@/players/mini.md). He was runner-up again in [ASL Season 14](@/tournaments/asl-s14.md) in 2022, beaten 2–4 by [Royal](@/players/royal.md). He reached the semifinals of [ASL Season 16](@/tournaments/asl-s16.md) and the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 13](@/tournaments/asl-s13.md), [ASL Season 17](@/tournaments/asl-s17.md), [ASL Season 18](@/tournaments/asl-s18.md) and [ASL Season 19](@/tournaments/asl-s19.md). Fans call him the "Future Terran".
