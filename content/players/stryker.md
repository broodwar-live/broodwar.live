StRyKeR is an American Zerg player who plays for NaS (North American Squad) and streams on Twitch. Active in competition since 2020, he is one of the most consistent North American entrants in the Bombastic StarLeague.

He has played in [BSL Season 10](@/tournaments/bsl-s10.md), [BSL Season 11](@/tournaments/bsl-s11.md), [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md), [BSL Season 17](@/tournaments/bsl-s17.md), [BSL Season 18](@/tournaments/bsl-s18.md), [BSL Season 19](@/tournaments/bsl-s19.md) and [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md). In the playoff brackets he reached Lower Round 1 of [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md) and Lower Round 2 of [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md), his deepest run in the league so far.
