ChoJJa (Jo Yong Ho) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1984, who played for SouL and KTF MagicNs. Known for his heavy use of Ultralisks and for his sportsmanship, he was the second Zerg to reach No. 1 in the KeSPA rankings and stayed in the KeSPA top 30 for 66 consecutive months from November 2001 to April 2007. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he won the [2005-2006 LG CYON MSL](/tournaments/2005-2006-lg-cyon-msl/), finished runner-up in the [2002-2003 Stout & Baskin Robbins KPGA 4th Tour](/tournaments/2002-2003-stout-baskin-robbins-kpga-4th-tour/), [2002-2003 Panasonic OSL](/tournaments/2002-2003-panasonic-osl/) and [2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 1](/tournaments/2006-shinhan-bank-osl-season-1/) and reached the semifinals of the [2002 Reebok KPGA 2nd Tour](/tournaments/2002-reebok-kpga-2nd-tour/), [2003 Stout MSL](/tournaments/2003-stout-msl/), [2004-2005 You Are the Golf King MSL](/tournaments/2004-2005-you-are-the-golf-king-msl/) and [2005 UZOO MSL](/tournaments/2005-uzoo-msl/).
