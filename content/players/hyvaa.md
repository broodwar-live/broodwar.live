hyvaa (Shin Dae Kun) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1991, who has played for eSTRO and STX SouL. He became a regular in eSTRO's Proleague lineup in 2008 and was one of the standout players of the Shinhan Winners League, later moving to STX SouL and StarCraft II.

He appears in 6 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2009 and 2012.
