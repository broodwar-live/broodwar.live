hyvaa (Shin Dae Kun) is a South Korean Zerg born in 1991. After some success in the minor leagues he became a regular in the [eSTRO](@/wiki/teams/estro.md) Proleague lineup by May 2008, and in the Shinhan Bank Winners League he was briefly eSTRO's best player and one of the league's statistical leaders. His form dipped afterwards: he finished the first round of the 2009–10 Proleague at 4–8.

When eSTRO disbanded in October 2010 he moved to [STX SouL](@/wiki/teams/stx-soul.md), where he stayed until 2013, also playing StarCraft II. He went 1–1 for STX in the playoff first round of the [2010–11 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2010-2011-shinhan-bank-proleague.md).

Individually he qualified for the [2009 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2009-bacchus-osl.md), where [Hwasin](@/players/hwasin.md) knocked him out in the round of 36, and for the [2009–2010 NATE MSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-nate-msl.md), losing to [Flash](@/players/flash.md) and [Iris](@/players/iris.md) in the round of 32. His best result was a quarterfinal in the [2012 Tving OSL](@/tournaments/2012-tving-osl.md).
