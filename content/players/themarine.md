TheMarine (Kim Jeong Min) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1982, who played for Greatest One and KTF MagicNs. A standout Terran of the GO team known for his by-the-book style, he moved to KTF MagicNs in 2003, retired in early 2006 and became a commentator for OnGameNet's Proleague coverage.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2001 SKY OSL](/tournaments/2001-sky-osl/) and [2004 HanaFOS CENGAME MSL](/tournaments/2004-hanafos-cengame-msl/).
