TheMarine (Kim Jeong Min) is a retired South Korean Terran player born in 1982. On the GO team (now [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md)) he became one of the most promising Terrans of the early era, known as the "By-the-Book Terran" for sticking to standard, straightforward play. He reached the semifinals of the [2001 SKY OSL](@/tournaments/2001-sky-osl.md).

KTF MagicNs bought him in June 2003. He made the semifinals of the [2004 HanaFOS CENGAME MSL](@/tournaments/2004-hanafos-cengame-msl.md), but otherwise never recaptured his GO form, and is often cited as an example of the "KTF curse". With KTF he played in the grand final of the [2003–04 LG IBM MBC Team League](@/tournaments/2003-2004-lg-ibm-mbc-team-league.md) and in the finals of the [2004 SKY Proleague Round 3](@/tournaments/2004-sky-proleague-round-3.md) and [2005 SKY Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2005-sky-proleague-round-1.md), all three ending with KTF as runner-up.

He retired in early 2006 and became a popular commentator on OnGameNet's Proleague broadcasts, leaving in May 2009 to begin military service.
