Junwi (Park Gyung Lak) was a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1984, who played for Hanbit Stars and Woongjin Stars. Nicknamed the "Terran Killer", he was one of the leading Zergs of 2002–2003. He passed away on 30 July 2019.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2002-2003 Panasonic OSL](/tournaments/2002-2003-panasonic-osl/), [2003 Olympus OSL](/tournaments/2003-olympus-osl/) and [2003 MyCube OSL](/tournaments/2003-mycube-osl/).
