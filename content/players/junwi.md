Junwi (Park Gyung Lak) was a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1984, who played for [Hanbit Stars](@/wiki/teams/hanbit-stars.md) from 2001 and finished his career with [Woongjin Stars](@/wiki/teams/woongjin-stars.md). Nicknamed the "Terran Killer", he was one of the leading Zergs of 2002–03.

He made the semifinals of three consecutive OSLs. In the [2002-2003 Panasonic OSL](@/tournaments/2002-2003-panasonic-osl.md) he fell to [Chojja](@/players/chojja.md) in five games; in the [2003 Olympus OSL](@/tournaments/2003-olympus-osl.md) he escaped a group with [BoxeR](@/players/boxer.md), [NaDa](@/players/nada.md) and [fOru](@/players/foru.md) before [YellOw](@/players/yellow.md) swept him; in the [2003 MyCube OSL](@/tournaments/2003-mycube-osl.md) he lost to [Kingdom](@/players/kingdom.md), then took third place by beating [Reach](@/players/reach.md) 3–2. The streak ended at the [2003-2004 NHN OSL](@/tournaments/2003-2004-nhn-osl.md).

With Hanbit he played in the final of the [2003 LifeZone KPGA Team League](@/tournaments/2003-lifezone-kpga-team-league.md), where the team was runner-up. He was also part of the side that won the [2004 SKY Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2004-sky-proleague-round-1.md), taking his set in the final. He died on 30 July 2019.
