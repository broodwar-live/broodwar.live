firebathero (Lee Sung Eun) is a retired South Korean Terran progamer, born in 1988. He played for [Samsung KHAN](@/wiki/teams/samsung-khan.md) from 2005 to 2010 and then for [Air Force ACE](@/wiki/teams/air-force-ace.md) during his military service until 2012. Fans knew him for his energetic victory celebrations.

His best individual run came in the [2007 GOMTV MSL Season 2](@/tournaments/2007-gomtv-msl-season-2.md): after beating [BoxeR](@/players/boxer.md) and [Canata](@/players/canata.md) in his group, he went on to the semifinals. He also reached the quarterfinals of the [2006-2007 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 3](@/tournaments/2006-2007-shinhan-bank-osl-season-3.md) and the [2008 Club Day Online MSL](@/tournaments/2008-club-day-online-msl.md).

In Proleague he played in the final for Samsung KHAN when the team won the [2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2007-shinhan-bank-proleague-round-1.md), and he was named Finals MVP of the [2008 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2008-shinhan-bank-proleague.md), which Samsung also won.
