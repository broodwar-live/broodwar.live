firebathero (Lee Sung Eun) is a South Korean Terran and former progamer who played for Samsung KHAN from 2005 and then for Air Force ACE during his military service until 2012. He debuted in the Starleague in the 2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 2. In Season 3 of that OSL he advanced to the quarterfinals, losing to Casy, and finished sixth.
