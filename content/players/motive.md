Motive (Won Seon Jae) is a South Korean Protoss player born on 12 July 1993. He joined [KT Rolster](@/wiki/teams/kt-rolster.md) on 28 March 2011, during the final seasons of the KeSPA Brood War league, and stayed with the team into its StarCraft II years, playing Protoss in that game as well. He has also used the IDs Lizzy and WanGGoChi.

Years after the KeSPA era he returned to the individual leagues on AfreecaTV, qualifying for [ASL Season 18](@/tournaments/asl-s18.md) in 2024.
