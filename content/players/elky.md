ElkY (Bertrand Grospellier) is a French Terran player, born in 1981, who played for AMD Dream Team and Hexatron Dream Team. One of the first foreigners to play professionally in Korea, he was televised from 2002 to 2004 and is often ranked as the second most successful foreigner in Korea after [Grrrr...](/players/grrrr/). He later became a professional poker player.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2002 SKY OSL](/tournaments/2002-sky-osl/).
