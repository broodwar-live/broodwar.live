ElkY (Bertrand Grospellier) is a French Terran player born in 1981 and one of the first foreigners to compete professionally in Korea. He played for AMD Dream Team, later Hexatron Dream Team, and was on televised leagues from early 2002 until mid-2004; he is commonly ranked second among foreigners in Korea behind [Grrrr...](@/players/grrrr.md).

His best run was the [2002 SKY OSL](@/tournaments/2002-sky-osl.md), where he made the semifinals and fell to [Boxer](@/players/boxer.md) in a series overshadowed by a regame over an allied-mine trick, then lost the third-place match to [YellOw](@/players/yellow.md). He also qualified for several other OSLs, including the [2002-2003 Panasonic OSL](@/tournaments/2002-2003-panasonic-osl.md). Internationally he reached the final of the first World Cyber Games in 2001. After missing out in 2005 qualifiers he left StarCraft and became a professional poker player.
