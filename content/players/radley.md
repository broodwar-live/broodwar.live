Radley (Radoslaw Gierek) is a Polish Terran player, born in 1991. A member of NetWars, he played for the Polish national team as a 2v2 player and is known for an unorthodox, micro-heavy style.

He appears in 4 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2013 and 2025.
