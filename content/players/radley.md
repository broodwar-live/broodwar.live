Radley (Radoslaw Gierek) is a Polish Terran player, born in 1991, and a member of NetWars. He was active on the Polish scene from 2006, playing both 1v1 and 2v2, and in 2009–10 he was on Poland's main national team as a 2v2 player alongside [trutaCz](@/players/trutacz.md). After a two-year break he joined the wider foreign scene in 2012 through C-rank tournaments. He returned to the main national team in 2015, and when the team split in 2016 he became one of the captains of Poland B in ICCup Nation League Seasons 1 and 2.

Radley relies on micro rather than macro, does not prepare builds, and often springs single-game upsets on higher-rated opponents; BBS openings are a favourite. He has qualified for the Bombastic StarLeague in [BSL Season 2](@/tournaments/bsl-s2.md), [BSL Season 18](@/tournaments/bsl-s18.md), [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md) and [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md).
