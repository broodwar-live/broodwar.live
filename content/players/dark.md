DarK (Alan Bruno Barrios) is a Bolivian Protoss player born in 1994, active in competition since 2019 and also known by the ID OnlyDark. He played for Crazy Warriors Team from 2021 to 2024 and joined RED in 2025.

He is one of the few Bolivian players to appear regularly in the Bombastic StarLeague, the main league for players outside Korea. On this site's records he played in [BSL Season 6](@/tournaments/bsl-s6.md) in 2019, returned for [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md), and then took part in three consecutive seasons: [BSL Season 18](@/tournaments/bsl-s18.md), [BSL Season 19](@/tournaments/bsl-s19.md) and [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md).
