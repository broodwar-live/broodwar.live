DarK (Alan Bruno Barrios) is a Bolivian Protoss player, born in 1994, who has played for Crazy Warriors Team and RED. He competes regularly in the Bombastic StarLeague.

He appears in 5 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2019 and 2025.
