Rage (Kim Hwan Joong) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1982, who played for Greatest One, Air Force ACE and CJ Entus. He qualified for several MSL seasons in 2003–2004.

He appears in 4 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2003 and 2004.
