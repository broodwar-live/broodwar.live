Rage (Kim Hwan Joong) is a retired South Korean Protoss player born on 11 September 1982. He played for Greatest One, the team that became [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md), and later served with [Air Force ACE](@/wiki/teams/air-force-ace.md).

He was a regular in the early double-elimination MSL, qualifying for the [2003 Stout MSL](@/tournaments/2003-stout-msl.md), the [2003 TriGem MSL](@/tournaments/2003-trigem-msl.md) and the [2004-2005 You Are the Golf King MSL](@/tournaments/2004-2005-you-are-the-golf-king-msl.md). His deepest run came in the [2004 SPRIS MSL](@/tournaments/2004-spris-msl.md), where he beat [YellOw](@/players/yellow.md), [ChoJJa](@/players/chojja.md) and [July](@/players/july.md) in the lower bracket before [Nal_rA](@/players/nal-ra.md) stopped him in the lower quarterfinals.

In team play he took part in the playoffs of the [2003–04 LG IBM MBC Team League](@/tournaments/2003-2004-lg-ibm-mbc-team-league.md), played in the [2005 SKY Proleague Grand Final](@/tournaments/2005-sky-proleague-grand-final.md), taking a 2v2 set with [sAviOr](@/players/savior.md), and played in the final of the [2006 SKY Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2006-sky-proleague-round-2.md), which CJ lost to MBCGame HERO.
