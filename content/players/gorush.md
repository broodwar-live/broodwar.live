GoRush (Park Tae Min) is a retired South Korean Zerg progamer who won the World Cyber Games Challenge in 2000. He later played for GO (SUMA GO), Greatest One, SK Telecom T1 and Air Force ACE. Noted for his ZvZ, he won the 2004 You Are The Golf King MSL and was part of the Zerg resurgence of that year alongside July.

After retiring he became a commentator for OnGameNet, and he was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.
