sSak (Choi Ho Seon) is a South Korean Terran player born in 1989 who played for SK Telecom T1 from 2008. In the PDPop MSL he defeated Flash in the round of 32 and advanced to the round of 16.

After the KeSPA era he competed in amateur leagues and streamed on Afreeca, winning the 10th SonicTV Starleague. He is commonly called "Ho Seon King."
