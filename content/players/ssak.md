sSak (Choi Ho Seon) is a South Korean Terran player born in 1989 who played for [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md) from 2008 to 2013. His best-known KeSPA moment was an upset of [Flash](@/players/flash.md) in the round-of-32 group of the PDPop MSL, after which [Calm](@/players/calm.md) eliminated him in the round of 16. He was on the SKT rosters that won the [2011 Shinhan Bank Winners League](@/tournaments/2011-shinhan-bank-winners-league.md), where he played in the earlier playoff rounds, and the [2011–12 SK Planet Proleague Season 1](@/tournaments/2011-2012-sk-planet-proleague-season-1.md), where he played a set in the final.

In the streaming era he plays amateur leagues and streams on Afreeca. He won the 10th SonicTV Starleague, beating [HiyA](@/players/hiya.md) and [Last](@/players/last.md) on the way. In the Afreeca Starleague he reached the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 1](@/tournaments/asl-s1.md) and [ASL Season 3](@/tournaments/asl-s3.md), and has qualified for most seasons since. Fans know him as "Ho Seon King".
