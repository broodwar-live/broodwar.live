OversKy (Lee Joo Yeong) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1984, who played for Greatest One, CJ Entus and Air Force ACE. He captained CJ Entus before joining Air Force ACE; his military service kept him out of the GOM TV MSL Season 2 he had qualified for.

He appears in 5 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2004 and 2007.
