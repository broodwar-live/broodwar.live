OversKy (Lee Joo Yeong) is a retired South Korean Zerg born in 1984, nicknamed "Pretty Boy Zerg". He played for Greatest One, which became [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md) in 2006, and captained CJ before enlisting with [Air Force ACE](@/wiki/teams/air-force-ace.md).

He qualified for the [2004 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2004-ever-osl.md), the [2005 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2005-ever-osl.md) and the [2005 So1 OSL](@/tournaments/2005-so1-osl.md). In the [2007 GOMTV MSL Season 2](@/tournaments/2007-gomtv-msl-season-2.md) he beat TT 2–0 in the round of 16 but did not play on, as his military service intervened; he also qualified for the [2007 GOMTV MSL Season 3](@/tournaments/2007-gomtv-msl-season-3.md).

In team leagues he went 2–1 for his team in the [2003–04 LG IBM MBC Team League](@/tournaments/2003-2004-lg-ibm-mbc-team-league.md), 2–0 in the playoffs of the [2005 SKY Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2005-sky-proleague-round-1.md), and played in the final of the [2006 SKY Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2006-sky-proleague-round-2.md), which CJ lost. He was known for aggressive early pools, such as a proxy-Hatchery sunken rush against Freedom in December 2007.
