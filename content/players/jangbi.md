JangBi (Heo Yeong Moo) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1989, who spent his whole career with [Samsung KHAN](@/wiki/teams/samsung-khan.md), from 2006 to 2013.

He first reached a premier-league final in the [2008 Club Day Online MSL](@/tournaments/2008-club-day-online-msl.md), losing 3–1 to [Bisu](@/players/bisu.md), and was runner-up again in the [2009 Lost Saga MSL](@/tournaments/2009-lost-saga-msl.md), losing 3–1 to [Luxury](@/players/luxury.md). He also reached the semifinals of the [2008 GOMTV MSL Season 4](@/tournaments/2008-gomtv-msl-season-4.md). His titles came in the last two Brood War OSLs: he won the [2011 Jin Air OSL](@/tournaments/2011-jin-air-osl.md), beating [Fantasy](@/players/fantasy.md) 3–2, and the [2012 Tving OSL](@/tournaments/2012-tving-osl.md), again against Fantasy, 3–1.

In Proleague he played in the finals of Samsung's winning [2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2007-shinhan-bank-proleague-round-1.md) and [2008 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2008-shinhan-bank-proleague.md) campaigns. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012 and later turned to streaming on Afreeca.
