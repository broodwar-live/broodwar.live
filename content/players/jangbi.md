JangBi (Heo Yeong Moo) is a South Korean Protoss player born in 1989 who played for Samsung KHAN from 2006 to 2013. He was runner-up in the ClubDay Online MSL in 2008 and the Lost Saga MSL in 2009.

He won the Jin Air Starleague in 2011 and the Tving Starleague in 2012. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012 and, after competing in Proleague's StarCraft II period, went on to stream on Afreeca.
