Mini (Byun Hyun Je) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1994. He joined [STX SouL](@/wiki/teams/stx-soul.md) in 2010 and stayed through the team's StarCraft II years until it disbanded in 2013, playing mostly in Proleague. He enlisted for military service in April 2015 and returned to Brood War as an Afreeca streamer in January 2017. He is known for his liberal use of Shield Batteries.

Since his return he has been one of the most consistent Protoss players. He reached the semifinals of [ASL Season 5](@/tournaments/asl-s5.md) and then reached four finals that he lost: [ASL Season 7](@/tournaments/asl-s7.md) to [Last](@/players/last.md), [KSL Season 3](@/tournaments/ksl-s3.md) to [Rain](@/players/rain.md), [ASL Season 11](@/tournaments/asl-s11.md) to [Larva](@/players/larva.md) and [ASL Season 16](@/tournaments/asl-s16.md) to [Soulkey](@/players/soulkey.md). He won [ASL Season 12](@/tournaments/asl-s12.md) in 2021, beating [Rush](@/players/rush.md) 4–3 in the final.
