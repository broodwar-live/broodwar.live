Tempest (Park Jae Yung) is a retired South Korean Protoss player, born in 1988. He joined KTF MagicNs in March 2007 and stayed as the team became [KT Rolster](@/wiki/teams/kt-rolster.md) in 2009. After time in the minor leagues he became a semi-regular in the 2008–09 Proleague season. He was known as a strong macro player but also as an inconsistent one.

Individually he reached the round of 16 of the [2008 Club Day Online MSL](@/tournaments/2008-club-day-online-msl.md) and the [2009 Lost Saga MSL](@/tournaments/2009-lost-saga-msl.md), eliminating [Fantasy](@/players/fantasy.md) from both, and later qualified for the [2010-2011 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2010-2011-bacchus-osl.md) and the [2011 Jin Air OSL](@/tournaments/2011-jin-air-osl.md). He also knocked [Jaedong](@/players/jaedong.md) out of the Averatec-Intel Classic Season 2. He played in the final of the [2009–10 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2009-2010-shinhan-bank-proleague.md), won by KT, and was also on the KT team that won the [2010–11 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2010-2011-shinhan-bank-proleague.md).
