Flash (Lee Young Ho) is a South Korean Terran player who debuted in 2007 and played for KTF MagicNs and KT Rolster, staying with KT through the StarCraft II era until 2015. At 15 he won the 2008 Bacchus OSL, becoming the youngest Starleague champion; he went on to win the 2009 EVER OSL, the Korean Air OSL Season 2 (2010), and the Hana Daetoo Securities, BigFile and ABC Mart MSLs. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.

Since returning to Brood War on Afreeca in 2016, he has won ASL Seasons 2, 3, 4 and 8, and was runner-up in ASL 6 (2018) and ASL 21 (2026).
