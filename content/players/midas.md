Midas (Jun Sang Wook) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1987, who played for SUMA GO, Greatest One, SK Telecom T1 and WeMade FOX. Though he never reached a Starleague final, he was regarded as one of the strongest Terrans of his time, especially in Terran versus Protoss. He retired and entered military service when WeMade FOX disbanded in 2011.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2006 Pringles MSL Season 1](/tournaments/2006-pringles-msl-season-1/) and [2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 2](/tournaments/2006-shinhan-bank-osl-season-2/).
