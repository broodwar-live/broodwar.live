HyuN (Ko Seok Hyun) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1988. After a brief spell with Pirates of Space he joined [MBCGame HERO](@/wiki/teams/mbcgame-hero.md) in 2006 and stayed until October 2011. He became a free agent in 2009 but received no offers and returned to MBCGame. Fans remember him for arriving at an MSL group-selection ceremony pretending to be drunk.

He qualified for several OSL and MSL seasons, including the [2008 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2008-ever-osl.md), the [2010 Hana Daetoo Securities MSL](@/tournaments/2010-hana-daetoo-securities-msl.md) and the [2011 Jin Air OSL](@/tournaments/2011-jin-air-osl.md). In November 2011 he switched to StarCraft II with Team SCV Life and later played for Jin Air Green Wings. Back in Brood War since 2017, he has played in the ASL, including [ASL Season 3](@/tournaments/asl-s3.md) and [ASL Season 20](@/tournaments/asl-s20.md).
