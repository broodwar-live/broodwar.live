gypsy (Mihai Avrigeanu) is a Canadian Terran player, born in 1993, who is also a streamer and caster. Since StarCraft: Remastered launched he has competed mostly in North American events, and he co-cast Seasons 10 and 11 of the [Bombastic StarLeague](@/tournaments/bsl.md) with [Nyoken](@/players/nyoken.md).

As a player his best BSL result is a semifinal in [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md), followed by a quarterfinal in [BSL Season 14](@/tournaments/bsl-s14.md). He has also played in Seasons 12, 17, 18 and 21.
