gypsy (Mihai Avrigeanu) is a Canadian Terran player, born in 1993. He has played mainly in North American events since StarCraft: Remastered launched, streams and casts, and co-cast Bombastic StarLeague Seasons 10 and 11.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [BSL Season 13](/tournaments/bsl-s13/).
