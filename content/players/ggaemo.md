ggaemo (Kim Kyung Mo) is a South Korean Zerg player born in 1988. He joined Lecaf OZ in March 2007 and stayed through its renaming to [Hwaseung OZ](@/wiki/teams/hwaseung-oz.md), though he was often behind [Jaedong](@/players/jaedong.md) and [Killer](@/players/killer.md) in the team's Zerg pecking order. He played a set in the final of the [2008–09 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2008-2009-shinhan-bank-proleague.md), where OZ finished as runner-up.

In April 2010 he was announced for [Air Force ACE](@/wiki/teams/air-force-ace.md), the military team, and went 11–8 over the first two rounds of the 2010–11 Proleague; coming from Jaedong's team, the form earned him the nickname "MilitaryDong". Individually he qualified for the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md) and the [2011 ABC Mart MSL](@/tournaments/2011-abc-mart-msl.md). Foreign fans also call him "Gasp Zerg" after his reaction to losing a group of Hydralisks to a hidden mine from [UpMaGiC](@/players/upmagic.md). He later streamed on Afreeca and played in [ASL Season 3](@/tournaments/asl-s3.md), [ASL Season 11](@/tournaments/asl-s11.md), [ASL Season 13](@/tournaments/asl-s13.md) and [ASL Season 14](@/tournaments/asl-s14.md).
