ggaemo (Kim Kyung Mo) is a South Korean Zerg player who played for Lecaf OZ (later Hwaseung OZ) from 2007 before joining Air Force ACE in 2010 for his military service. He posted an 11–8 record over the first two rounds of the 10–11 Proleague with ACE. Foreign fans nicknamed him "Gasp Zerg" after his reaction to losing his Hydralisks to a hidden mine in a game against UpMaGiC.
