Bakuryu (Mike Lange) is a German Zerg player who started competing in Brood War in 2005. He came to wider attention by qualifying for WCG Germany 2010, where he finished fourth after joining the finals as a substitute.

In 2011 and 2012 he earned several top-three finishes in Defiler Tour events, represented Germany and Western Europe in nation-wars team competitions, and was nominated by ICCup for its Best Player and Best Zerg awards. In early 2013 he reached the semifinals and the finals of the first and second TeamLiquid Legacy Starleague qualifiers. He has been known by the nickname "MoustacheZerg".
