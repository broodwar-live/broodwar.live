Action (Kim Seong Dae) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1992. He debuted with [eSTRO](@/wiki/teams/estro.md) in 2008 and became the team's main Zerg in the 2009–10 Proleague. When eSTRO folded in 2010 he was drafted by [KT Rolster](@/wiki/teams/kt-rolster.md), where he drew his first salary as a progamer. He played in the final as KT won the [2010–11 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2010-2011-shinhan-bank-proleague.md), and KT were runners-up in the [2011–12 SK Planet Proleague Season 1](@/tournaments/2011-2012-sk-planet-proleague-season-1.md). Individually he reached the quarterfinals of the [2010 Korean Air OSL Season 2](@/tournaments/2010-korean-air-osl-season-2.md).

He stayed with KT through the StarCraft II transition and retired in 2015. Since returning on Afreeca in 2018 he has been a regular in the ASL, reaching the semifinals of [ASL Season 8](@/tournaments/asl-s8.md) and the quarterfinals of several other seasons.
