HiyA (Ku Sung Hoon) is a former South Korean Terran progamer who played for Hwaseung OZ from 2006 until the team disbanded. He won the deciding fifth set of the 2007 Shinhan Proleague Grand Final against firebathero, and went 17–9 in the 08–09 Winners League. He retired when Hwaseung OZ disbanded, returning briefly for the 2012 Tving OSL dual tournament, and has since streamed on Afreeca and played in amateur leagues.
