HiyA (Ku Sung Hoon) is a retired South Korean Terran player, born in 1989, who spent his whole career with Lecaf OZ, later [Hwaseung OZ](@/wiki/teams/hwaseung-oz.md), from 2006 until the team disbanded in 2011.

He is best remembered for the [2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague Grand Final](@/tournaments/2007-shinhan-bank-proleague-grand-final.md): playing the fifth set against [Firebathero](@/players/firebathero.md) on Python, he won the game that sealed Lecaf OZ's 4–1 title over Samsung KHAN. He was also on the OZ side that won the [2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2007-shinhan-bank-proleague-round-2.md). His breakout came in the [2009 Shinhan Bank Winners League](@/tournaments/2009-shinhan-bank-winners-league.md), where he went 17–9 and OZ finished runner-up, as they also did in the [2008–09 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2008-2009-shinhan-bank-proleague.md). Individually he reached the quarterfinals of the [2010 Hana Daetoo Securities MSL](@/tournaments/2010-hana-daetoo-securities-msl.md) and the [2010-2011 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2010-2011-bacchus-osl.md). He retired with the team's disbandment, returning briefly to play in the dual tournament of the [2012 Tving OSL](@/tournaments/2012-tving-osl.md).
