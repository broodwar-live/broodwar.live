TerrOr (Rodrigo Omar Campos Acosta) is a Peruvian Terran player, born in 1992, who has played for North American Squad. He first appeared at WCG Peru 2009 and became one of the strongest foreign Terrans in the early 2010s, taking medals at all four Defiler Tours he entered around the turn of 2011–12. He is a different player from the Korean Zerg [Terror](/players/terror/).

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he won the [BSL Season 12](/tournaments/bsl-s12/), finished runner-up in the [BSL Season 3](/tournaments/bsl-s3/) and [BSL Season 11](/tournaments/bsl-s11/) and reached the semifinals of the [BSL Season 9](/tournaments/bsl-s9/).
