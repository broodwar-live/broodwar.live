effOrt (Kim Jung Woo) is a South Korean Zerg who played his whole KeSPA career for [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md), staying with the team through its StarCraft II years until 2014. He became one of CJ's busiest aces, finishing the 2008–09 Proleague season with 39 wins and 21 losses, and won the BlizzCon 2009 tournament.

His first Starleague title came in the [2010 Korean Air OSL Season 1](@/tournaments/2010-korean-air-osl-season-1.md), where he beat [Flash](@/players/flash.md) 3–2 in the final. He also reached the quarterfinals of the [2009 Avalon MSL](@/tournaments/2009-avalon-msl.md) and the [2010 Bigfile MSL](@/tournaments/2010-bigfile-msl.md), and played in the final of the [2009 Shinhan Bank Winners League](@/tournaments/2009-shinhan-bank-winners-league.md), which CJ won.

Back in Brood War as an Afreeca streamer, he won [ASL Season 6](@/tournaments/asl-s6.md) in 2018, again defeating Flash 3–2 in the final. He later reached the semifinals of [ASL Season 7](@/tournaments/asl-s7.md) and [ASL Season 16](@/tournaments/asl-s16.md).
