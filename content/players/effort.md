effOrt (Kim Jung Woo) is a South Korean Zerg player who played for CJ Entus, continuing with the team into StarCraft II until 2014. Debuting on broadcast in 2008, he became one of CJ's most-used ace players and won the BlizzCon 2009 tournament. He won the Korean Air OSL Season 1 in 2010.

After returning to Brood War he streams on Afreeca and won ASL Season 6 in 2018.
