eOnzErG (Jose Luis Mora Diaz) is a Belgian Zerg player of Cuban origin, born in 1991, who has lived in Spain and plays for NaS. He started competing at the end of 2008 and broke through in 2011–12, winning several Defiler Tour events and, with his team, the ICCup Clan League Season 21.

He is one of the most decorated players of the Bombastic StarLeague. After reaching the semifinals of [BSL Season 4](@/tournaments/bsl-s4.md), he won [BSL Season 5](@/tournaments/bsl-s5.md) in 2018 with a 4–2 final against [Bonyth](@/players/bonyth.md), and he finished runner-up in [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md), losing 2–4 to [trutaCz](@/players/trutacz.md). He also won the StarLadder Pro Series in 2018. In 2019 he travelled to Korea for the offline qualifiers of the third KSL season and reached their second stage. He went as far as the last lower-bracket round in both [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md) and [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md).
