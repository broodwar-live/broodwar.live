eOnzErG (Jose Luis Mora Diaz) is a Belgian Zerg player of Cuban origin who has lived in Spain. He began competing at the end of 2008 and won several Defiler Tour events and the ICCup Clan League Season 21 in 2012.

His strongest year was 2018, when he won BSL5 and the StarLadder Pro Series. In 2019 he travelled to Korea and reached the second stage of the offline qualifiers for KSL Season 3.
