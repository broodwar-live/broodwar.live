Hawk (Devan Subramaniam) is an American Zerg player born in 1993, also known as Hawk248. He played for inte Riktigt kloka (iRk) until December 2021 and has been with North American Squad since January 2022.

He is a regular in the Bombastic StarLeague. He reached the quarterfinals of [BSL Season 8](@/tournaments/bsl-s8.md) and [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md). His deepest run came in [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md) in 2025: he beat [DragOn](@/players/dragon.md) and [UltrA](@/players/ultra.md) in the upper bracket, then lost to [Bonyth](@/players/bonyth.md) and [Dewalt](@/players/dewalt.md) to take third place. Other appearances include [BSL Season 3](@/tournaments/bsl-s3.md), [BSL Season 11](@/tournaments/bsl-s11.md), [BSL Season 15](@/tournaments/bsl-s15.md) and [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md).
