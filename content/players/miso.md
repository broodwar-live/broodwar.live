MIsO (Han Doo Yeol) is a South Korean Zerg born in 1992. He joined [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md) in August 2008, played under the [Hite Entus](@/wiki/teams/hite-entus.md) banner during the 2010–11 merger period, and stayed with CJ until 2012. In KeSPA play he qualified for the [2010-2011 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2010-2011-bacchus-osl.md) and appeared for CJ in the playoffs of the [2010–11 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2010-2011-shinhan-bank-proleague.md).

After the KeSPA era he joined SouLGaming, a team made up mostly of non-Korean players, and became a regular in the Afreeca leagues. He played in [ASL Season 2](@/tournaments/asl-s2.md) and several later ASL seasons, and his best Afreeca-league result was a quarterfinal in [KSL Season 2](@/tournaments/ksl-s2.md); he also played in [KSL Season 3](@/tournaments/ksl-s3.md).
