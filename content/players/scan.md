Scan (Ryoo Seung Kohn) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1993, who studied in the United States and competed mainly in the foreign scene. After returning around 2008, he won several international events in the early post-StarCraft II period, including ISL 1, and was a frequent winner of the Defiler.ru tournament series.

He received multiple bans from foreign tournaments, including an indefinite GosuGamers.net ban for account sharing and playing under other accounts. He has played for StarCastTV Stars since October 2021.
