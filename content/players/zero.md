Queen (Kim Myung Woon), formerly known as ZerO, is a South Korean Zerg who played for Hanbit Stars and then Woongjin Stars from 2008 to 2013. Noted early on for his Mutalisk control and ZvZ, he reached the semifinals of the 2009 Lost Saga MSL and was runner-up in the 2011 ABC Mart MSL. After the end of the KeSPA era he retired and turned to streaming on Afreeca; following his military service, he returned under the ID Queen and won ASL 9 and ASL 10 in 2020.
