RoyaL (Kim Ji Sung), who previously played as sOrry, is a South Korean Terran player born in 1995. He was a member of SK Telecom T1 from 2011 to 2016, including in StarCraft II. In 2019 he qualified for KSL Season 4, his first premier Brood War tournament, and in 2022 he won AfreecaTV StarCraft League Season 14.
