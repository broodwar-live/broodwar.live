RoyaL (Kim Ji Sung) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1995, who previously competed as sOrry. He was a member of [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md) from 2011 to 2016, a period that took him through the team's StarCraft II years.

He returned to Brood War in 2019 and qualified for [KSL Season 4](@/tournaments/ksl-s4.md) by beating [Horang2](@/players/horang2.md), his first premier Brood War tournament. After reaching the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 12](@/tournaments/asl-s12.md), he won [ASL Season 14](@/tournaments/asl-s14.md) in 2022, beating [Rush](@/players/rush.md) 4–2 in the final. He has since been a regular in the ASL, playing in every season from 15 to 21.
