Dewalt (Andrey Belyaev) is a Russian Protoss player from Novosibirsk, born in 1994. He won Defiler Tour 40 in 2012 and was named best Protoss and biggest talent by ICCup in 2013.

After briefly announcing his retirement in 2013, he returned to Brood War later that year. He finished fourth at BSL 4 in 2018 and reached the top four of BSL 6 in 2019. He played for Crazy Warriors Team from 2022 to 2024 and joined inte Riktigt kloka (iRk) in 2025.
