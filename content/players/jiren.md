Jiren (Mauricio Andrés Herrera) is a Chilean Zerg player, born in 1990. Formerly known as babo, he is regarded as one of the best 2v2 players outside Korea. He captained Chile in the BSL Season 6 Nation Wars.

He appears in 8 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2018 and 2022.
