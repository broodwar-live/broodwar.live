Jiren (Mauricio Andrés Herrera) is a Chilean Zerg player born on 28 February 1990 who long competed as babo. He reached Olympic rank on the iCCup 2v2 ladder and is often named among the best 2v2 players outside Korea. He has played for several foreign clans, including iRk, and now represents SouLGaming, with whom he has played in the Shinhan Tank Proleague.

He was a fixture of the early Bombastic StarLeague. He captained Team Chile in the Nation Wars of [BSL Season 6](@/tournaments/bsl-s6.md), and his deepest individual run was the quarterfinals of [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md). He also played in [BSL Season 5](@/tournaments/bsl-s5.md), [BSL Season 8](@/tournaments/bsl-s8.md), [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md), [BSL Season 10](@/tournaments/bsl-s10.md), [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md) and [BSL Season 14](@/tournaments/bsl-s14.md).
