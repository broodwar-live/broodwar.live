YellOw (Hong Jin-Ho) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1982, who played for Gamei, Ideal Space, KTF MagicNs, Toona SG, Air Force ACE and KT Rolster. Nicknamed the "Storm Zerg" for his aggressive style, he was one of the strongest Zerg players of the early 2000s and a long-time rival of [BoxeR](/players/boxer/). He later captained Air Force ACE during his military service and was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he finished runner-up in the [2001 Coca-Cola OSL](/tournaments/2001-coca-cola-osl/), [2002 KPGA 1st Tour](/tournaments/2002-kpga-1st-tour/), [2002 Reebok KPGA 2nd Tour](/tournaments/2002-reebok-kpga-2nd-tour/), [2003 Olympus OSL](/tournaments/2003-olympus-osl/) and [2003 TriGem MSL](/tournaments/2003-trigem-msl/) and reached the semifinals of the [2001 SKY OSL](/tournaments/2001-sky-osl/), [2002 SKY OSL](/tournaments/2002-sky-osl/), [2002-2003 Panasonic OSL](/tournaments/2002-2003-panasonic-osl/), [2004 EVER OSL](/tournaments/2004-ever-osl/) and [2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 1](/tournaments/2006-shinhan-bank-osl-season-1/).
