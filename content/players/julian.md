Julian (Paweł Wojtasiak) is a Polish Zerg player born in 1984, active since 2013 and also known under the IDs Julia, Kinsoo and Aejong. Over the years he has played for the clans VBK, NW and cwt, joined Netwars for Gambit's Cup 3 and team sas for the SBWI team league, and has been a member of Nightmares of Mengsk (nOm) since 2025.

He is one of the longest-serving Bombastic StarLeague players. In the first two seasons, both in 2013, he reached the playoffs, getting to round three of the lower bracket in [BSL Season 1](@/tournaments/bsl-s1.md) and to round two in [BSL Season 2](@/tournaments/bsl-s2.md). He has since played in [BSL Season 6](@/tournaments/bsl-s6.md), [BSL Season 8](@/tournaments/bsl-s8.md), [BSL Season 10](@/tournaments/bsl-s10.md), [BSL Season 12](@/tournaments/bsl-s12.md), [BSL Season 16](@/tournaments/bsl-s16.md), [BSL Season 17](@/tournaments/bsl-s17.md) and [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md).
