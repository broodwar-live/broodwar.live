Julian (Paweł Wojtasiak) is a Polish Zerg player, born in 1984, who has played for Nightmares of Mengsk. He has played in the Bombastic StarLeague since its first season, earlier under the ID Julia.

He appears in 8 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2013 and 2025.
