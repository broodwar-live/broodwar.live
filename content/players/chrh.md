ChRh (Choi In Kyu) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1981, who played for Greatest One, Samsung KHAN and Air Force ACE. One of the oldest players of the early televised era, he was considered the best Random player in the world before settling on Terran. His peak came around 2002, when he finished third in the NATE OSL.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2000 Hanaro OSL](/tournaments/2000-hanaro-osl/), [2002 KPGA 1st Tour](/tournaments/2002-kpga-1st-tour/), [2002 NATE OSL](/tournaments/2002-nate-osl/) and [2002 Pepsi Twist KPGA 3rd Tour](/tournaments/2002-pepsi-twist-kpga-3rd-tour/).
