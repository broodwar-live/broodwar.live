Side (Ju Jin Chul) is a retired South Korean Zerg player born on 8 February 1982. After playing for SouL he spent the core of his career with KOR, which became OnGameNet SPARKYZ in May 2006. His expansion-heavy style earned him nicknames such as "Father of Hatcheries" and "Expansion Zerg".

He was a team-league mainstay for KOR, often in 2v2 sets. In the [2003–04 LG IBM MBC Team League](@/tournaments/2003-2004-lg-ibm-mbc-team-league.md) playoffs he beat [BoxeR](@/players/boxer.md) and [kOs](@/players/kos.md) in consecutive sets. He played in the final of the [2004 SKY Proleague Round 3](@/tournaments/2004-sky-proleague-round-3.md), which KOR won 4–3 over KTF MagicNs. He also played in the playoffs of the [2004 SKY Proleague Grand Final](@/tournaments/2004-sky-proleague-grand-final.md).

Individually he qualified for the [2002 Pepsi Twist KPGA 3rd Tour](@/tournaments/2002-pepsi-twist-kpga-3rd-tour.md) and the [2003 MyCube OSL](@/tournaments/2003-mycube-osl.md). In the [2004 HanaFOS CENGAME MSL](@/tournaments/2004-hanafos-cengame-msl.md) he beat [XellOs](@/players/xellos.md) and [JinSu](@/players/jinsu.md) in the lower bracket before losing to [ChoJJa](@/players/chojja.md) in Lower Round 3.
