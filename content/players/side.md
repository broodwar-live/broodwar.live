Side (Ju Jin Chul) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1982, who played for SouL, KOR and OnGameNet SPARKYZ. He qualified for several OSL and MSL seasons in the early 2000s.

He appears in 4 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2002 and 2004.
