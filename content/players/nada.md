NaDa (Lee Yoon Yeol) is a South Korean Terran player born in 1984, known by the nickname "Genius Terran." He played for Ideal Space, KTF MagicNs, Toona SG, Pantech and WeMade FOX between 2001 and 2010.

He won the KPGA 2nd, 3rd and 4th Tours in 2002 and the OSL three times (Panasonic OSL 2002, IOPS Starleague 2004 and Shinhan Bank Starleague Season 2 in 2006), along with several MSL and OSL runner-up finishes. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012 and, after his discharge from the military in 2015, has played Brood War on Afreeca.
