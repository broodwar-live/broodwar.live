Dienmax (Jimmy Ordóñez Prado) is a Peruvian Protoss player born in 1989. He first competed at WCG Peru 2009 under the name Yukito, getting through the group stage before going out in the next round. He later played for the Peruvian team LaGFree, then Ayac Gamers from 2012, and has represented Eternal Friends since January 2014. He reached A- rank on iCCup and peaked around 2450 MMR on the Remastered ladder.

In the Bombastic StarLeague he has been a recurring participant, playing in [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md), [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md), [BSL Season 16](@/tournaments/bsl-s16.md) and every season from [BSL Season 18](@/tournaments/bsl-s18.md) through [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md).
