Dienmax (Jimmy Ordóñez Prado) is a Peruvian Protoss player, born in 1989, who has played for Ayac Gamers and Eternal Friends. He first appeared at WCG Peru 2009 under the name Yukito and later played for Eternal Friends, becoming a regular in the Bombastic StarLeague.

He appears in 7 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2019 and 2025.
