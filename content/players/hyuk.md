Hyuk (Park Jae Hyuk) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1987, who played for SK Telecom T1. He joined SK Telecom T1 in 2006 and by 2008 had become the team's leading Zerg in Proleague. He later competed in the AfreecaTV StarCraft League.

He appears in 14 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2008 and 2021.
