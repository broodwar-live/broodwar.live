Hyuk (Park Jae Hyuk) is a South Korean Zerg player born on 11 April 1987 who played his whole KeSPA career, from 2006 to 2014, for [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md). Initially behind GoRush in the team's pecking order, he became SKT's leading Zerg in 2008.

The high point of his career came in the final of the [2008–09 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2008-2009-shinhan-bank-proleague.md), where he beat [Ggaemo](@/players/ggaemo.md) and [Jaedong](@/players/jaedong.md) as SKT took the title. He was known for all-kills, including a triple kill of KTF in the Mobile Rival Battle exhibition and a four-player all-kill of STX SouL at the 2009 STX Masters. Individually he reached the quarterfinals of the [2010-2011 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2010-2011-bacchus-osl.md) and the [2011 Jin Air OSL](@/tournaments/2011-jin-air-osl.md), and the round of 16 of the [2010 Bigfile MSL](@/tournaments/2010-bigfile-msl.md).

He was later a playing coach for SKT's StarCraft II team and enlisted for military service in June 2016. In the AfreecaTV era he played in [ASL Season 8](@/tournaments/asl-s8.md), [KSL Season 4](@/tournaments/ksl-s4.md) and [ASL Season 12](@/tournaments/asl-s12.md).
