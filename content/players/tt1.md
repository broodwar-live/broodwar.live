TT1 (Payam Toghyan) is a Canadian player from Montreal, born on 18 March 1988, who plays mainly Protoss and also uses Terran against Protoss; he has also competed as KabaL. In 2008 he was caught map hacking in games played in 2007.

In the 2009 Pokerstrategy.com TSL (TSL2) he won his group over Avi-Love and Strelok to reach the elimination bracket, where he lost 2–3 to White-Ra. Like many foreign players he then moved to StarCraft II, but he returned for the 2014 Brood War Reunion Tournament and finished third–fourth.

In the Bombastic StarLeague he reached the semifinals of [BSL Season 8](@/tournaments/bsl-s8.md) and the quarterfinals of [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md). He also played in [BSL Season 6](@/tournaments/bsl-s6.md) and [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md), and reached Lower Round 1 of the playoffs in [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md).
