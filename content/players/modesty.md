Modesty (Kim Hyun Woo) is a South Korean Zerg player born in 1992. He joined [STX SouL](@/wiki/teams/stx-soul.md) as a rookie in August 2007 and was mostly fielded as a ZvZ specialist in Proleague. His best individual result came in the [2010-2011 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2010-2011-bacchus-osl.md), where he reached the semifinals. He followed it with a quarterfinal in the [2011 Jin Air OSL](@/tournaments/2011-jin-air-osl.md). He retired from professional StarCraft II in December 2012.

In the streaming era he took the 41 Starleague Season 1 title with a 4–0 final over [herO](@/players/hero.md), was runner-up in LoveTV Starleague Season 2 after a 4–1 loss to [Killer](@/players/killer.md), and went 16–7 for ex-KT as the team finished first in the 2014 Gabangpop Sonic Proleague. He enlisted for military service in April 2015, and later played in [KSL Season 2](@/tournaments/ksl-s2.md), [KSL Season 3](@/tournaments/ksl-s3.md) and [KSL Season 4](@/tournaments/ksl-s4.md).
