TY (Jun Tae Yang), also known as BaBy, is a South Korean Terran player born in 1994. He joined WeMade FOX at age 12 in 2006 and later moved to Team 8, where he played until 2013. His breakthrough came in the GOM Classic Season 3, where he reached the round of 16 with wins over JangBi and Modesty. He went on to compete in StarCraft II.
