TY (Jun Tae Yang), also known as BaBy, is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1994. He played StarCraft from a very young age, finishing second in a Hanbitsoft amateur contest before he was nine, and joined [WeMade FOX](@/wiki/teams/wemade-fox.md) at twelve in 2006. In September 2011 he moved to Team 8, where he played until 2013.

He first made his name in the GOM Classic Season 3, reaching the round of 16 with wins over [JangBi](@/players/jangbi.md) and [Modesty](@/players/modesty.md) before losing to [SkyHigh](@/players/skyhigh.md). In the premier leagues he reached the quarterfinals of the [2010 Korean Air OSL Season 1](@/tournaments/2010-korean-air-osl-season-1.md) and the round of 16 of the [2010 Hana Daetoo Securities MSL](@/tournaments/2010-hana-daetoo-securities-msl.md). He then had a long StarCraft II career, and since returning to Brood War in 2024 he has played in [ASL Season 17](@/tournaments/asl-s17.md) and [ASL Season 20](@/tournaments/asl-s20.md).
