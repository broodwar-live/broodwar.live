beast (Yoo Jin Woo, also known as "jatgirl") is a South Korean Zerg and former progamer who was with Hwaseung OZ and Team 8 in 2011. He began his mandatory military service around the turn of 2015. After returning, he reached first place on the Korean StarCraft: Remastered ladder shortly after its closed release in 2017.
