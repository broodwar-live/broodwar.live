Kingdom (Park Yong Wook) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1983, who played for Hanbit Stars, Orion, 4 Union and SK Telecom T1. Counted among the "Three Kings" of Protoss and known for his Probe and Dragoon control, he was a key player for 4U and SK Telecom T1 in team leagues. After retiring he coached SK Telecom T1 and later worked as a commentator for OnGameNet. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he won the [2003 MyCube OSL](/tournaments/2003-mycube-osl/), finished runner-up in the [2004 SPRIS MSL](/tournaments/2004-spris-msl/) and reached the semifinals of the [2001 Hanbitsoft OSL](/tournaments/2001-hanbitsoft-osl/) and [2006 Pringles MSL Season 1](/tournaments/2006-pringles-msl-season-1/).
