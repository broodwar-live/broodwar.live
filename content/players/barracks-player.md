BarrackS (Hwang Byung Yung) is a South Korean Terran player born in 1991. He joined KTF MagicNs in August 2007 and stayed with the organisation after it became [KT Rolster](@/wiki/teams/kt-rolster.md), leaving at the end of 2013 after also playing StarCraft II for the team. In the KeSPA era his individual results were modest: early exits in the GOM Classic series and a qualification for the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md), where [JangBi](@/players/jangbi.md) eliminated him in the round of 36. He played sets in the playoffs of the [2010–11 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2010-2011-shinhan-bank-proleague.md), which KT went on to win.

Returning in 2020, he became a regular in the Afreeca Starleague, playing in [ASL Season 9](@/tournaments/asl-s9.md) and most later seasons. He reached the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 18](@/tournaments/asl-s18.md) and the semifinals of [ASL Season 20](@/tournaments/asl-s20.md).
