BoxeR (Lim Yo Hwan) is a South Korean Terran player, widely known by the nickname "The Emperor" and one of the most recognizable figures in early professional StarCraft. He won the HanbitSoft OSL and Coca-Cola OSL in 2001 and the first KPGA Tour in 2002, and was OSL runner-up four times (2001, 2002, 2004 and 2005). He also won the WCG twice.

He played for teams including Ideal Space, Orion, SK Telecom T1 and Air Force ACE, and later served as SK Telecom T1's head coach in StarCraft II. He was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.
