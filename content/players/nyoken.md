Nyoken (Daniel Eidson) is an American Terran who competed from the Game-i server era and qualified for the WCG USA Finals every year from 2003, reaching the World Finals once. He is no longer active as a player and has worked as a caster for the Bombastic StarLeague (BSL) since Season 5.
