Nyoken (Daniel Eidson) is an American Terran player and caster who has competed since the days of the Game-i server, where he reached an Elo-style rating above 1800. He qualified for the WCG USA Finals every year from 2003 and advanced to the WCG World Finals once. Over his career he played for teams including Micro Media and EG.

He is best known today as a caster for the Bombastic StarLeague, which he has covered since Season 5, alongside RAPiD in Seasons 5 to 9 and Gypsy in Seasons 10 and 11. He also competed in the league: his best run was the quarterfinals of [BSL Season 6](@/tournaments/bsl-s6.md), and he reached Lower Round 2 of the playoffs in [BSL Season 15](@/tournaments/bsl-s15.md). He also played in [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md), [BSL Season 16](@/tournaments/bsl-s16.md) and [BSL Season 18](@/tournaments/bsl-s18.md). In 2018 he lost 1–2 to [Jaeyun](@/players/jaeyun.md) in a StarCraft 20th-anniversary showmatch. He is no longer active as a player.
