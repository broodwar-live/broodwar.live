Rumble (Yoon Jong Min) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1986, who played for SK Telecom T1. A veteran Zerg of SK Telecom T1 who was mainly a 2v2 player, he reached the round of 16 of both the Bacchus and EVER OSLs in 2008.

He appears in 4 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2006 and 2008.
