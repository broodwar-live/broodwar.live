Rumble (Yoon Jong Min) is a retired South Korean Zerg player born in 1986 who spent his career with [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md) until 2009. He was mainly used in 2v2 team games while they were part of Proleague, and featured in the finals of the [2005 SKY Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2005-sky-proleague-round-1.md) and the [2005 SKY Proleague Grand Final](@/tournaments/2005-sky-proleague-grand-final.md), both won by T1. He also played in the final of the [2006 SKY Proleague Grand Final](@/tournaments/2006-sky-proleague-grand-final.md), where SKT finished runner-up.

Once 2v2 was dropped his playing time dwindled, and he applied to join Air Force ACE, though [Much](@/players/much.md) was chosen instead. His individual high point came in 2008, when he made the round of 16 of both the [2008 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2008-bacchus-osl.md) and the [2008 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2008-ever-osl.md).
