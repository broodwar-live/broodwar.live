PerfectMan (Kim Tae Gyoon) is a retired South Korean Protoss player, born in 1991. He joined [Hwaseung OZ](@/wiki/teams/hwaseung-oz.md), then still Lecaf OZ, in March 2008 and stayed until the team disbanded in 2011.

He played in the final of the [2008–09 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2008-2009-shinhan-bank-proleague.md), which OZ lost to SK Telecom T1, and in the semifinal stage of the [2011 Shinhan Bank Winners League](@/tournaments/2011-shinhan-bank-winners-league.md). One of his best-remembered games was a 2009 Proleague win over [Hwasin](@/players/hwasin.md) on Shades of Twilight, built on reaver and zealot micro. In the individual leagues he qualified for the [2010 Korean Air OSL Season 1](@/tournaments/2010-korean-air-osl-season-1.md).

After OZ folded he joined [KT Rolster](@/wiki/teams/kt-rolster.md) in January 2012 but left professional play that year instead of switching to StarCraft II. He then played in amateur and SOSPA events, reaching the round of 16 of the 7th SonicTV Starleague and playing for an ex-KT Rolster team in the Gabangpop Proleague.
