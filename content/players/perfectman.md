PerfectMan (Kim Tae Gyoon) is a South Korean Protoss player born in 1991. He played for Hwaseung OZ from 2008 and joined KT Rolster in January 2012 after OZ disbanded, but stepped away from professional play later that year rather than move to StarCraft II.

He then competed in amateur and SOSPA events, reaching the round of 16 of the 7th SonicTV Starleague and playing for ex-KT Rolster in the Gabangpop Proleague.
