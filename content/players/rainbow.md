RainBOw (Kim Sung Je) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1984, who played for Ideal Space, Orion, 4 Union and SK Telecom T1. A protégé of [IntoTheRain](/players/intotherain/), he never won a major title but became famous for his Reaver control, which shaped how the unit is used by Protoss players. He later played and coached in StarCraft II.

He appears in 7 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2002 and 2006.
