RainBOw (Kim Sung Je) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1984. A protégé of [IntoTheRain](@/players/intotherain.md), he started with Ideal Space in 2001, was brought to Orion by [BoxeR](@/players/boxer.md) in 2002, and stayed with the franchise as it became 4 Union and then [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md), where he played until 2009. He never won an individual title, but his Reaver control became the standard for how Protoss players use the unit.

His value showed in team play. He played in the deciding finals of three SKT title runs: the [2003–04 LG IBM MBC Team League](@/tournaments/2003-2004-lg-ibm-mbc-team-league.md), the [2004 Tucson MBCGame Team League](@/tournaments/2004-tucson-mbcgame-team-league.md) and the [2005 SKY Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2005-sky-proleague-round-1.md). Individually he qualified for several OSL and MSL seasons, among them the [2004 Gillette OSL](@/tournaments/2004-gillette-osl.md). He later played Terran in StarCraft II and coached for StarTale.
