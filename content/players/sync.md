Sync (Byun Gil Sup) is a retired South Korean Terran player, born in 1984, nicknamed the "Sparks Terran" after his aggressive three-Barracks Firebat and Medic build against Zerg. He debuted with [Hanbit Stars](@/wiki/teams/hanbit-stars.md) and in December 2003 moved to KTF MagicNs together with [Reach](@/players/reach.md), staying until 2008.

After an early appearance in the [2001 Coca-Cola OSL](@/tournaments/2001-coca-cola-osl.md), his breakthrough was the [2002 NATE OSL](@/tournaments/2002-nate-osl.md): he beat [fOru](@/players/foru.md) and [ChRh](@/players/chrh.md) on the way and won the title by defeating [H.O.T-Forever](@/players/hot-forever.md) 3–1. A long slump followed; he returned to form in the [2004 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2004-ever-osl.md), reaching the quarterfinals before losing to [BoxeR](@/players/boxer.md). In team leagues he appeared in several finals that his teams lost, including the [2003 LifeZone KPGA Team League](@/tournaments/2003-lifezone-kpga-team-league.md) with Hanbit and the [2005 SKY Proleague Grand Final](@/tournaments/2005-sky-proleague-grand-final.md) with KTF. Late in his career he served KTF mainly as a mentor and 2v2 player.
