Sync (Byun Gil Sup) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1984, who played for Hanbit Stars and KTF MagicNs. He debuted with Hanbit Stars and won the 2002 NATE OSL, beating [H.O.T-Forever](/players/hot-forever/) 3–1 in the final. He later moved to KTF MagicNs together with [Reach](/players/reach/).

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he won the [2002 NATE OSL](/tournaments/2002-nate-osl/).
