Goodfriend (Lee Byung Min) is a retired South Korean Terran player, born in 1986. He played for Toona SG, Pantech & Curitel Curriors, KTF MagicNs and finally [eSTRO](@/wiki/teams/estro.md) between 2003 and 2008. A patient macro player, he was a key Proleague player in his prime, yet he was often called underrated; fans turned his Hangul name typed on an English keyboard into the joke title "Count dlqudals von emfznekftm".

He was runner-up in the [2005 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2005-ever-osl.md), losing the final 3–2 to [July](@/players/july.md). He also reached the semifinals of the [2003 TriGem MSL](@/tournaments/2003-trigem-msl.md), the [2004 SPRIS MSL](@/tournaments/2004-spris-msl.md), the [2004-2005 IOPS OSL](@/tournaments/2004-2005-iops-osl.md) and the [2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 2](@/tournaments/2006-shinhan-bank-osl-season-2.md). With Pantech & Curitel he won the [2004 SKY Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2004-sky-proleague-round-2.md) and was named its Finals MVP.
