DoGGi (Choi Soo Bum) is a retired South Korean Terran player, born in 1984. He was active from 2000 to 2007 and spent his professional career with [Samsung KHAN](@/wiki/teams/samsung-khan.md), which he joined around 2002.

He played in the very first OnGameNet Starleague seasons, qualifying for the [2000 Freechal OSL](@/tournaments/2000-freechal-osl.md), and returned to the Starleague in the [2003-2004 NHN OSL](@/tournaments/2003-2004-nhn-osl.md) and the [2004 Gillette OSL](@/tournaments/2004-gillette-osl.md). His deepest individual run came in the [2004-2005 IOPS OSL](@/tournaments/2004-2005-iops-osl.md), where he reached the quarterfinals. In team play he represented Samsung in the [2003–04 LG IBM MBC Team League](@/tournaments/2003-2004-lg-ibm-mbc-team-league.md). After retiring as a player he stayed with Samsung KHAN as a coach until 2007.
