Movie (Jin Young Hwa) is a South Korean Protoss who played for CJ Entus from 2007 and moved to Team 8 in May 2012. His best result came in the 2009 EVER OSL, where he defeated ZerO in the quarterfinals and Shine in the semifinals before losing the final 1–3 to Flash. After a short time in StarCraft II following the KeSPA transition, he retired and has since played in amateur leagues.
