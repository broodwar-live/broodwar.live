Movie (Jin Young Hwa) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1990, nicknamed the "Iron Reaver". He joined [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md) in August 2007 and moved to Team 8 in May 2012.

His best individual result is the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md), where he beat [ZerO](@/players/zero.md) in the quarterfinals and [Shine](@/players/shine.md) in the semifinals before losing the final 3–1 to [Flash](@/players/flash.md). He also reached the quarterfinals of the [2010 Korean Air OSL Season 1](@/tournaments/2010-korean-air-osl-season-1.md). After a short time in StarCraft II he retired, and he has since played in amateur leagues and in the streaming-era premier leagues, including [ASL Season 2](@/tournaments/asl-s2.md), [KSL Season 1](@/tournaments/ksl-s1.md) and [ASL Season 13](@/tournaments/asl-s13.md).
