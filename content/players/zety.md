ZeTy (Erick Maraví Baca) is a Peruvian Zerg player and streamer, born in 1990, who has competed since 2009. He played for FKC until the end of 2021 and joined disturbed Mind in January 2022. With Team LaGFree he finished second in the SBWI Teamleague.

In the Bombastic StarLeague he won the BSL 12 HasuLeague, and also took part in the BSL 13 GosuLeague. He has played in the main tournament of [BSL Season 5](@/tournaments/bsl-s5.md), [BSL Season 15](@/tournaments/bsl-s15.md), [BSL Season 16](@/tournaments/bsl-s16.md) and [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md).
