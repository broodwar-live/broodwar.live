ZeTy (Erick Maraví Baca) is a Peruvian Zerg player, born in 1990, who has played for FKC and disturbed Mind. A competitive player since 2009 and a streamer, he won the BSL 12 HasuLeague and has played for disturbed Mind.

He appears in 4 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2018 and 2025.
