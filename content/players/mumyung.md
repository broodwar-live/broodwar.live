MuMyung (Sung Hak Seung) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1984, who played for Plus, SK Telecom T1, Air Force ACE and MBCGame HERO. He played for Plus before joining SK Telecom T1 in 2004, served with Air Force ACE, and later coached SK Telecom T1 and MBCGame HERO.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2005-2006 LG CYON MSL](/tournaments/2005-2006-lg-cyon-msl/).
