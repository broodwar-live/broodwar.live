MuMyung (Sung Hak Seung) is a retired South Korean Zerg player born in 1984, nicknamed "Mummy" and the "Prince of Zerg". He began with Plus, the forerunner of [OZ](@/wiki/teams/hwaseung-oz.md), and was traded to [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md) in July 2004.

At SKT he played in the upper-bracket final of the [2004 Tucson MBCGame Team League](@/tournaments/2004-tucson-mbcgame-team-league.md), which T1 won, and won his sets in the finals of the [2005 SKY Proleague Grand Final](@/tournaments/2005-sky-proleague-grand-final.md) and the [2006 SKY Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2006-sky-proleague-round-1.md), both also won by the team. His best individual run was a semifinal in the [2005-2006 LG CYON MSL](@/tournaments/2005-2006-lg-cyon-msl.md); he also qualified for several OSLs from the [2002 SKY OSL](@/tournaments/2002-sky-osl.md) onward.

After military service with [Air Force ACE](@/wiki/teams/air-force-ace.md) he returned to SKT as a coach, leaving in February 2010, and in September 2010 became head coach of [MBCGame HERO](@/wiki/teams/mbcgame-hero.md).
