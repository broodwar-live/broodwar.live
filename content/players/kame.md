Kame (Handren Ibrahim) is a Swedish Zerg player who has been active in the international scene since 2018. He is listed as a member of the team IRk and has also played under IDs such as LuRe_Nr1FanBoy and ICanFlyLow.

He has been a recurring participant in the [Bombastic StarLeague](@/tournaments/bsl.md), the league for players outside Korea, playing in its early international seasons, [BSL Season 5](@/tournaments/bsl-s5.md) and [BSL Season 6](@/tournaments/bsl-s6.md), and returning for later editions up to [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md). In all he has appeared in six BSL seasons between 2018 and 2025.
