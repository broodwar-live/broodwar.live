JinSu (Jang Jin Su) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1981, who played for AMD Dream Team and Hexatron Dream Team. He qualified for several OSL and MSL seasons in the early 2000s.

He appears in 6 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2002 and 2004.
