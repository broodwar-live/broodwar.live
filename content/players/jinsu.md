JinSu (Jang Jin Su) is a retired South Korean Zerg player, born in 1981, who was active from 2002 to 2004. He played for AMD Dream Team and stayed with the squad when it became Hexatron Dream Team in February 2004.

His best individual result was a quarterfinal in the [2002 Reebok KPGA 2nd Tour](@/tournaments/2002-reebok-kpga-2nd-tour.md). He also qualified for the [2002 Pepsi Twist KPGA 3rd Tour](@/tournaments/2002-pepsi-twist-kpga-3rd-tour.md) and the [2003 Olympus OSL](@/tournaments/2003-olympus-osl.md), and played in three MSL seasons: the [2003 Stout MSL](@/tournaments/2003-stout-msl.md), the [2004 HanaFOS CENGAME MSL](@/tournaments/2004-hanafos-cengame-msl.md) and the [2003 TriGem MSL](@/tournaments/2003-trigem-msl.md), in which he reached the lower-bracket quarterfinals. He also played sets in the [2003 LifeZone KPGA Team League](@/tournaments/2003-lifezone-kpga-team-league.md).
