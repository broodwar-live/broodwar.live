Avi-Love (Mathias Høyer) is a Danish Zerg player from Copenhagen who has competed in the foreign scene since 2005. He was a member of clan eye and later Clan Revolution. After stepping away from Brood War between 2011 and 2016 to play StarCraft II, he came back to the game with StarCraft: Remastered and took part in BSL Season 4.
