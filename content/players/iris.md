Iris (Byun Hyung Tae) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1987, also known as Berserker. He played for Greatest One, captained CJ Entus from 2006 to 2010, and finished his KeSPA career with Air Force ACE in 2012. He was runner-up of the 2007 Daum Starleague and, in 2009, reached the GOM Classic Season 3 final and the semifinals of the Avalon MSL.

After playing StarCraft II and coaching, he returned to Brood War, streaming on Afreeca and competing in SonicTV Starleagues and ASL Season 2 in 2016.
