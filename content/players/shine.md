Shine (Lee Young Han) is a South Korean Zerg player born in 1991, nicknamed the "Typhoon Zerg". He joined [WeMade FOX](@/wiki/teams/wemade-fox.md) in March 2008 and broke out in the 2009–10 season, going 5–3 in the first Proleague round.

In the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md) he upset [Bisu](@/players/bisu.md) in the round of 36, beat [EffOrt](@/players/effort.md) and [Fantasy](@/players/fantasy.md) in a group of death, then overcame [Stork](@/players/stork.md) to reach the semifinals, where [Movie](@/players/movie.md) beat him 3–1. He later reached the quarterfinals of the [2012 Tving OSL](@/tournaments/2012-tving-osl.md). Moving to [Samsung KHAN](@/wiki/teams/samsung-khan.md) in November 2011, he played in the final of the [2011–12 SK Planet Proleague Season 2](@/tournaments/2011-2012-sk-planet-proleague-season-2.md), where Samsung was runner-up. He stayed until late 2015 and became an observer for the GSL.

In the Afreeca era he reached the final of [ASL Season 3](@/tournaments/asl-s3.md) in 2017, losing 3–0 to [Flash](@/players/flash.md), and made the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 14](@/tournaments/asl-s14.md).
