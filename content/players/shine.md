Shine (Lee Young Han) is a South Korean Zerg player born in 1991. He played for WeMade FOX from 2008 and moved to Samsung KHAN in 2011, staying until 2015.

He reached the semifinals of the 2009 EVER OSL after eliminating Bisu in the round of 36. In December 2015 he became a broadcast observer for the Global StarCraft League, and he was runner-up in ASL Season 3 in 2017.
