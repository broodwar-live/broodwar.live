UltrA (Nelson Javier Céspedes Sánchez) is a Chilean Terran player, born in 1989, who has played for SouL Gaming and inte Riktigt kloka. He is regarded as Chile's strongest StarCraft: Remastered player.

He appears in 12 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2018 and 2026.
