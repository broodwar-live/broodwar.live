UltrA (Nelson Javier Céspedes Sánchez) is a Chilean Terran player born on 7 April 1989, also known by IDs such as PatrOn and Dragonite. He reached A rank on iCCup and won local events such as the 2011 Second LRM Island Tournament. He played for SouL Gaming from 2021 to February 2023 and has since represented inte Riktigt kloka (iRk). He is regarded as Chile's best StarCraft: Remastered player.

He is one of the most frequent entrants in the Bombastic StarLeague, playing in [BSL Season 5](@/tournaments/bsl-s5.md), [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md), [BSL Season 8](@/tournaments/bsl-s8.md), [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md), [BSL Season 10](@/tournaments/bsl-s10.md), [BSL Season 11](@/tournaments/bsl-s11.md), [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md), [BSL Season 14](@/tournaments/bsl-s14.md), [BSL Season 16](@/tournaments/bsl-s16.md), [BSL Season 17](@/tournaments/bsl-s17.md), [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md) and [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md). His best results are the quarterfinals of [BSL Season 12](@/tournaments/bsl-s12.md) and a run to Lower Round 4 of the playoffs in [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md).
