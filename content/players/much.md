Much (Park Yeong Min) is a retired South Korean Protoss player who debuted in 2005, played for CJ Entus from 2006 to 2009, and then served in Air Force ACE. He reached the semifinals of the Arena MSL and the Korea WCG 2008 qualifiers and won OnGameNet Star Challenge Season 2 in 2007. Fans nicknamed him "Grandpa Toss".
