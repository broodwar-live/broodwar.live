Much (Park Yeong Min) is a retired South Korean Protoss born in 1984. He debuted in 2005 with Greatest One, stayed when the team became [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md) in 2006, and became one of the scene's leading Protoss players in 2007, the year he won OnGameNet Star Challenge Season 2. Older than most of his peers, he was nicknamed "Grandpa Toss".

He reached the quarterfinals of the [2006-2007 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 3](@/tournaments/2006-2007-shinhan-bank-osl-season-3.md), the [2008 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2008-bacchus-osl.md) and the [2008 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2008-ever-osl.md). His best result was a semifinal in the [2008 Arena MSL](@/tournaments/2008-arena-msl.md); he also made the semifinals of the 2008 Korean WCG qualifiers, after which he went through a slump.

In Proleague he usually shared CJ's Protoss slot with [Movie](@/players/movie.md). He played in the finals of the [2006 SKY Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2006-sky-proleague-round-2.md) and the [2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2007-shinhan-bank-proleague-round-2.md), both lost by CJ. He enlisted in October 2009, joining [Air Force ACE](@/wiki/teams/air-force-ace.md) together with [Rumble](@/players/rumble.md).
