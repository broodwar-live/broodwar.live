Shark (Seo Gyung Jon) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1987, who played for Pirates of Space and MBCGame HERO. Drafted by Pirates of Space in 2002 as the youngest progamer of the time, he played his first televised game in 2003 and later moved to MBCGame HERO.

He appears in 4 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2006 and 2008.
