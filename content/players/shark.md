Shark (Seo Gyung Jon) is a retired South Korean Zerg player, born in 1987 in Seoul. Pirates of Space drafted him in 2002, when he drew attention as the youngest progamer of the time; he played his first televised games in September 2003 in the [2003 LifeZone KPGA Team League](@/tournaments/2003-lifezone-kpga-team-league.md). He stayed as the team became [MBCGame HERO](@/wiki/teams/mbcgame-hero.md).

He broke through in 2006. In the [2006 SKY Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2006-sky-proleague-round-1.md) his wins helped MBCGame reach the final, which they lost to SK Telecom T1, and he also played in the final of the [2006 SKY Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2006-sky-proleague-round-2.md), won by MBCGame HERO. That year he also qualified for the [2006 Pringles MSL Season 2](@/tournaments/2006-pringles-msl-season-2.md), and he later reached the [2007 Daum OSL](@/tournaments/2007-daum-osl.md) and the [2008 Arena MSL](@/tournaments/2008-arena-msl.md). He is credited with developing the modern Mutalisk control technique of clumping the flock with a distant unit.
