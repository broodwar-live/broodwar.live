Britney (Kim Bong Joon) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1991. Selected in the 2009 KeSPA Rookie Draft, he was part of Hite SPARKYZ's B-team until 2010 without notable individual results. After the KeSPA era he kept playing Brood War on the Fish server and took part in SOSPA events, including as a member of an STX SouL alumni team.
