Britney (Kim Bong Joon) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1991. He was selected in the 2009 KeSPA Rookie Draft and joined Hite SPARKYZ in March 2009, playing on the team's B-squad without producing notable results in the major leagues. His contract ended in 2010, in the aftermath of that year's match-fixing scandal, in which he was not implicated.

After the KeSPA teams moved to StarCraft II, Britney kept playing Brood War on the Fish server. He became a regular in SOSPA events, both individually and as part of an STX SouL alumni team, and later competed in Korean amateur tournaments.
