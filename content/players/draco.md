Draco (Krzysztof Nalepka) is a retired Polish Protoss player, born in 1987, regarded as one of the few foreigners of his time able to compete with top Korean progamers. He played for teams including pro Gaming, Meet Your Makers and Betson Vodoo Gaming, and was a member of the Korean team OGN SPARKYZ from August 2006 to February 2007.

He finished second at WCG Poland 2004, won several Eastern European LAN events such as the Rymarov LanCrafts, and retired from professional play in February 2010.
