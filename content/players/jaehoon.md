Jaehoon (Kim Jae Hoon) is a retired South Korean Protoss player, born in 1989. As an amateur he impressed in a televised MBCGame showmatch staged to unveil the maps Longinus and Blitz, and in August 2006 he joined [MBCGame HERO](@/wiki/teams/mbcgame-hero.md).

HERO first used him in 2v2, where he and teammate TheZerg became the team's regular pairing. He played in the final as HERO won the [2006 SKY Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2006-sky-proleague-round-2.md), and HERO were runners-up in the [2010 Shinhan Bank Winners League](@/tournaments/2010-shinhan-bank-winners-league.md). In the individual leagues he qualified for the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md). He moved to Team 8 in November 2011, then played StarCraft II for Jin Air Green Wings before retiring in August 2013.

Back in Brood War on Afreeca, he reached the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 1](@/tournaments/asl-s1.md) and also played in [ASL Season 3](@/tournaments/asl-s3.md), [KSL Season 2](@/tournaments/ksl-s2.md) and [ASL Season 8](@/tournaments/asl-s8.md).
