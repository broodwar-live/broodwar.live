Jaehoon (Kim Jae Hoon) is a South Korean Protoss player who joined MBCGame HERO in 2006 after drawing attention in a televised amateur showmatch. He was used mainly as a 2v2 specialist early on and qualified for the 2009 EVER OSL. After a spell with Team 8, he played StarCraft II for Jin Air Green Wings before retiring in August 2013, and has since played in Brood War amateur leagues.
