HoGiL (Kim Sang Wook) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1991, who played for OnGameNet SPARKYZ, Hite SPARKYZ and [Hite Entus](@/wiki/teams/hite-entus.md) between 2006 and 2011. He made his Proleague debut in the 2007–08 season but struggled at first and dropped to the minor league. During the 2008–09 season he became a regular in the lineup and, by Round 4, the team's most-used Zerg.

He qualified for seven premier-league seasons between 2008 and 2010, among them the [2009 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2009-bacchus-osl.md), the [2009-2010 NATE MSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-nate-msl.md) and the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md), without getting past the opening stage. He retired in February 2011, saying he had lost interest in the game. After completing his military service he returned to Brood War in the streaming era, playing in events such as the 41 Starleague.
