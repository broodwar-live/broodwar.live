HoGiL (Kim Sang Wook) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1991, who has played for OnGameNet SPARKYZ, Hite SPARKYZ and Hite Entus. He became the most-used Zerg of the SPARKYZ Proleague lineup during the 2008–09 season. After retiring and completing his military service, he returned to Brood War competition in the streaming era.

He appears in 7 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2008 and 2010.
