Sharp (Jo Ki Seok) is a South Korean Terran born in 1992. He joined [KT Rolster](@/wiki/teams/kt-rolster.md) in 2009 and later played for [Samsung KHAN](@/wiki/teams/samsung-khan.md), appearing for them in the playoffs of the [2010–11 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2010-2011-shinhan-bank-proleague.md). In October 2012 he announced his retirement, citing military service, but instead began streaming on Afreeca and playing online events; he won his first tournament in August 2014, beating [Free](@/players/free.md) in the final.

He has been one of the most consistent players of the Afreeca era. He was runner-up in the inaugural [ASL Season 1](@/tournaments/asl-s1.md), swept 3–0 by [Shuttle](@/players/shuttle.md), and lost two later finals 4–1 to [Soulkey](@/players/soulkey.md): [KSL Season 2](@/tournaments/ksl-s2.md) in 2018 and [ASL Season 18](@/tournaments/asl-s18.md) in 2024. He also reached the semifinals of [ASL Season 17](@/tournaments/asl-s17.md) and the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 6](@/tournaments/asl-s6.md), [ASL Season 7](@/tournaments/asl-s7.md), [ASL Season 8](@/tournaments/asl-s8.md), [ASL Season 16](@/tournaments/asl-s16.md), [KSL Season 1](@/tournaments/ksl-s1.md) and [KSL Season 3](@/tournaments/ksl-s3.md).
