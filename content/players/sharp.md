Sharp (Jo Ki Seok) is a South Korean Terran player who began with KT Rolster in 2009 and later played for Samsung KHAN until announcing his retirement in October 2012. He went on to stream on Afreeca and compete in online tournaments, winning his first event in August 2014.

In the post-KeSPA era he has been runner-up in ASL Season 1 (2016), KSL Season 2 (2018) and ASL Season 18 (2024).
