cRoSs (Jihi Chun) is a Canadian Zerg player, born in 1993, who has competed in StarCraft: Remastered events since 2019. He has been one of the most consistent entrants of the Bombastic StarLeague, appearing in every season from [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md) to [BSL Season 14](@/tournaments/bsl-s14.md) and again from [BSL Season 17](@/tournaments/bsl-s17.md) onward.

His best result came in [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md), where he reached the grand final and finished runner-up after losing 1–5 to [Dewalt](@/players/dewalt.md). In [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md) he had reached the third round of the lower bracket. He has also played as cRoSsy and cR, and has represented the teams vT and Wico.
