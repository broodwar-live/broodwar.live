cRoSs (Jihi Chun) is a Canadian Zerg player, born in 1993. He competes in StarCraft: Remastered events, including the Bombastic StarLeague.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he finished runner-up in the [BSL Season 21](/tournaments/bsl-s21/).
