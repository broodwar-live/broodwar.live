UpMaGiC (Shin Hee Sung) is a former South Korean Terran progamer, born in 1989. He started with eNature Top Team in 2006 and moved to [eSTRO](@/wiki/teams/estro.md) later that year, becoming the team's ace. Nicknamed "Southpaw", he was known for creative, mind-game-heavy strategies, and his "Wachanic" build helped make mech a standard approach in TvZ.

His best individual result was a semifinal in the [2007 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2007-ever-osl.md). He also reached the quarterfinals of the [2008 GOMTV MSL Season 4](@/tournaments/2008-gomtv-msl-season-4.md) and the round of 16 in the [2006-2007 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 3](@/tournaments/2006-2007-shinhan-bank-osl-season-3.md), the [2009 Avalon MSL](@/tournaments/2009-avalon-msl.md) and the [2009 Lost Saga MSL](@/tournaments/2009-lost-saga-msl.md). In 2010 he was found to have been involved in the match-fixing scandal and was permanently banned by KeSPA.
