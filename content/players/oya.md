OyA (Johan Persson) is a Swedish Protoss player born in 1996, sometimes called "Saunatoss". Before settling with team iRk (inte Riktigt kloka) at the start of 2018, he played for iG, Los Reyes Del Mambo Evolutions and Neo.G.

He has been one of the more consistent Europeans in the Bombastic StarLeague. After debuting in [BSL Season 8](@/tournaments/bsl-s8.md), he reached the quarterfinals of [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md) and made his deepest run in [BSL Season 11](@/tournaments/bsl-s11.md), finishing in the semifinals. He returned to the quarterfinals in [BSL Season 12](@/tournaments/bsl-s12.md) and [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md), and has kept qualifying since, with appearances through [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md).
