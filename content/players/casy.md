Casy (Han Dong Wook) is a South Korean former professional Terran player, born in 1986. He played for KOR, which became OnGameNet SPARKYZ, and later for WeMade FOX and Air Force ACE. He won the 2006 Shinhan Bank OnGameNet Starleague Season 1, defeating ChoJJa 3–1 in the final, and also finished fourth in the ShinHan 2005 OSL and in Shinhan Bank OSL Season 3.

He was nicknamed the "Neo Emperor" for the similarities fans saw between his style and BoxeR's.
