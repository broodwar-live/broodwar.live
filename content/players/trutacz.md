trutaCz (Michał Szarejko) is a Polish Zerg player, born in 1994, who plays for iRk; his ID is a Polish spelling of "true touch". He started playing Brood War in 2005 and joined the Polish national team as a 2v2 player in 2009, partnering [Radley](@/players/radley.md). His 1v1 breakthrough came in 2012, when he won both Netwars Cups on the strength of his Zerg mirror play, and in January 2013 he won the second TeamLiquid Legacy Starleague qualifier over [Bakuryu](@/players/bakuryu.md).

He is the most successful player in Bombastic StarLeague history. He won the first two seasons, [BSL Season 1](@/tournaments/bsl-s1.md) and [BSL Season 2](@/tournaments/bsl-s2.md), both over [ZZZero](@/players/zzzero.md). He later won [BSL Season 6](@/tournaments/bsl-s6.md) against [Bonyth](@/players/bonyth.md) and [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md) against [eOnzErG](@/players/eonzerg.md). In [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md) he finished runner-up after a 0–4 final against Bonyth. He also reached the semifinals of [BSL Season 5](@/tournaments/bsl-s5.md) and [BSL Season 12](@/tournaments/bsl-s12.md).
