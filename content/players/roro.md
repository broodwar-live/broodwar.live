RorO (Shin No Yeol) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1991, who played for WeMade FOX and Samsung KHAN. He became WeMade FOX's most-used Zerg after a six-game Proleague winning streak in 2008–09 that included wins over [Jaedong](/players/jaedong/) and [BoxeR](/players/boxer/), and later played StarCraft II for Samsung KHAN.

He appears in 6 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2009 and 2012.
