RorO (Shin No Yeol) is a retired South Korean Zerg player born on 19 January 1991. He joined [WeMade FOX](@/wiki/teams/wemade-fox.md) in March 2008 and, after the team was disbanded, was drafted by [Samsung KHAN](@/wiki/teams/samsung-khan.md) in November 2011, staying until 2014 and also playing StarCraft II there.

After losing his first two televised Proleague games in 2008–09, he won six in a row, beating [Jaedong](@/players/jaedong.md), [July](@/players/july.md), [Calm](@/players/calm.md) and [BoxeR](@/players/boxer.md) among others, and became WeMade's most-used Zerg. He went 4–1 in sets for WeMade over the first two rounds of the [2009–10 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2009-2010-shinhan-bank-proleague.md) recorded on this site.

Individually he qualified for the [2009 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2009-bacchus-osl.md), the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md), the [2011 Jin Air OSL](@/tournaments/2011-jin-air-osl.md) and the [2012 Tving OSL](@/tournaments/2012-tving-osl.md), and his best result was the round of 16 of the [2010 Bigfile MSL](@/tournaments/2010-bigfile-msl.md).
