MadiNho (Geir-Helge Løvold) is a Norwegian Terran player, born in 1989, who has played for AoV, LRM, Clan Revolution and inte Riktigt kloka. Regarded as one of the strongest Norwegian players, he is a regular on the ladder and in team leagues, plays strong off-races and 2v2, and has represented clans such as AoV and Clan Revolution before joining inte Riktigt kloka.

He appears in 9 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2020 and 2026.
