MadiNho (Geir-Helge Løvold) is a Norwegian Terran player, born in 1989, regarded as one of the strongest players from Norway. He played for LRM from 2011 to 2013, AoV from 2016 to 2018 and Clan Revolution from 2018 to 2020, and has been with inte Riktigt kloka (iRk) since December 2020. A dedicated ladder player, he reached the top ten of the iCCup ladder and S rank on the Remastered ladder, and he is also known as a strong 2v2 player with solid off-races.

In the [Bombastic StarLeague](@/tournaments/bsl.md) he reached the quarterfinals of [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md) and made the playoffs of [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md). He has played in nine BSL seasons in all.
