Mighty (Lee Gwang Yong) is a South Korean Protoss player, born on 28 August 1995, who competes as an amateur rather than as a former KeSPA team-league professional. He plays for the amateur team SouLGaming under the ID SouL)P(Mighty and streams on SOOP. Korean fans also know him by the nickname 프발, and Chinese-speaking viewers by the names 梅婷 and 刚勇.
