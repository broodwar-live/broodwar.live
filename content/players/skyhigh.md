sKyHigh (Jo Byung Se) is a South Korean Terran player born in 1991 who spent his entire career, from 2007 to 2016, with [CJ Entus](@/wiki/teams/cj-entus.md). Drafted in April 2007, he first stood out in the 2008–09 Averatec-Intel Classic Season 2, beating [NaDa](@/players/nada.md) and [Best](@/players/best.md) before [Bisu](@/players/bisu.md) swept him in the semifinals. He was named rookie of the [2008–09 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2008-2009-shinhan-bank-proleague.md).

His defining moment came in the final of the [2009 Shinhan Bank Winners League](@/tournaments/2009-shinhan-bank-winners-league.md): coming in for CJ against Hwaseung OZ, he survived a 5-pool from [Jaedong](@/players/jaedong.md) and went on to beat four OZ players in a row, finishing with [HiyA](@/players/hiya.md), a reverse all-kill that clinched the title. Individual leagues were less fruitful; [ggaemo](@/players/ggaemo.md) eliminated him from the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md) in the round of 36. Nicknamed the "Neo-Berserker" for a TvP style resembling teammate [Iris](@/players/iris.md)'s, he later played StarCraft II and coached for CJ.
