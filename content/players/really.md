Really (Park Sang Woo) is a retired South Korean Terran player born in 1989. He joined [eSTRO](@/wiki/teams/estro.md) in March 2007 and, thanks to solid, steady play, overtook [UpMaGiC](@/players/upmagic.md) to become the team's ace in the 2009–10 Proleague. He went 8–3 in the first round and 10–4 in the second, beating [Fantasy](@/players/fantasy.md), [Flash](@/players/flash.md) and [JangBi](@/players/jangbi.md) on the way and stretching an unbeaten run against Protoss to 11 games.

Individual leagues were less kind: he qualified for the [2009-2010 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-ever-osl.md) but [Shine](@/players/shine.md) knocked him out in the round of 36, and he also appeared in the [2008-2009 Batoo OSL](@/tournaments/2008-2009-batoo-osl.md), the [2010 Bigfile MSL](@/tournaments/2010-bigfile-msl.md) and the [2011 ABC Mart MSL](@/tournaments/2011-abc-mart-msl.md). When eSTRO disbanded in 2010 he was drafted by [Woongjin Stars](@/wiki/teams/woongjin-stars.md) and played in the [2010–11 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2010-2011-shinhan-bank-proleague.md) playoffs. He announced his retirement on 3 August 2011.
