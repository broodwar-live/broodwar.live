SoO (Park Jong Soo) is a retired South Korean Protoss player born on 21 May 1988, who also used the IDs CreationToss and Soo_STX. He spent his career with [STX SouL](@/wiki/teams/stx-soul.md). He should not be confused with the Zerg player n.Die_soO.

He reached team-league play young. He played in the final of the [2004 SKY Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2004-sky-proleague-round-2.md), which SouL lost 1–4 to Pantech & Curitel Curriors, and appeared in the playoffs of the [2004 Tucson MBCGame Team League](@/tournaments/2004-tucson-mbcgame-team-league.md), the [2004–05 MBCMovies MBCGame Team League](@/tournaments/2004-2005-mbcmovies-mbcgame-team-league.md) and the [2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague Round 1](@/tournaments/2007-shinhan-bank-proleague-round-1.md).

Individually he qualified for the [2008 Incruit OSL](@/tournaments/2008-incruit-osl.md) and the [2008-2009 Batoo OSL](@/tournaments/2008-2009-batoo-osl.md) as an STX player.
