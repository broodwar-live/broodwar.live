JyJ (Jung Young Jae) is a South Korean Terran player born on 24 January 1993. He was a member of [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md) from 2008 until November 2013.

He made his name in the AfreecaTV era. In October 2018 he qualified for [KSL Season 2](@/tournaments/ksl-s2.md) with a 2–0 win over [Snow](@/players/snow.md), a former CJ Entus player then regarded as one of the best against Terran. He became a regular in the ASL, reaching the quarterfinals of [ASL Season 11](@/tournaments/asl-s11.md) and [ASL Season 13](@/tournaments/asl-s13.md).

In 2023 he won [ASL Season 15](@/tournaments/asl-s15.md), beating fellow Terran [Mind](@/players/mind.md) 4–1 in the final. In the next season he again reached the quarterfinals, in [ASL Season 16](@/tournaments/asl-s16.md).
