Lee (Lee Woon Jae) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1982, who played for Hanbit Stars, KTF and Pirates of Space. He qualified for several of the early OSL seasons.

He appears in 5 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2001 and 2003.
