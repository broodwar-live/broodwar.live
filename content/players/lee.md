Lee (Lee Woon Jae) is a retired South Korean Terran player, born in 1982, from the early years of professional Brood War. Nicknamed "The Real Marine", he played for [Hanbit Stars](@/wiki/teams/hanbit-stars.md) from 2001 to 2002, for KTF from October 2003 into 2004, and then for Pirates of Space until 2005.

He qualified for several early premier-league seasons: the [2001 Hanbitsoft OSL](@/tournaments/2001-hanbitsoft-osl.md), the [2001 Coca-Cola OSL](@/tournaments/2001-coca-cola-osl.md), the [2002 Pepsi Twist KPGA 3rd Tour](@/tournaments/2002-pepsi-twist-kpga-3rd-tour.md), the [2002-2003 Panasonic OSL](@/tournaments/2002-2003-panasonic-osl.md) and the [2003 Olympus OSL](@/tournaments/2003-olympus-osl.md). With KTF he also played in the [2003–04 LG IBM MBC Team League](@/tournaments/2003-2004-lg-ibm-mbc-team-league.md).
