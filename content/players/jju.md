JJu (Byun Eun Jong) is a retired South Korean Zerg player, born in 1983. He played for STX SouL from 2002 and joined Samsung KHAN in 2005, helping the team win the first KeSPA Cup that year and earning the event's MVP award. He placed fourth in the NHN Hangame OSL.

A former amateur boxer, he was nicknamed the "Straight Zerg" and was known for an unpredictable style and for wearing sunglasses during matches.
