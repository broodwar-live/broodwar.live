JJu (Byun Eun Jong) is a retired South Korean Zerg player, born in 1983. A former amateur boxer, he was nicknamed the "Straight Zerg" after a saying of his, and in his Samsung years he became known for unpredictable openings such as 4- and 5-pools and for wearing sunglasses in matches.

He began his career with Soul, the team that became [STX SouL](@/wiki/teams/stx-soul.md), and moved to [Samsung KHAN](@/wiki/teams/samsung-khan.md) in April 2005; within months he helped KHAN win the first KeSPA Cup and was named its MVP. Individually he reached the semifinals of the [2003-2004 NHN OSL](@/tournaments/2003-2004-nhn-osl.md), finishing fourth, as well as the [2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 1](@/tournaments/2006-shinhan-bank-osl-season-1.md) and the [2006 Pringles MSL Season 2](@/tournaments/2006-pringles-msl-season-2.md). With Soul he played in the final of the [2004 SKY Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2004-sky-proleague-round-2.md), where his team finished runner-up. He retired in 2008.
