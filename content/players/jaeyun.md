Jaeyun (David Jaeyun Kim) is an American Protoss player born on 27 September 1990. Before stepping away in 2012 he played minor foreign events, the USA B-Team and the Collegiate Star League for UC San Diego, where he won a deciding ace match against NonY to complete a comeback from 3–1 down and give his team the championship.

He returned with StarCraft: Remastered as a Twitch streamer, caster and coach known for macro-oriented, analytical play. He placed third at the Twitch Holiday Bash and beat Nyoken 2–1 in a StarCraft 20th-anniversary showmatch in 2018.

In the Bombastic StarLeague his best result is the semifinals of [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md). He also played in [BSL Season 6](@/tournaments/bsl-s6.md), [BSL Season 8](@/tournaments/bsl-s8.md), [BSL Season 10](@/tournaments/bsl-s10.md), [BSL Season 12](@/tournaments/bsl-s12.md), [BSL Season 14](@/tournaments/bsl-s14.md), [BSL Season 16](@/tournaments/bsl-s16.md) and [BSL Season 18](@/tournaments/bsl-s18.md).
