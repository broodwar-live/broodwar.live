Jaeyun (David Jaeyun Kim) is an American Protoss player, born in 1990. He first played in foreign team and collegiate leagues, including for UC San Diego in the Collegiate Star League, before stepping away in 2012. He returned with StarCraft: Remastered as a streamer, caster and coach.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [BSL Season 7](/tournaments/bsl-s7/).
