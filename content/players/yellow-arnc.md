YellOw\[ArnC\] (Park Myung Soo) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1987, who played for KOR, OnGameNet SPARKYZ and Hite SPARKYZ. Because he shares the ID with Hong Jin-ho ([YellOw](/players/yellow/)), he is often called "Fake YellOw"; he is a different player. Known for his Zerg-versus-Terran play, he reached the round of 8 of the 2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 1 and was the runner-up of the 2009 Bacchus OSL.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he finished runner-up in the [2009 Bacchus OSL](/tournaments/2009-bacchus-osl/).
