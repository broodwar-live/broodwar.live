ZergMaN (Park Sung Joon) is a South Korean Zerg player, born in 1984, who played for Samsung KHAN. Known for his Zerg-versus-Zerg play, he reached the round of 8 in three consecutive Shinhan Bank OSL seasons while playing for Samsung KHAN.

He appears in 5 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2005 and 2006.
