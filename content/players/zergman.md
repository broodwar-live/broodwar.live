ZergMaN (Park Sung Joon) is a former South Korean Zerg player born in 1984 who played for [Samsung KHAN](@/wiki/teams/samsung-khan.md). He shares his real name with [July](@/players/july.md). His ZvZ was exceptional, and at his peak he beat [JJu](@/players/jju.md), [GoRush](@/players/gorush.md) and July in best-of-three series, though his ZvT lagged far behind.

After qualifying for the [2005 So1 OSL](@/tournaments/2005-so1-osl.md) and the [2005-2006 Shinhan Bank OSL](@/tournaments/2005-2006-shinhan-bank-osl.md), he reached the quarterfinals of three consecutive OSLs: the [2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 1](@/tournaments/2006-shinhan-bank-osl-season-1.md), the [2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 2](@/tournaments/2006-shinhan-bank-osl-season-2.md) and the [2006-2007 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 3](@/tournaments/2006-2007-shinhan-bank-osl-season-3.md). Twice he went out to the eventual champions, [Casy](@/players/casy.md) and [NaDa](@/players/nada.md). He later played in amateur leagues.
