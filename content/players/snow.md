SnOw (Jang Yoon Chul) is a South Korean Protoss player who played for CJ Entus (Hite Entus during 2010–11) from 2008 to 2013. He broke through in the 09–10 Proleague with a 16–8 record, and his best KeSPA-era Starleague result was a Ro8 finish in the PDPop MSL. He enlisted for military service in March 2015 and returned to Afreeca in December 2016.

In the ASL he has been runner-up three times: Season 5 (2018), Season 8 (2019) and Season 20 (2025).
