kogeT (Marcin Nowak) is a Polish player born in 1988 who lived in Łódź until 2013 and has since been based in Copenhagen. Playing online since 2002, he spent years in Polish and international clans and leads team Netwars. He played Zerg from 2006, came back from a break in 2013, and switched to Terran in 2014. Offline highlights include second at a 2008 PGA tournament in Poznań and first at Rymarov LanCraft Summer.

In the Bombastic StarLeague he was runner-up in [BSL Season 4](@/tournaments/bsl-s4.md), losing 4–3 to [Dandy](@/players/dandy.md). He followed it with a semifinal in [BSL Season 5](@/tournaments/bsl-s5.md) and a quarterfinal in [BSL Season 11](@/tournaments/bsl-s11.md), and also played in Seasons 6, 9, 10, 12 and 19. He also creates maps for StarCraft and other games.
