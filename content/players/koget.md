kogeT (Marcin Nowak) is a Polish Terran player, born in 1988, who has lived in Copenhagen, Denmark, since 2013. Active online since 2002, he played mainly Zerg from 2006 to 2014 before switching to Terran, and led team Netwars. His results include second place at a 2008 PGA tournament in Poznań, a win at Rymarov LanCraft Summer, and later third place in the TeamLiquid Legacy Starleague Championship and second place in Bombastic StarLeague 4.
