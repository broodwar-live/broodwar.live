BoA (John Dong) is a Canadian Protoss player, born in 1993, who lives in the United States. He has been a member of Crazy Warriors Team since January 2022.

He is a regular in the [Bombastic StarLeague](@/tournaments/bsl.md), the international league for players outside Korea. His best results there are quarterfinal appearances in [BSL Season 11](@/tournaments/bsl-s11.md) and [BSL Season 14](@/tournaments/bsl-s14.md). He also made the playoffs of [BSL Season 15](@/tournaments/bsl-s15.md), going out in the lower bracket, and has played in Seasons 10, 13 and 16 through 19.
