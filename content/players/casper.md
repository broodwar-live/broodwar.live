Casper (Sergey Nosov) is a Russian Terran player, born in 1989. After a long period of inactivity he qualified for BSL Season 6 through the ladder qualifier and became one of the surprises of its round of 24.

He appears in 6 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2019 and 2021.
