Casper (Sergey Nosov) is a Russian Terran player born in 1989. Over the years he has played under several IDs, including iG.Casper and iFU.Casper, reflecting stints with the iG and iFU clans.

After a long spell away from competition he came back through the ladder qualifier of [BSL Season 6](@/tournaments/bsl-s6.md) and was one of the unexpected names in that season's round of 24. He stayed a fixture of the Bombastic StarLeague over the following two years, also playing in [BSL Season 7](@/tournaments/bsl-s7.md), [BSL Season 8](@/tournaments/bsl-s8.md), [BSL Season 9](@/tournaments/bsl-s9.md), [BSL Season 10](@/tournaments/bsl-s10.md) and [BSL Season 11](@/tournaments/bsl-s11.md), which places his active period at 2019 to 2021.
