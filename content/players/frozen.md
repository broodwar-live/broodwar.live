FrOzen (Kim Dong Kun) is a retired South Korean Terran player born on 29 November 1988, who also played under the ID FrOzean. He began his career with [Samsung KHAN](@/wiki/teams/samsung-khan.md) and was traded to [STX SouL](@/wiki/teams/stx-soul.md) in late 2009.

His best starleague run was the quarterfinals of the [2008 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2008-bacchus-osl.md). He reached the round of 16 of both the [2008 Club Day Online MSL](@/tournaments/2008-club-day-online-msl.md) and the [2009 Lost Saga MSL](@/tournaments/2009-lost-saga-msl.md), going out to NaDa and [JangBi](@/players/jangbi.md) respectively. In the [2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague Grand Final](@/tournaments/2007-shinhan-bank-proleague-grand-final.md), which Samsung lost to Lecaf OZ, he won the opening set against [Jaedong](@/players/jaedong.md).

His 2008–09 Proleague season ended at 0 wins and 12 losses, the worst record in the league. After the move to STX he improved to an even 5–5 over the first two rounds of 2009–10.
