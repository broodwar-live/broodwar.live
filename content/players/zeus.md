Zeus (Jun Tae Gyu) is a South Korean Protoss player, born in 1984, who played for KOR, OnGameNet SPARKYZ and Hite SPARKYZ. One of the original professional Protoss players, he stayed with the KOR / SPARKYZ team throughout his career and captained it from October 2008 until his retirement in December 2009, and was known for his match ceremonies.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he finished runner-up in the [2003-2004 NHN OSL](/tournaments/2003-2004-nhn-osl/) and reached the semifinals of the [2003 Stout MSL](/tournaments/2003-stout-msl/).
