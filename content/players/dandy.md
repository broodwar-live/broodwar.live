Dandy (Luis Enrique Cutipa Orco) is a Peruvian Terran player, born in 1991, and one of the strongest players from Latin America. He plays for SouLGaming and has also used IDs such as LG)LUiS and El_Cheroka.

His biggest result is the title in [BSL Season 4](@/tournaments/bsl-s4.md) of the [Bombastic StarLeague](@/tournaments/bsl.md), where he beat [kogeT](@/players/koget.md) 4–3 in the final. He made the playoffs of [BSL Season 3](@/tournaments/bsl-s3.md), reaching the late rounds of the lower bracket, reached the semifinals of [BSL Season 8](@/tournaments/bsl-s8.md), and made the quarterfinals in Seasons 5, 6, 12 and 14.
