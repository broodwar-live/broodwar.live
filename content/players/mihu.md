Mihu (Hu Chunxu) is a Chinese Terran player, born in 1989, from Huainan in Anhui province. Known to Chinese fans as "Mihu the Great", he is widely regarded as the best player in China and the strongest Terran outside Korea. He has played full-time since 2019 and represents team Y.H. At home he won six consecutive China Seasons League titles, and in 2023 he added the Wuhan Offline Cup and Platinum StarCraft League S1. In April 2021 he passed 2700 MMR and ranked third on the Korean Remastered ladder.

He has dominated the Bombastic StarLeague. He won [BSL Season 13](@/tournaments/bsl-s13.md) and [BSL Season 14](@/tournaments/bsl-s14.md), both times beating [Dewalt](@/players/dewalt.md) in the final, then took [BSL Season 16](@/tournaments/bsl-s16.md) with a 5–0 sweep of [Bonyth](@/players/bonyth.md), [BSL Season 17](@/tournaments/bsl-s17.md) over Dewalt again and [BSL Season 19](@/tournaments/bsl-s19.md) over [Zhanhun](@/players/zhanhun.md). In [BSL Season 18](@/tournaments/bsl-s18.md) he went out in the last round of the lower bracket.
