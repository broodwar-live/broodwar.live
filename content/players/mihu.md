Mihu (Hu Chunxu) is a Chinese Terran player, regarded as the top player in China and one of the strongest non-Korean players. Playing full-time since 2019, he won six consecutive China Seasons League (CSL) titles and, in 2023, the Wuhan Offline Cup and Platinum StarCraft League S1. Internationally he won BSL 13 in 2021 and defended the title at BSL 14 in 2022. He plays for team Y.H.
