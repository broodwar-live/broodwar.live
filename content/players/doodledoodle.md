DoodleDoodle (Wayne Cheon) is a Canadian Protoss player born on 8 November 1991. He plays StarCraft: Remastered for North American Squad and has also used the IDs Doodle, Doodle2x and ddlddl.

His results on this site come from the Bombastic StarLeague, where he has competed since 2024. He played in [BSL Season 18](@/tournaments/bsl-s18.md) and [BSL Season 21](@/tournaments/bsl-s21.md), reached the second round of the lower bracket in [BSL Season 20](@/tournaments/bsl-s20.md), and reached the first lower-bracket round of [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md).
