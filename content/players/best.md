Best (Doh Jae Wook) is a South Korean Protoss player who spent his entire KeSPA career with SK Telecom T1 from 2006 to 2013. He reached the final of the 2008 EVER OSL, finishing runner-up. After leaving professional play he completed his military service and returned to streaming on Afreeca in 2015.

In the post-KeSPA era he was runner-up in ASL Season 19 (2025).
