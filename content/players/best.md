Best (Doh Jae Wook) is a South Korean Protoss player who spent his entire KeSPA career, from 2006 to 2013, with [SK Telecom T1](@/wiki/teams/sk-telecom-t1.md) and stayed with the team after its switch to StarCraft II. He first drew attention at WCG 2006. In 2008 he reached the quarterfinals of the [2008 Bacchus OSL](@/tournaments/2008-bacchus-osl.md). He then finished runner-up in the [2008 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2008-ever-osl.md), where [July](@/players/july.md) beat him 3–0 in the final. He also reached the semifinals of the [2008 Incruit OSL](@/tournaments/2008-incruit-osl.md). In Proleague he played in the finals won by SKT in the [2008–09 Shinhan Bank Proleague](@/tournaments/2008-2009-shinhan-bank-proleague.md) and the [2011–12 SK Planet Proleague Season 1](@/tournaments/2011-2012-sk-planet-proleague-season-1.md).

After military service, which ended in September 2015, he returned as an AfreecaTV streamer. He reached the semifinals of [ASL Season 2](@/tournaments/asl-s2.md) and [ASL Season 15](@/tournaments/asl-s15.md), and was runner-up in [ASL Season 19](@/tournaments/asl-s19.md), losing the final 3–4 to [Soulkey](@/players/soulkey.md).
