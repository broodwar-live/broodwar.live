Rain is one of the most prominent Protoss players of the modern era. Rain's signature style revolves around precise storm drops, aggressive gateway pressure, and clutch performances in elimination matches that have earned him a reputation as a big-game player. His ability to read opponent strategies and adjust mid-series is unmatched among active Protoss players. Beyond his individual brilliance, Rain's sustained excellence across dozens of ASL seasons demonstrates a level of consistency that few players in any era have achieved. He remains the gold standard for modern Protoss play.
