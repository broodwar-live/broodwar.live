Rain (Jung Yoon Jong), formerly known as By.Sun, is a South Korean Protoss player drafted by MBCGame HERO in 2009 who joined SK Telecom T1 in 2010. He was named Rookie of the Year for the 10–11 Proleague and recorded an all-kill of Hwaseung OZ in the STX Masters final, before moving to StarCraft II with SKT.

He returned to Brood War streaming on Afreeca in 2016. He won ASL Season 5 (2018) and KSL Season 3 (2019), and was runner-up in KSL Season 4 (2019) and ASL Season 13 (2022).
