WolFix (Daniel Wilczyński) is a Polish Terran player, born in 1991. He came up through Polish servers and clans in the mid-2000s, appeared in the first Bombastic StarLeague season in 2013, and has been a regular in the league again since 2023.

He appears in 7 seasons covered on broodwar.live, between 2013 and 2026.
