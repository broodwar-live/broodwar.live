WolFix (Daniel Wilczyński) is a Polish Terran player born in 1991. He started around 2004 on the small Polish server Starbnet and moved to Netcraft in 2005, passing through clans such as Executors, RAF and Akademia Pana Kleksa before settling with Army From Future until StarCraft II came out. Better as Protoss but choosing Terran because Poland had few of them, he later joined the Polish national B team.

After a spell in StarCraft II and League of Legends he came back with Remastered, regularly reaching S rank on the ladder without joining a team. He played in [BSL Season 1](@/tournaments/bsl-s1.md) in 2013, and since [BSL Season 17](@/tournaments/bsl-s17.md) he has qualified for every season through [BSL Season 22](@/tournaments/bsl-s22.md). He has also coached in the CPL and appeared in the BSL Proleague.
