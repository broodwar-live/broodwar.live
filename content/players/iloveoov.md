iloveoov (Choi Yun Sung) is a retired South Korean Terran player born in 1983. He joined BoxeR's team in 2003, which became SK Telecom T1, and stayed with the organization until 2011.

He won three MSL titles (TriGem MSL 2003, HanaFOS CENGAME MSL 2004 and SPRIS MSL 2004) and two OSL titles (EVER Starleague 2004 and Shinhan Bank Starleague 2005). He was later head coach of SK Telecom T1 in StarCraft II and was inducted into the Korean esports Hall of Fame in 2012.
