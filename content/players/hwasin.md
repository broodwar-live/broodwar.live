Hwasin (Jin Yung Soo) is a retired South Korean Terran player, born in 1988, who played for [STX SouL](@/wiki/teams/stx-soul.md) from 2004 to 2010 and was for years the team's leading player. Nicknamed the "Red Sniper" for his Terran-versus-Zerg play, he rounded out his other matchups in 2007 and won that year's Korean WCG qualifier.

He was a fixture of the individual leagues without ever reaching a final. His deepest run was a semifinal in the [2006-2007 GOMTV MSL Season 1](@/tournaments/2006-2007-gomtv-msl-season-1.md), and he reached the quarterfinals of the [2007 Daum OSL](@/tournaments/2007-daum-osl.md), the [2007 EVER OSL](@/tournaments/2007-ever-osl.md), the [2007 GOMTV MSL Season 2](@/tournaments/2007-gomtv-msl-season-2.md), the [2008 Arena MSL](@/tournaments/2008-arena-msl.md), the [2008-2009 Batoo OSL](@/tournaments/2008-2009-batoo-osl.md) and the [2009-2010 NATE MSL](@/tournaments/2009-2010-nate-msl.md). With his team he played in the final of the [2004 SKY Proleague Round 2](@/tournaments/2004-sky-proleague-round-2.md), where SouL finished second.
