Hwasin (Jin Yung Soo) is a South Korean Terran player, born in 1988, who played for STX SouL. Long the star player of STX SouL, he was noted for his Terran-versus-Zerg play and won the Korean WCG qualifier in 2007. He repeatedly reached late rounds of individual leagues without making a final.

Among the seasons covered on broodwar.live, he reached the semifinals of the [2006-2007 GOMTV MSL Season 1](/tournaments/2006-2007-gomtv-msl-season-1/).
