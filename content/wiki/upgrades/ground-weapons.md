Ground Weapons adds damage per level: +1 to each of the Zealot's two Psi Blade strikes (so +2 per attack), +2 to the Dragoon, and +3 to the Dark Templar and the Archon. The Reaver's Scarabs are not affected — they have their own Scarab Damage upgrade. Levels 2 and 3 need a Templar Archives. Its cost rises by 50 minerals and gas per level.

It is a staple of gateway-heavy Protoss play in every matchup and is commonly started as soon as a Forge is available in the mid game.
