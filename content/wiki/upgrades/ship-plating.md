Each level of Ship Plating adds 1 armor to the Wraith, Dropship, Science Vessel, Battlecruiser and Valkyrie. Levels 2 and 3 need a completed Science Facility.

It is a low-priority upgrade in most games and appears mainly when Terran commits to a large air fleet, such as Battlecruisers in the late game.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 150 / 150 | 266 s | — |

| 3 | 300 / 300 | 330 s | Science Facility |

Research times are in game seconds at Normal speed (frames ÷ 15), the convention used across this wiki.
