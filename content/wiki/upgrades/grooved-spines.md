Grooved Spines adds 1 range (32 pixels) to the Hydralisk's Needle Spines, raising it from 4 to 5 against both ground and air targets. It is researched at the Hydralisk Den and does not require a Lair.

Together with Muscular Augments it is standard in Hydralisk-based play.
