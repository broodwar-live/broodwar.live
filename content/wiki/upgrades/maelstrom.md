Maelstrom costs 100 energy and stuns all biological units in an area for several seconds; mechanical units are not affected. It is researched at the Templar Archives. See the [Maelstrom ability](/wiki/abilities/maelstrom/) page.

It is used against Zerg, whose units are all biological — for example to freeze Mutalisks or Lurkers for Archons, Corsairs or Reavers to finish.
