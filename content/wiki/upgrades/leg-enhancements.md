Leg Enhancements raises the Zealot's movement speed, letting Zealots catch retreating units and reinforce quickly. It is researched at the Citadel of Adun and affects only Zealots.

Zealot speed is a common research in PvZ and in the late game of PvT, where Zealots are mass-produced alongside other units.
