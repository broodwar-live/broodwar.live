Consume lets a Defiler kill one of its owner's own nearby units (not a building or a Larva) to gain 50 energy. It is researched at the Defiler Mound. See the [Consume ability](/wiki/abilities/consume/) page.

Because Dark Swarm costs 100 energy and Plague 150, Consume — usually on cheap Zerglings — is what lets Defilers cast repeatedly, and it is researched in virtually every game that features Defilers.
