Ship Weapons adds damage per level to Terran attack ships: the Wraith gains +1 on its Burst Lasers (ground) and +2 on its Gemini Missiles (air), the Battlecruiser +3 on its laser batteries, and the Valkyrie +1 per Halo Rocket. Levels 2 and 3 need a completed Science Facility. Its cost rises by 50 minerals and gas per level, less than the other Terran weapon upgrades.

Ship Weapons is mostly seen in Wraith- or Battlecruiser-based games, such as late-game Battlecruiser transitions in TvP and TvT.
