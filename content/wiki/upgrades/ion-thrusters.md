Ion Thrusters raises the Vulture's top speed, making the already fast hover bike one of the quickest ground units in the game. It affects only Vultures.

Vulture speed is a near-universal research in Factory play — Vulture harassment of mineral lines, map control and Spider Mine placement all rely on it, especially in TvP.
