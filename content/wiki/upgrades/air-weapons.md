Air Weapons adds +1 damage per level to the Corsair, the Arbiter, each Carrier Interceptor, the Scout's ground attack and each of the Scout's two anti-air missiles. Because every Interceptor gains the bonus, the upgrade scales with the number of Interceptors in a Carrier fleet. Levels 2 and 3 need a Fleet Beacon.

It is researched mainly when Protoss commits to air units — Carriers in the late game, or Corsairs in PvZ.
