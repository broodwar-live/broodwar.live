Muscular Augments raises the Hydralisk's movement speed. It is researched at the Hydralisk Den and, unlike Lurker Aspect, does not require a Lair.

Together with Grooved Spines it is standard whenever Zerg relies on Hydralisks, most commonly in ZvP.
