Irradiate lets a Science Vessel spend 75 energy to cover a unit in radiation that damages it and nearby biological units over time; mechanical units and buildings are not harmed. It is especially effective against Zerg, whose units are all biological. See the [Irradiate ability](/wiki/abilities/irradiate/) page.

In TvZ, Irradiate is the Science Vessel's main weapon against Mutalisks, Lurkers and Defilers.
