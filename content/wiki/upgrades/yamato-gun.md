Yamato Gun unlocks the Battlecruiser's Yamato Cannon, which costs 150 energy and fires a single powerful shot at one target. See the [Yamato Cannon ability](/wiki/abilities/yamato-cannon/) page.

It is researched whenever Battlecruisers are built in numbers, and is used to snipe high-value targets such as Carriers, Photon Cannons or Missile Turrets.
