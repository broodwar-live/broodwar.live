Disruption Web costs 125 energy and creates a field on the ground in which no ground unit or building can attack; units inside can still move, and air units are unaffected. It is researched at the Fleet Beacon. See the [Disruption Web ability](/wiki/abilities/disruption-web/) page.

It is most associated with Corsair play in PvZ, where webs can neutralize Spore and Sunken Colonies or Hydralisks.
