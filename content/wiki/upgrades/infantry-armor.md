Each level of Infantry Armor adds 1 armor to all Terran infantry — Marines, Firebats, Ghosts, Medics — and to SCVs. Armor is subtracted from every hit, so it matters most against fast, low-damage attacks such as Zergling claws (5 damage per hit at base). Levels 2 and 3 need a completed Science Facility.

In bio-heavy Terran play, especially against Zerg, Infantry Armor is commonly researched alongside Infantry Weapons from a second Engineering Bay.
