Chitinous Plating adds 2 armor to the Ultralisk on top of its base armor of 1 and any Carapace levels, for a maximum of 6 armor with level 3 Carapace. Against a Marine without weapon upgrades (6 damage per shot), 6 armor reduces each shot to the minimum of 0.5 damage.

It is normally researched together with Anabolic Synthesis whenever Zerg commits to Ultralisks.
