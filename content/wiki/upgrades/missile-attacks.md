Missile Attacks adds +1 damage per level to the Hydralisk's Needle Spines and +2 to the Lurker's Subterranean Spines. Level 2 requires a Lair and level 3 a Hive. Its cost rises by 50 minerals and gas per level.

It is the usual attack upgrade for Hydralisk-based play against Protoss and for Lurker-heavy compositions.
