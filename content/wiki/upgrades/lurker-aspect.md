Lurker Aspect allows Hydralisks to morph into Lurkers (an additional 50 minerals and 100 gas per Lurker). It is researched at the Hydralisk Den and requires a completed Lair. Lurkers attack only while burrowed, hitting ground units in a line with their Subterranean Spines.

Lurkers are a core Zerg unit against Terran bio, and fast Lurker Aspect openings are common in ZvT.
