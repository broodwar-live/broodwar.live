Cloaking Field lets Wraiths turn invisible. Activating it costs 25 energy and the cloak then drains energy over time until the Wraith decloaks or runs out. Cloaked Wraiths can only be targeted with detection, such as Missile Turrets, Science Vessels, Comsat scans, Overlords, Spore Colonies, Observers or Photon Cannons. See the [Cloaking Field ability](/wiki/abilities/cloaking-field/) page.

It is used mainly in Wraith-based builds, most often in TvT.
