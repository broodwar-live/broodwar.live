Tank Siege Mode allows Siege Tanks to deploy into an immobile artillery mode. In siege mode the tank's Arclite Shock Cannon deals 70 explosive splash damage at a range of 12, compared with 30 damage at range 7 in tank mode, but it has a minimum range and cannot move until it unsieges. See the [Siege Mode ability](/wiki/abilities/siege-mode/) page.

Siege Mode is researched in nearly every game in which Terran builds tanks, and sieged tank lines are central to TvP and TvT.
