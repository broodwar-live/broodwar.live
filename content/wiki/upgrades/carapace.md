Each level of Carapace adds 1 armor to Zerg ground units: Drones, Zerglings, Hydralisks, Lurkers, Ultralisks, Defilers and Infested Terrans (plus Broodlings, Larvae and eggs). Level 2 requires a Lair and level 3 a Hive.

Armor is subtracted from every hit, so Carapace is valuable against Marines (6 damage per shot at base) and Zealots. Zerg players commonly run Carapace and an attack upgrade from two Evolution Chambers in the mid game. For Ultralisks it stacks with Chitinous Plating.
