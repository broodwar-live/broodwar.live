U-238 Shells adds 1 range (32 pixels) to the Marine's Gauss Rifle, raising it from 4 to 5. It affects only Marines — Ghosts and Firebats are unchanged.

Alongside Stim Pack, it is one of the two Academy researches at the heart of Marine/Medic play; the extra range lets Marines fire from behind a front line and engage Lurkers, Hydralisks and Dragoons on more even terms.
