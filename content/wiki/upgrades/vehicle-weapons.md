Vehicle Weapons adds damage per level to Factory units: +2 for the Vulture's Fragmentation Grenade, +3 for the Siege Tank in tank mode and +5 in siege mode, and for the Goliath +1 on its ground cannons and +2 on each of its two anti-air missiles. Spider Mines are not affected. Levels 2 and 3 need a completed Science Facility.

Vehicle Weapons is the core upgrade of Terran mech play, most notably in TvP, where Terran players commonly build one or two Armories to upgrade tanks and Vultures through the mid and late game.
