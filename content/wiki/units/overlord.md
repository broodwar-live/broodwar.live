The Overlord provides supply for the Zerg (8 per Overlord) and is also a flying detector. It has no attack. After the Lair is built, Ventral Sacs lets Overlords transport ground units, Antennae increases their sight range, and Pneumatized Carapace increases their speed.

## Role in All Matchups

Losing Overlords means losing supply, which can stall Zerg production, so Protoss Corsairs and Terran Marines, Wraiths and Valkyries often target them. Zerg players spread Overlords across the map for scouting and keep some over their bases for detection.

## Key Mechanics

- Provides 8 supply
- Detector
- 200 HP, 0 armor, large size, very slow without Pneumatized Carapace
- Ventral Sacs: transport capacity of 8 slots
