The Battlecruiser is the most expensive Terran unit. It requires a Starport with a Control Tower and a Science Facility with a Physics Lab. It has 500 HP and 3 base armor, attacks both ground and air, and can fire the Yamato Cannon once the Yamato Gun is researched at the Physics Lab.

## Versus Zerg

Battlecruisers are uncommon in TvZ; they are slow and expensive, and Scourge and Defiler spells deal with them well.

## Versus Terran

Battlecruisers occasionally appear in long TvT games, where the opponent must answer them with Goliaths, Wraiths or their own Battlecruisers.

## Versus Protoss

Battlecruisers are rare in TvP. When they do appear, Protoss usually answers with Dragoons, Corsairs, Scouts or Dark Archon spells.

## Key Mechanics

- 25 normal damage, 30-frame cooldown, range 6, hits both ground and air
- Yamato Cannon: 150 energy, deals 260 damage to a single target
- Large size, 500 HP, 3 base armor, very slow
