The High Templar requires a Templar Archives. It has no attack. Its main spell, Psionic Storm, deals heavy damage to every unit in an area over a few seconds. Two High Templar can merge into an Archon.

## Versus Terran

Psionic Storm is very effective against Marine and Medic groups and useful against Vultures and Goliaths. Terran's main answer is EMP Shockwave from Science Vessels, which drains Templar energy.

## Versus Protoss

High Templar appear in PvP mostly in the mid and late game; Archons merged from Templar are also used.

## Versus Zerg

In PvZ, High Templar are a key part of the Protoss army. Storm is effective against Hydralisks, Zerglings and Mutalisks, and Templar are often merged into Archons.

## Key Mechanics

- Psionic Storm (75 energy, researched at the Templar Archives): 112 total damage to units in the area; overlapping storms do not stack
- Hallucination (100 energy, researched at the Templar Archives): creates two illusory copies of a unit that deal no damage
- Khaydarin Amulet (Templar Archives) increases the Templar's starting energy
- 40 HP, 40 shields, 0 armor, small size, slow
