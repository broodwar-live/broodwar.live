The Shuttle is built at the Robotics Facility. It has no attack and carries up to 8 slots of ground units. Gravitic Drive, researched at the Robotics Support Bay, increases its speed.

## Role in All Matchups

Shuttles move Reavers around the map and drop them on worker lines, carry High Templar for Psionic Storm drops, and transport Zealots and Dragoons for flanking or base attacks. In PvP and PvT, careful Shuttle-Reaver micro is an important skill.

## Key Mechanics

- Capacity: 8 slots. Probes take 1; Zealots, High Templar and Dark Templar take 2; Dragoons, Reavers, Archons and Dark Archons take 4
- 80 HP, 60 shields, 1 base armor, large size
