Scourge are built in pairs from a single larva once a Spire is completed. They can only attack air units and do so by flying into the target and exploding for 110 damage, which destroys them.

## Versus Terran

Scourge are the main Zerg answer to Science Vessels, Wraiths, Dropships, Valkyries and Battlecruisers in ZvT.

## Versus Protoss

In ZvP Scourge are used against Corsairs, Shuttles, Observers, Scouts and Carriers, although Corsair splash can kill groups of them.

## Versus Zerg

Scourge play an important role in ZvZ Mutalisk battles, where trading a pair of Scourge for a Mutalisk can decide air control.

## Key Mechanics

- 110 normal damage on impact against air units only; the Scourge dies when it hits
- A pair costs 25/75 and 1 supply
- 25 HP, 0 armor, small size
