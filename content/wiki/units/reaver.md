The Reaver requires a Robotics Support Bay. It is extremely slow and is usually moved around in a Shuttle. It attacks by building and launching Scarabs (15 minerals each) that travel along the ground and explode for 100 damage with splash.

## Versus Terran

Shuttle and Reaver harassment against SCVs is a common tactic in PvT, and Reavers can support the Protoss army against Siege Tanks and Goliaths.

## Versus Protoss

Reavers are a major factor in PvP, both for harassing Probes and for winning fights against Dragoons.

## Versus Zerg

In PvZ, Reavers are dangerous against Hydralisks, Lurkers and Drones, often combined with Corsairs for air control.

## Key Mechanics

- Scarab: 100 normal splash damage (125 with Scarab Damage), range 8, ground only
- Holds 5 Scarabs, or 10 with Reaver Capacity
- 100 HP, 80 shields, 0 armor, large size, very slow
- Takes 4 slots in a Shuttle
