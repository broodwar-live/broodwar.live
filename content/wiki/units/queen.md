The Queen requires a Queen's Nest. It has no normal attack and relies on spells. A Queen can also infest a Terran Command Center that is below half HP, turning it into an Infested Command Center.

## Role in Competitive Play

Queens are uncommon in competitive play. Ensnare can slow and reveal enemy groups such as Mutalisks or Marines, and Spawn Broodling can kill a single ground unit such as a Siege Tank or High Templar, but Zerg players usually prefer other Hive-tech units.

## Key Mechanics

- Parasite (75 energy): gives the Zerg player permanent vision from the target unit
- Ensnare (75 energy, researched at the Queen's Nest): slows units in an area and reveals cloaked units for the duration
- Spawn Broodling (150 energy, researched at the Queen's Nest): instantly kills most non-robotic ground units and spawns two Broodlings in their place
- 120 HP, 0 armor, medium size
