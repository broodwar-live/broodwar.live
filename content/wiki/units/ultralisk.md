The Ultralisk requires an Ultralisk Cavern (Hive tech). It is a large melee unit with 400 HP. Chitinous Plating adds 2 armor and Anabolic Synthesis increases its speed, both researched at the Ultralisk Cavern.

## Versus Terran

In late-game ZvT, armored Ultralisks with Zerglings and Defilers are a common way to break Terran bio. Dark Swarm protects them from Marines and Siege Tanks, while Irradiate and massed fire remain threats.

## Versus Protoss

Ultralisks appear in late ZvP, especially alongside Defilers and Zerglings, but Dark Archon Mind Control and massed Dragoons can deal with them.

## Versus Zerg

Ultralisks are very rare in ZvZ, which rarely reaches Hive tech.

## Key Mechanics

- 20 normal damage, 15-frame cooldown, melee
- 400 HP, 1 base armor (+2 with Chitinous Plating), large size
- 4 supply; cannot burrow
