The Valkyrie was added in Brood War. It requires a Starport with a Control Tower and an Armory. It can only attack air units and fires a volley of Halo Rockets that deal explosive splash damage, which makes it effective against groups of clumped air units.

## Versus Zerg

Valkyries are the main Terran answer to large groups of Mutalisks in TvZ, especially when Mutalisks clump together. They are sometimes built early to defend against Mutalisk harassment.

## Versus Terran

Valkyries are uncommon in TvT, but can help against massed Wraiths or Dropships.

## Versus Protoss

Valkyries see little use in TvP, though they can damage Interceptors and groups of Corsairs.

## Key Mechanics

- Halo Rockets: explosive splash damage against air units only, 6 damage per rocket, range 6
- Cannot attack ground units
- Large size, 200 HP, 2 base armor, 3 supply
