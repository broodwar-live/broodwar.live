The Carrier requires a Fleet Beacon. It attacks with Interceptors, small fighters that launch from the Carrier and each deal 6 normal damage. A Carrier holds 4 Interceptors, or 8 with Carrier Capacity researched at the Fleet Beacon. Interceptors cost 25 minerals each.

## Versus Terran

Carriers are a common late-game PvT option, often supported by Arbiters. Terran responds with Goliaths with Charon Boosters, and sometimes with Ghosts or Science Vessels.

## Versus Protoss

Carriers are uncommon in PvP but can appear in long games.

## Versus Zerg

Carriers sometimes appear in PvZ, but Zerg can counter them with Scourge, Devourers and Hydralisks.

## Key Mechanics

- Attacks via Interceptors: 6 normal damage per shot, launch range 8, ground and air
- 4 Interceptors by default, 8 with Carrier Capacity
- 300 HP, 150 shields, 4 base armor, large size, 6 supply
