The Corsair was introduced in Brood War. It is a Stargate unit that only attacks air units. Its attack has a very short cooldown and deals splash damage, which makes it effective against groups of light flyers. Disruption Web, researched at the Fleet Beacon, prevents ground units and buildings under it from attacking.

## Versus Zerg

Corsairs are a standard part of PvZ. They kill Overlords, protect against Mutalisks and give Protoss air control, often combined with Reavers, High Templar or Dark Templar.

## Versus Terran

Corsairs are rarely used in PvT, where Terran usually builds few air units.

## Versus Protoss

Corsairs are uncommon in PvP but can be used against Shuttles and Observers.

## Key Mechanics

- 5 explosive splash damage, 8-frame cooldown, range 5, air targets only
- Disruption Web (125 energy): ground units and buildings under the web cannot attack
- 100 HP, 80 shields, 1 base armor, medium size
