The Zealot is the first Protoss combat unit, built at the Gateway for 100 minerals. Each attack strikes twice for 8 normal damage. Leg Enhancements, researched at the Citadel of Adun, greatly increases its speed.

## Versus Terran

Zealots work alongside Dragoons in PvT, absorbing Siege Tank fire, closing the gap on sieged tanks and running into Terran bases with Shuttles or Recall. Vultures and Spider Mines are their main problem.

## Versus Protoss

Early PvP often revolves around Zealot and Dragoon fights, and Zealots are used to protect against early aggression.

## Versus Zerg

Zealots are the core of early PvZ armies, especially after a forge-first fast expansion. Later they fight alongside Archons, High Templar, Corsairs and Reavers. Lurkers and Ultralisks are dangerous for them.

## Key Mechanics

- 2 × 8 normal damage per attack, 22-frame cooldown, melee
- 100 HP, 60 shields, 1 base armor, small size
- Leg Enhancements (Citadel of Adun) increases movement speed
- Takes 2 slots in a Shuttle
