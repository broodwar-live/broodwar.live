The Scout is built at the Stargate. It deals 2 × 14 explosive damage to air targets and 8 normal damage to ground targets. Apial Sensors (sight) and Gravitic Thrusters (speed) are researched at the Fleet Beacon.

## Role in Competitive Play

Scouts are rarely used in competitive play because they are expensive for their combat value. They sometimes appear against Zerg air units or Terran Battlecruisers.

## Key Mechanics

- Air: 2 × 14 explosive damage, 22-frame cooldown, range 4
- Ground: 8 normal damage, 30-frame cooldown, range 4
- 150 HP, 100 shields, 0 armor, large size, 3 supply
