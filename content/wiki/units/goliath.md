The Goliath is built at the Factory once an Armory is completed. It has two weapons: twin autocannons against ground targets and Hellfire missiles against air targets. The Charon Boosters research at the Machine Shop increases its anti-air range from 5 to 8.

## Versus Zerg

Goliaths provide anti-air for mech-based TvZ armies, defending against Mutalisks and Guardians.

## Versus Terran

In TvT, Goliaths protect tank lines against Wraiths, Dropships and Battlecruisers.

## Versus Protoss

Goliaths are the main Terran answer to Carriers in TvP. With Charon Boosters they can shoot Carriers and Arbiters from a safe distance behind Siege Tanks.

## Key Mechanics

- Ground: 12 normal damage, 22-frame cooldown, range 6
- Air: 2 × 10 explosive damage, 22-frame cooldown, range 5 (8 with Charon Boosters)
- Large size, 125 HP, 1 base armor
- Takes 2 slots in a Dropship
