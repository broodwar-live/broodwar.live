MBCGame HERO was the professional team sponsored by MBCGame, the game channel of the broadcaster MBC, which also produced StarCraft leagues of its own.

## Name history

- August 2002 — founded as Pirates of Space
- October 2004 — Egosys POS
- October 2005 — Pirates of Space again
- April 2006 — MBCGame HERO
- 1 February 2012 — disbanded

## Proleague

The team's best season was 2006: it reached the final of the SKY Proleague's first round, won the second round by beating CJ Entus 4–2, and then beat SK Telecom T1 4–3 in the Grand Final. Bisu and July, two of the players behind that run, later moved to SK Telecom T1, and the team was a mid-table side afterwards. Its later regulars included Sea, Light, Jaehoon, HyuN and the veteran Pusan, and Light led it to second place in the 2009–10 Winners League.
