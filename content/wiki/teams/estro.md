eSTRO was a Korean professional team managed by Daniel Lee, notable for bringing foreign progamers to Korea throughout its history.

## Name history

- January 2002 — AMD Dream Team
- February 2004 — Hexatron Dream Team
- March 2005 — eNature Top Team
- October 2006 — eSTRO

## Players

Notable former members include Grrrr..., ElkY, Legionnaire, GoodFriend, UpMagic, kOs and V-Gundam. The American Protoss NonY also played for eSTRO in Korea. The team also ran a Counter-Strike squad, which later moved to WeMade FOX.

## Dissolution

In August 2010 the team's dormitory and practice house were shut down. KeSPA took over stewardship and looked for a buyer; when none was found, the team was dissolved and its players were distributed to the remaining Proleague teams through an auction-style draft in October 2010 (for example Really and Flying to Woongjin Stars, Action to KT Rolster and Larva to SK Telecom T1).
