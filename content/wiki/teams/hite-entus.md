Hite Entus was not a separate organisation but the name used by [CJ Entus](/wiki/teams/cj-entus/) between October 2010 and May 2011. It was created when Hite SPARKYZ, gutted by the match-fixing scandal, merged into CJ Entus before the 2010–11 Proleague season; the Hite beer naming rights carried over to the merged team. When that contract expired on 8 May 2011, the team reverted to the CJ Entus name. Leta, previously Hite SPARKYZ's captain, captained the merged team.

## Hite SPARKYZ background

The Hite side of the merger had its own long history:

- October 2000 — founded as KOR
- May 2006 — OnGameNet SPARKYZ (sponsored by the broadcaster OGN)
- April 2009 — Hite SPARKYZ after Hite bought the naming rights

As KOR the team won the **2004 SKY Proleague Round 3**, and as OGN SPARKYZ it finished second in the 2008 Shinhan Bank Proleague with players such as Luxury, Leta and go.go. The match-fixing scandal of 2009–10 hit the team harder than any other: Justin was identified as a ringleader and several other former members were banned, leaving only a small core (Leta, Horang2, Hogil) before the merger.
