The Greater Spire is morphed from a [Spire](@/wiki/buildings/spire.md) and requires a [Hive](@/wiki/buildings/hive.md). It allows [Mutalisks](@/wiki/units/mutalisk.md) to morph into [Guardians](@/wiki/units/guardian.md) and [Devourers](@/wiki/units/devourer.md), and it continues to research flyer attack and carapace upgrades. While the Spire is morphing, those upgrades cannot be researched.

Guardians are used in late-game ZvP and ZvT to siege static positions from outside the range of Photon Cannons, Missile Turrets and Siege Tanks; Devourers support them against Corsairs, Valkyries and Wraiths.
