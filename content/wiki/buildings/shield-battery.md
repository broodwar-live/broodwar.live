The Shield Battery requires a Gateway. It uses its energy to recharge the shields of nearby friendly units; units can also be right-clicked on it to receive a recharge.

Shield Batteries show up mostly in early defensive situations — for example behind a wall against early Zerg aggression, or next to Dragoons holding against Terran pressure — and in some PvP builds. They are rarely seen in large numbers.
