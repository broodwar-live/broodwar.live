The Armory is Terran's vehicle and ship upgrade building, researching Vehicle Weapons, Vehicle Plating, Ship Weapons, and Ship Plating (each up to level 3). It is also required to unlock Goliath production at the Factory and Valkyrie production at the Starport. Levels 2 and 3 of its upgrades require a Science Facility.

In competitive play, the Armory is central to mech builds, above all in TvP and TvT. Vehicle Weapons upgrades are critical for Siege Tank and Vulture damage output, and double Armory builds allow simultaneous weapon and plating upgrades for faster 3/3 mech progression. Bio players in TvZ also build one, mainly to unlock Valkyries or Goliaths against Mutalisks.

The Armory does not lift off and has no special placement considerations. It is typically built safely inside the main base or behind defensive lines. Since it serves primarily as a research structure with no add-on, placement is flexible — the main concern is protecting it from harassment, as losing an Armory while researching an expensive upgrade can set back the Terran player significantly.
