The Nydus Canal requires a [Hive](@/wiki/buildings/hive.md). After it is built on creep, the player places its exit elsewhere on creep; ground units that enter one end emerge from the other.

Nydus Canals are rare in professional 1v1 play. They are mostly used on large maps to move defending units between distant bases, and occasionally for surprise attacks into a base where the Zerg has spread creep.
