The Arbiter Tribunal requires both a Templar Archives and a Stargate and is needed to build [Arbiters](@/wiki/units/arbiter.md). It researches [Recall](@/wiki/abilities/recall.md), [Stasis Field](@/wiki/abilities/stasis-field.md) and Khaydarin Core (+50 Arbiter energy).

The Arbiter Tribunal is a PvT building: Arbiters cloak the Protoss army against Terran mech, and Stasis Field and Recall are key late-game tools in that matchup.
