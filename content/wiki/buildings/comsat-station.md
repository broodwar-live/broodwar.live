The Comsat Station is an add-on for the Command Center and requires an Academy. Its only ability, Scanner Sweep, costs 50 energy and reveals a small area anywhere on the map for a few seconds, detecting cloaked and burrowed units inside it.

Scans are Terran's main mobile detection against Lurkers and Dark Templar, and they double as scouting: a scan of the opponent's main reveals tech and army composition. Because energy is shared by nothing else, Terran players build Comsats on every Command Center and keep energy banked for fights against cloaked units.
