The Hive is morphed from a [Lair](@/wiki/buildings/lair.md) and requires a [Queen's Nest](@/wiki/buildings/queens-nest.md). It unlocks the [Defiler Mound](@/wiki/buildings/defiler-mound.md), [Ultralisk Cavern](@/wiki/buildings/ultralisk-cavern.md), [Greater Spire](@/wiki/buildings/greater-spire.md) and [Nydus Canal](@/wiki/buildings/nydus-canal.md), as well as the [Adrenal Glands](@/wiki/upgrades/adrenal-glands.md) research for Zerglings and level 3 attack and carapace upgrades.

In ZvT the Hive transition — typically into Defilers with Dark Swarm and Plague, often alongside Ultralisks — is what lets Zerg fight a maxed Terran army. In ZvP, Hive tech brings Defilers and Guardians or Ultralisks against Protoss ground armies.
