The Assimilator is warped in on a Vespene Geyser and lets Probes harvest gas from it. Like every Protoss structure it has both hit points and shields that regenerate over time. It does not need Pylon power.

The timing of the first Assimilator is an important scouting clue: an early gas usually means a fast Cybernetics Core and Dragoons or tech, while a late or missing gas suggests Zealot pressure or a fast expansion. It is the Protoss equivalent of the Terran [Refinery](@/wiki/buildings/refinery.md) and the Zerg [Extractor](@/wiki/buildings/extractor.md).
