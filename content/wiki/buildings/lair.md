The Lair is morphed from a [Hatchery](@/wiki/buildings/hatchery.md) and requires a Spawning Pool. It keeps producing larvae while morphing and unlocks the [Spire](@/wiki/buildings/spire.md), [Queen's Nest](@/wiki/buildings/queens-nest.md) and the [Lurker Aspect](@/wiki/upgrades/lurker-aspect.md) research at the Hydralisk Den. Overlord upgrades (Ventral Sacs, Antennae and Pneumatized Carapace) are researched at a Lair or Hive.

The Lair timing is one of the clearest signals in a Zerg build. In ZvT and ZvP, an early Lair usually leads into Mutalisks or Lurkers; in ZvZ, Lair timing often decides who gets Mutalisks first.
