The Refinery is built on a Vespene Geyser and lets SCVs harvest gas from it. Three workers per geyser is the usual saturation in Brood War. Every Terran tech path past the Barracks needs gas, so the timing of the first Refinery is one of the main ways a build order signals its intent — an early gas points to a fast Factory or Academy, while a late gas usually means an economic or Barracks-heavy opening.

The Refinery has no prerequisites. It is the Terran equivalent of the Protoss [Assimilator](@/wiki/buildings/assimilator.md) and the Zerg [Extractor](@/wiki/buildings/extractor.md).
