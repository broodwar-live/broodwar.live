The Robotics Support Bay requires a Robotics Facility and is needed to build [Reavers](@/wiki/units/reaver.md). It researches Scarab Damage, Reaver Capacity and Gravitic Drive (Shuttle speed).

Gravitic Drive matters more than its modest description suggests: fast Shuttles carrying Reavers are a staple harassment tool in PvZ and PvP, and the speed upgrade is what makes picking the Reaver back up after a shot reliable.
