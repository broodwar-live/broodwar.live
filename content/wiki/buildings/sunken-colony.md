The Sunken Colony is morphed from a [Creep Colony](@/wiki/buildings/creep-colony.md) and requires a Spawning Pool. It attacks ground units only, with 40 explosive damage per hit at a range of 7. Explosive damage deals full damage to large units but only half to medium and a quarter to small units such as Marines and Zerglings, so Sunkens are relatively weak against massed small units and strong against Dragoons and vehicles.

Sunkens are the backbone of Zerg early defense: against Zergling rushes in ZvZ, against early Zealot or Marine pressure, and to protect expansions while the Zerg drones up. Because they cannot move and cost a Drone plus 125 minerals in total, building too many of them slows the Zerg economy.
