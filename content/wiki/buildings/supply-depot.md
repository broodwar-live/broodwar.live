The Supply Depot raises the Terran supply cap by 8. Unlike Pylons or Overlords it has no other function, so its value lies in where it is placed: Terran players routinely use Supply Depots together with a Barracks to wall off the main ramp in TvZ, or to block the entrance against early Zealots in TvP.

Losing several depots at once can supply-block a Terran in the middle of a fight, so experienced players spread them around the base rather than clustering them where a drop or a Reaver can destroy them together. Like other Terran buildings, a damaged depot can be repaired by SCVs and burns down when its HP falls into the red.
