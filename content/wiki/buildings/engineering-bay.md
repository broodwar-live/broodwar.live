The Engineering Bay is Terran's infantry upgrade building, researching Infantry Weapons and Infantry Armor upgrades (up to level 3 each; levels 2 and 3 require a Science Facility). It is also the prerequisite for constructing Missile Turrets, providing Terran with static anti-air defense and detection capability.

In competitive play, the Engineering Bay is built early in TvZ bio builds to begin +1 Infantry Weapons research, which significantly increases Marine damage output. The +1 attack timing push is a well-known strategic milestone. In TvP, where mech is the standard style, the Engineering Bay matters mostly for Missile Turrets. Double Engineering Bay builds allow simultaneous weapon and armor upgrades for faster progression to 3/3.

The Engineering Bay can lift off and relocate, though this is rarely relevant. Like the Barracks and Supply Depots, it is a large building that can be used to block a ramp or choke. Placement in the wall should ensure no gaps while leaving room for units to pass through when needed. The Engineering Bay can also be placed at expansion bases where Missile Turrets are needed for detection against burrowed or cloaked units.
