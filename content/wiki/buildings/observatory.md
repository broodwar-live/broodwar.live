The Observatory requires a Robotics Facility and is needed to build [Observers](@/wiki/units/observer.md), the permanently cloaked detector that Protoss relies on for scouting and for fighting Lurkers, Spider Mines, Dark Templar and Wraiths. It researches Gravitic Boosters (Observer speed) and Sensor Array (Observer sight).

Because Observers are Protoss's only mobile detection, an Observatory is built in nearly every PvT and PvZ game; against Lurkers and Spider Mines, losing Observers can decide a fight.
