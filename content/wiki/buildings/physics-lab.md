The Physics Lab is one of the two Science Facility add-ons (the other is [Covert Ops](@/wiki/buildings/covert-ops.md)); a Science Facility can only hold one of them. It is required to build [Battlecruisers](@/wiki/units/battlecruiser.md) and researches Yamato Cannon and Colossus Reactor (+50 Battlecruiser energy).

Battlecruiser play is most common in late-game TvP, where Terran players sometimes transition into Battlecruisers against Protoss armies that lack enough Scouts, Carriers or Arbiters to answer them.
