The Fleet Beacon requires a Stargate and is needed to build [Carriers](@/wiki/units/carrier.md). It researches Carrier Capacity (8 Interceptors instead of 4), [Disruption Web](@/wiki/abilities/disruption-web.md) and Argus Jewel for Corsairs, and Apial Sensors and Gravitic Thrusters for Scouts.

In competitive play the Fleet Beacon appears mainly in late-game PvT Carrier transitions and in PvZ Corsair builds that research Disruption Web.
