Covert Ops is the second Science Facility add-on, alongside the [Physics Lab](@/wiki/buildings/physics-lab.md). It is required to train [Ghosts](@/wiki/units/ghost.md) and to build a [Nuclear Silo](@/wiki/buildings/nuclear-silo.md), and it researches [Lockdown](@/wiki/abilities/lockdown.md), Personnel Cloaking, Ocular Implants (+2 Ghost sight) and Moebius Reactor (+50 Ghost energy).

Ghosts see occasional use in TvP late games, where Lockdown can neutralize Carriers, Arbiters and Reavers, and in nuke-based strategies.
