The Defiler Mound is a late-game Zerg tech building that enables Defiler production from the Hatchery. It researches two critical abilities: Consume (sacrifice a friendly Zerg unit to restore 50 Defiler energy) and Plague (heavy damage over time to units and buildings in an area that cannot reduce them below 1 HP). Defilers also have the innate Dark Swarm ability, which creates a cloud that protects units underneath from most ranged attacks. The Defiler Mound requires a Hive.

In competitive play, the Defiler Mound unlocks what many consider the most strategically impactful late-game unit in Brood War. Defilers with Dark Swarm fundamentally change ZvT — casting Dark Swarm over advancing Zerglings and Ultralisks stops Marines and other direct ranged attacks from hitting them, forcing Terran to rely on other answers. Plague is devastating against clumped armies, reducing unit HP across an entire formation. Consume research is essential since Defilers need energy to cast, and sacrificing Zerglings (cheapest Zerg unit) is the standard method.

The Defiler Mound must be built on creep and requires a Hive, placing it firmly in the late-game tech tree. It is typically built at the main base. Getting the Defiler Mound and completing Consume research as quickly as possible after Hive is a key timing for Zerg's transition to late-game army compositions. In ZvT, the arrival of Defilers with Dark Swarm often marks the turning point where Zerg's ground army becomes nearly unstoppable against bio-mech compositions. Protecting the Defiler Mound is critical during the Hive transition period.
