## Overview

Terrain shapes combat in StarCraft: Brood War: elevation affects vision and accuracy, narrow passages limit how many units can fight, and water or cliffs decide where ground armies can go.

## High ground

Units on low ground cannot see onto higher ground, so they need a spotter (an air unit, a unit already up the ramp, or a Comsat scan) before they can target anything up there. Even with vision, a ranged ground attack against a ground unit on higher ground hits only 136 times in 256 — about a **47% miss chance**. The same penalty applies to targets standing under certain doodads such as trees. Melee attacks never miss, attacks involving air units are unaffected, and a unit on high ground is briefly revealed when it fires.

## Choke points

A **choke** is a narrow passage such as a ramp. It limits how many units can engage at once, which favours the defender: two Zealots on a ramp face only a few Zerglings at a time, and Lurkers or sieged tanks covering a choke are very hard to break. In ranged fights the side with the wide end of the choke can bring more units to bear.

## Walls, cliffs and water

- Terran and Protoss often wall a ramp or natural choke with buildings (see [Building Placement](@/wiki/mechanics/building-placement.md)).
- Ranged units on cliffs can shoot ground units that cannot path up to them.
- Water, space and cliffs block ground movement. Expansions reachable only by air are called island expansions; mineral-only expansions have no geyser.
