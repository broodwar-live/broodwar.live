Feedback drains all of a target unit's energy and deals damage equal to the amount drained, ignoring armor (shields are hit first). A Science Vessel at its base maximum of 200 energy has 200 hit points, so a full-energy Vessel dies outright. It cannot target buildings, units without energy, hallucinations or units in Stasis Field. A Defensive Matrix absorbs the damage.

It costs 50 energy, has a range of 10 and needs no research — every Dark Archon can use it.

Feedback is an occasional tool for sniping spellcasters — Science Vessels, Defilers, High Templar — when the opponent's caster count gets high. Dark Archons are expensive (two Dark Templar each), which keeps the spell rare in professional play.
