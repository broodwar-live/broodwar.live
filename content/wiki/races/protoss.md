The Protoss are a psionic alien race. In StarCraft: Brood War they field expensive, durable units whose **plasma shields** absorb damage before hit points and regenerate over time on every Protoss unit and building. Shields take full damage from every weapon type, so the size multipliers of concussive and explosive attacks only apply once they are gone.

## Building and economy

A Probe only starts a warp-in; the building then completes on its own while the Probe goes back to mining. Every building except the Nexus, Assimilator and Pylon must be placed inside a Pylon's psionic matrix, and buildings whose Pylons are destroyed stop producing and researching until they are powered again. Pylons provide 8 supply and the Nexus 9.

## Key units

- **Zealot** — melee unit with 100 HP and 60 shields, two 8-damage hits per attack.
- **Dragoon** — ranged unit with 100 HP and 80 shields; its range rises from 4 to 6 with Singularity Charge.
- **High Templar** — casts Psionic Storm (112 damage over 8 hits) and Hallucination; two merge into an Archon.
- **Reaver** — fires Scarabs for 100 splash damage (125 with the upgrade), usually carried in a Shuttle.
- **Corsair** — air-to-air splash and Disruption Web.
- **Arbiter** — cloaks nearby friendly units and casts Recall and Stasis Field.
- **Observer** — a permanently cloaked detector.

## In competitive play

Against Zerg, Protoss commonly takes a fast expansion behind a Forge, Gateway and Pylon wall and uses Corsairs to control the air. Against Terran, Dragoons and Zealots, Reaver or Storm drops and late-game Arbiters are typical. PvP revolves around Dragoon control and Reaver play.
