## Overview

The 1.22 series (July 2018 – May 2019) added the seasonal ranked ladder. Unit balance was not changed.

## Highlights

- **1.22.0**: ranked ladder Season 1 with leagues S, A, B, C, D, E and F after five placement matches; map pool of Blue Storm, Heartbreak Ridge, Polaris Rhapsody, Aztec, Roadkill, Circuit Breakers and Fighting Spirit; 18 new ramp tiles for map makers
- **1.22.1** / **1.22.3**: seasonal map-pool updates and race-themed rank rewards
- **1.22.2**: penalties for dodging ladder games
- **1.22.4**: purchasable Korean commentator announcer packs; both players see each other's MMR before a ranked game
