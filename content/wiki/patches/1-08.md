## Overview

Patch 1.08 is the last patch that changed unit balance. Every competitive Brood War game since 2001 — including the modern ASL era on Remastered — is played on the balance it established. It also introduced replay recording.

## New Features

- Replay recording ("Save Replay" on the score screen)
- "Top vs Bottom" game type
- Battle.net gateway selection

## Terran

- Battlecruiser: supply cost lowered to 6, build time reduced
- Valkyrie: 6 damage per missile, slightly faster speed and acceleration, build time reduced
- Goliath: ground attack range increased
- Dropship: speed increased
- Missile Turret: cost lowered to 75 minerals
- Science Facility: build time reduced; Irradiate research raised to 200/200; Yamato Cannon research lowered to 100/100
- Charon Boosters research lowered to 100/100

## Protoss

- Zealot: hit points raised to 100, shields lowered to 60
- Dragoon: build time increased
- Scout: cost lowered to 275/125
- Carrier: supply cost lowered to 6
- Psionic Storm: damage reduced
- Disruption Web: duration reduced

## Zerg

- Spawning Pool: cost raised to 200 minerals
- Sunken Colony: armor raised to 2, hit points lowered to 300
- Lurker Aspect research raised to 200/200; Hydralisk speed research raised to 150/150
- Queen: cost lowered to 100/100; Spawn Broodling research lowered to 100/100
- Ultralisk: supply cost lowered to 4

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed the "gliding SCV" and "teleporting Drone" bugs
- Landing a building on units can now crush Siege Tanks (a side effect of fixing tanks placed under Barracks)
