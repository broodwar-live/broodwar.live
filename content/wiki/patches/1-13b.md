## Patch 1.13b (2005-08-12)

- Fixed a crash caused by certain maps

## Later 1.13 hotfixes

- **1.13c** (2005-08-22): fixed a Zerg building bug and minimap unit ordering in replays
- **1.13d** (2005-09-06): fixed a bug that prevented training Infested Terrans
- **1.13e** (2005-09-12) and **1.13f** (2006-01-18): fixed bugs that enabled exploits

No balance changes.
