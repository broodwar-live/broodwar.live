## Overview

Version 1.00 is the launch build of the original StarCraft, released in North America at the end of March 1998. It predates Brood War: the expansion units (Medic, Valkyrie, Dark Templar as a buildable unit, Corsair, Dark Archon, Lurker, Devourer) did not exist yet, and many unit costs and stats differed from the values competitive players know today.

The first post-release fix, Patch 1.01, followed in April 1998. See the later patch pages for the balance changes that shaped modern Brood War, especially [1.04](@/wiki/patches/1-04.md) and [1.08](@/wiki/patches/1-08.md).
