## Changes

- "Enable CPU Throttling" option in the speed menu (off by default)
- Fixed the reply shortcut triggering on any word ending in "r"
- Removed a delay in palette-cycling updates

No balance changes. 1.16.1 stayed the current version for more than eight years, until [Patch 1.18](@/wiki/patches/1-18.md) in April 2017.
