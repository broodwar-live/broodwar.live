## Terran

- Academy cost lowered to 150 minerals
- Science Facility cost lowered to 100/150
- Spider Mines research cost lowered to 100/100

## General

- Shorter delay between units unloading from a transport
- Multiplayer games can only be saved once every two minutes

## Bug Fixes

- Spider Mines are now affected by Disruption Web
- Hallucinated Devourers no longer apply Acid Spores
- Neutral Medics no longer heal automatically
- "Morph to Lurker" can only be issued to Hydralisks
- Landed Terran buildings can no longer move
