## Bug Fixes

- Fixed Command Centers sliding after a cancelled Comsat Station
- Fixed Drones "flying" when surrounded, and a Drone confused by a second build order on the way
- Fixed High Templar flying across the map when an Archon merge is interrupted
- Fixed sound on Windows NT systems

No balance changes.
