## Overview

Patch 1.04 is the patch that made the game compatible with the Brood War expansion. It is also one of the two largest balance patches in the game's history (the other is [1.08](@/wiki/patches/1-08.md)); many of the numbers it set are still in use today.

## General

- Brood War compatibility added
- Ctrl-click / double-click now also selects all burrowed or all cloaked units of the same type
- Several selected Carriers or Reavers now build Interceptors or Scarabs at the same time
- Fixed the "invincible Drone" bug, among other fixes

## Terran

- Wraith: cost 150/100, air-to-air damage 20, faster ground-attack cooldown
- Science Vessel: cost 100/225, more Irradiate damage, larger sight range, faster acceleration
- Battlecruiser: base armor 3, Yamato Cannon damage 260
- Goliath: ground damage 12, stronger air weapon upgrade scaling
- Dropship slightly faster; Nuclear Missile builds faster; Comsat scan costs 50 energy
- Starport cost 150/100 and builds faster; Control Tower cost 50/50

## Protoss

- Dragoon: cost 125/50, shorter build time, Singularity Charge gives +1 range
- Carrier: cost 350/250, 300 HP, base armor 4; Interceptors have 40 HP / 40 shields, deal 6 damage and cost 25 minerals
- Scout: air damage 28, base armor 0, 150 HP / 100 shields, faster ground-attack cooldown
- Arbiter cost 100/350; Hallucination costs 100 energy; Archon accelerates faster
- Shuttle, Reaver and Robotics Facility take longer to build; Photon Cannon and Stargate build faster
- Building cost changes: Forge 150, Citadel of Adun 150/100, Templar Archives 150/200, Stargate 150/150, Robotics Support Bay 150/100, Observatory 50/100
- Shield Battery starts with 100 energy and recharges from a slightly greater distance

## Zerg

- Hatchery: cost 300 minerals, longer build time, slower larva spawning
- Sunken Colony: morph costs 50, builds faster, attacks faster, damage 40
- Spore Colony builds faster and now deals normal damage
- Queen: Parasite costs 75 energy with range 12; Spawn Broodling range +1
- Defiler cost 50/150; Scourge HP 25; Hydralisk build time increased; Greater Spire takes longer
- Overlord speed upgrade gives a bigger bonus; Ventral Sacs researches faster
