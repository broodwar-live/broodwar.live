## General

- Your own units are always drawn on top on the minimap
- Default Battle.net font for Korean Windows 9x changed to Gulim 9
- Fixed several exploit-related bugs and Korean/German chat issues

No balance changes. Follow-up hotfixes 1.13b–1.13f (August 2005 – January 2006) fixed further crashes and exploits; see [1.13b](@/wiki/patches/1-13b.md).
