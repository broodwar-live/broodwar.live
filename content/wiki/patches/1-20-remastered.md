## Overview

Patch 1.20.0 launched StarCraft: Remastered. The HD graphics and audio are a paid upgrade; the underlying game and unit balance are the same as in the classic client, so competitive Brood War moved to Remastered without any balance discussion.

## 1.20.0 (2017-08-14)

- HD graphics and remastered audio (Remastered license)
- Ranked matchmaking with race-based skill ratings
- Observer improvements
- A Battle.net account is required for multiplayer

## Notable hotfixes (1.20.1 – 1.20.11, August – November 2017)

- 1.20.1: burrow holes made more visible on dark tilesets
- 1.20.3 / 1.20.4: hotkey fixes and additions (Ghost, Infested Terran, siege mode, lifted buildings, Defiler and Queen attack)
- 1.20.5: observer game clock
- 1.20.7: revamped replay interface with a production tab
- 1.20.8: dynamic turn-rate reduction in matchmaking when latency is high
