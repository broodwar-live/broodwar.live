## Overview

Patch 1.12 did not touch unit balance, but it is one of the most important quality-of-life patches in Brood War history.

## New Features

- Ladder games are played on "Fastest" speed instead of "Fast"
- Shift + number adds the selected units to an existing control group
- Right-clicking with a production building selected sets its rally point
- Minimap ping (Alt + left-click) and alliance colors (Shift + Tab)
- Friends list, friend mail, password recovery and full Korean language support on Battle.net
- Map preview in the game lobby

## Maps

- Lost Temple updated; Dire Straits and Rivalry adjusted

No balance changes.
