## Overview

The 1.23 series (July 2019 – September 2022) is the most recent patch line. It contains no unit balance changes; most updates are ladder map pools, rewards and technical improvements.

## Highlights

- **1.23.0** (2019-07-09): StarCraft: Cartooned skin; optional 64-bit client; keyboard commands work while a mouse button is held; StarEdit removed in favor of SCMDraft 2
- **1.23.1** (2019-09-24): color selection in unranked games; worker counts in the replay interface
- **1.23.5** (2020-07-01): DirectX 11/12 and Metal graphics back-ends; fixed a nuke exploit used by cheaters
- **1.23.8.9713** (2021-07-27): hotfix for a "flying SCV" bug
- **1.23.10** (2022-09-07): Season 11 ladder map pool (Butter, Eclipse, Neo Sylphid, Nemesis, Vermeer SE, Fighting Spirit, Polypoid)
