## Overview

Released after a public test period that began in late March 2017, Patch 1.18 was the first update since 1.16.1. With it, StarCraft and Brood War became free to download and play. Unit balance was not changed.

## Features (1.18.0)

- Windowed and windowed-fullscreen modes
- Built-in observer mode and automatic replay saving
- APM display and in-game timer options
- Higher Battle.net turn rate, matching LAN responsiveness
- UPnP support, UTF-8 text, cursor confinement in windowed mode
- Support for Windows 7, 8.1 and 10, improved anti-cheat

## Follow-up hotfixes (1.18.1 – 1.18.8, April – June 2017)

- Lobby latency options and latency bars
- Mouse-sensitivity settings
- Fixes to control groups and command handling at very high APM
