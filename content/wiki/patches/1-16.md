## Features

- In-game chat (not whispers) is saved in replays
- `/reply` (`/r`) whispers the last person who whispered you
- The game uses only as much CPU as it needs

## Exploits Fixed

- Several Zerg exploits: gaining minerals through mutations, recycling a building while it upgrades, extending creep with a Drone, Drones crossing impassable terrain
- A Terran exploit for dropping a nuke anywhere on the map
- Pausing the game in the pre-game lobby

No balance changes. Together with [1.16.1](@/wiki/patches/1-16-1.md), this is the "classic" client that the scene played on until the 1.18 update in 2017.
