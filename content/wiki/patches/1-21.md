## Overview

The 1.21 series (December 2017 – April 2018) focused on networking and custom maps. Unit balance was not changed.

## Highlights

- **1.21.0**: EUD emulator for custom maps; higher sprite/bullet limits (optional); new turn-rate algorithm; ladder pool of Circuit Breakers, Fighting Spirit, Match Point, Longinus II, Neo Jade, Tau Cross and Destination
- **1.21.2**: STUN NAT support so most players no longer need to forward port 6112; turn rate and FPS overlays (Ctrl+Alt+T / Ctrl+Alt+F)
- **1.21.3**: dynamic turn rate for custom games; matchmaking favors low-latency pairings
- **1.21.4**: turn rates 20 and 24 added
- **1.21.5**: Transistor and Gladiator replace Neo Jade and Match Point in the ladder pool; red vs blue becomes the default 1v1 colors in Top vs Bottom
