## Features

- Chat messages show the speaker's name in their team color
- Mouse-wheel scrolling in chat and list boxes
- Screenshots use date/time stamps and are no longer capped at 100
- Small corrections to Lost Temple and Dire Straits

## Exploits Fixed

- Cancelling a Nydus Canal could create a mobile exit
- Infesting a Command Center without a Queen or from a distance
- Workers mining from a distance; floating Drones over obstacles
- Terran buildings lifting off while training; SCVs repairing Protoss buildings
- Morphing Terran and Protoss buildings; stacking buildings; making Zerg buildings cloaked
- Two mineral-gain exploits

No balance changes.
