The PvZ mid game is when Protoss turns its fast expansion into map control — usually with Corsairs — and works toward Psionic Storm, while Zerg builds up Hydralisks and heads for Hive.

## Corsairs

Corsairs kill Overlords (costing Zerg supply and vision) and fight Mutalisks; their attack splashes against air units, so clumped Mutalisks take extra damage. Their **Disruption Web** prevents all units and buildings under it from attacking for a short time, which is mainly used in Corsair/Reaver play. Liquipedia's Bisu Build notes give rules of thumb: about four Corsairs with Cannon support defend bases well against Mutalisks, and Scourge should be dodged rather than traded into.

## The Bisu Build

Named after Bisu, who used it in the GOMTV MSL Season 1 final to beat reigning champion sAviOr 3–0, the Bisu Build is a follow-up to a Forge FE:

1. Stargate and a first Corsair
2. Citadel of Adun at 100 gas, a second Corsair and a second Gateway
3. Templar Archives when the Citadel finishes
4. Two Dark Templar, then more Gateways

Corsairs gather information and pick off Overlords; the first Dark Templar deny a Zerg bust or contain and threaten undetected bases. Liquipedia notes that Zerg later countered it with the **3 Base Spire into 5 Hatch Hydra** plan (early Spire for Scourge), and that today it is mostly used as a reactive follow-up.

## Corsair/Reaver

A variant uses Corsairs for air control and Shuttle-Reavers for harassment, with Disruption Web to protect Reavers from Hydralisks. It needs very high APM and precise control.

## Psionic Storm

Storm deals 112 damage over a short time to units in a 3×3-matrix area, ignoring armour, which makes it very effective against Hydralisks and Zerglings. It does not stack with itself and also damages friendly units. Shuttles carrying High Templar can drop Storms on Drone lines, but need Corsair cover against Scourge.

## Common Mistakes

- Trading Corsairs into Scourge instead of keeping them for air control.
- Getting Storm too late to hold a Hydralisk push.
- Storming your own Zealots in close fights.
