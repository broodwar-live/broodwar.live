## Overview

In PvZ, Protoss almost always fast-expands — usually with a Forge-first wall — and then uses Corsairs, Dark Templar, Reavers and Psionic Storm to control the game, while Zerg uses its faster economy to build a large Hydralisk-based army and reach Hive tech.

Phase guides: [early game](/wiki/strategies/pvz-early/) · [mid game](/wiki/strategies/pvz-mid/) · [late game](/wiki/strategies/pvz-late/)

## Common Openings

### Protoss
- **Forge FE** — Pylon at the natural, Forge, Photon Cannon(s), Nexus and Gateway; the most common PvZ opening. The exact order depends on what the scouting Probe sees (9 Pool, 12 Pool, 12 Hatch or Overpool).
- **Bisu Build** — a Forge FE follow-up with Corsairs and Dark Templar (see the mid-game guide).
- **One-base Corsair/Dark Templar** — popular in the early days of competitive BW, now rare and risky.
- **Corsair/Reaver** — an air-control strategy revolutionised by Nal_rA, now mostly a mid-game option.

### Zerg
- **3 Base Spire into 5 Hatch Hydra** — fast Lair and Spire with Scourge to stop heavy Corsair play, then Hydralisks from five Hatcheries. Liquipedia calls it the standard modern ZvP plan and the answer that countered the Bisu Build.
- **2 Hatch Lurker** — a Lair-first plan aimed at a Lurker attack against a fast-expanding Protoss.

## Mid Game

Protoss uses Corsairs to kill Overlords and contest Mutalisks, Dark Templar or Reavers to harass, and High Templar with Psionic Storm against Hydralisks. Zerg answers with Scourge against Corsairs and Shuttles, Hydralisks and Lurkers on the ground, and pressure across multiple bases.

## Late Game

- **Psionic Storm, Archons and Zealots** — Storm damages units under Dark Swarm, and Zealots and Archons (melee and splash) also still work there.
- **Defilers** — Dark Swarm shuts down Dragoons and Photon Cannons; Plague weakens Protoss units' hit points (not shields).
- **Expansion control** — the long game is usually decided by bases.

## Associated Players

- **Bisu** — the Bisu Build is named after the Corsair/Dark Templar follow-up he used in the GOMTV MSL Season 1 final, which he won 3–0 against reigning champion sAviOr.
- **Nal_rA** — credited by Liquipedia with revolutionising Corsair/Reaver.
