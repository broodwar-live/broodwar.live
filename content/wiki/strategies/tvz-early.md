The early game of TvZ is about getting a second Command Center running without losing SCVs or the expansion to Zergling pressure, while learning what Zerg is doing.

## Opening Builds

**1 Rax FE** is the standard. A typical order is Supply Depot at 9, Barracks at 11 (sending an SCV to scout), and Command Center between 15 and 18 supply. Liquipedia's notes for adapting it:

- Against a Hatchery-first Zerg, the Command Center can go down at 15 and Marine production can wait.
- Against a 9 Pool, pull a few SCVs to the ramp before the Zerglings arrive and build the Command Center in the main, floating it to the natural later.
- If 4–6 Zerglings leave the Zerg base and you have expanded, add a Bunker at the natural.
- If Zerg takes gas but does not start a Lair soon after, expect a Zergling-speed all-in: build a Bunker, block with a Depot and keep SCVs ready to repair.

**2 Rax Academy** adds a second Barracks and an Academy for Stim Pack. Modern versions use the threat of a Marine/Medic push to force Sunken Colonies, then expand. It is strong against two-Hatchery Zerg builds but weaker than 1 Rax FE against 3 Hatch Muta.

**8 Rax / Bunker Rush** builds the Barracks at 8 supply, scouts, rallies Marines to the Zerg natural and builds a Bunker next to the Hatchery. The aim is to kill Drones or the expansion; if it fails, Terran is behind economically.

## Scouting

The scouting SCV usually leaves with the Barracks. Key information: Hatchery-first or Pool-first, the number of Zerglings and whether they leave the base, and whether Zerg follows gas with a Lair (Mutalisk or Lurker tech) or with Zergling speed (aggression).

## Defending Early Aggression

Against 4/5 Pool, the Zerglings can arrive while the Barracks is still building. SCVs should form a wall between the Zerglings and the first Marine, and the Marine should retreat through the SCVs rather than fight alone. A Bunker filled with Marines — repaired by SCVs — is the standard way to hold an early Zergling attack at the natural.

## Common Mistakes

- Not scouting, and so not knowing whether to pull SCVs, add a Bunker or expand freely.
- Over-committing to a bunker rush that has already been spotted.
- Building more Marines than needed against a Hatchery-first Zerg, which lets the Zerg economy pull ahead.
