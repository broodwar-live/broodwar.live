In the TvP mid game Terran's mech army comes together and pushes for map control, while Protoss uses harassment and flanks to break Terran positions.

## Mech Play

Vultures scout, raid Probes and lay Spider Mines; mines deal 125 damage with splash and trigger on cloaked units even without detection, so they deny ground paths and expansions until Protoss brings Observers. Siege Tanks provide the army's damage from Siege Mode, and Goliaths are added against Shuttles, Carriers and Arbiters. The usual plan is to advance in stages, sieging at strong positions so Protoss must attack into the tanks.

## Psionic Storm Drops

A Shuttle carrying High Templar can drop Psionic Storms onto sieged tanks. Storm deals 112 damage over a short time to units in a 3×3-matrix area, regardless of armour, but does not stack with itself. Spreading tanks so that one Storm cannot hit many of them, and shooting Shuttles down with Goliaths and Turrets before they unload, are the main defences.

## Reaver Drops

A Reaver's Scarab deals 100 damage (125 with the Scarab Damage upgrade) with splash, enough to kill many SCVs at once. Turrets in mineral lines give vision and damage against Shuttles, and pulling SCVs away from an unloaded Reaver limits the damage.

## Expansion Timing

Terran's third base is usually taken behind a forward tank position, with Spider Mines covering the approaches. Taking it too late lets Protoss out-expand; taking it too early can stretch the army between defending the base and holding the front.

## Common Mistakes

- Clumping tanks where one Storm can hit several of them.
- Leaving mineral lines without Turrets against Reaver drops.
- Pushing forward without Goliath support against Shuttle play.
