Late-game PvZ starts when Zerg has Hive tech and Defilers. Protoss must keep a working mix of Storm, melee units and splash damage because Dark Swarm shuts down most of its ranged attacks.

## Dealing with Defilers

**Dark Swarm** creates a 6×6-matrix cloud under which ground units take no damage from ordinary ranged attacks — Dragoons and Photon Cannons are the units most affected. Melee attacks, splash and special weapons still work, so Zealots, Archons, Reaver Scarabs and **Psionic Storm** all remain effective under the swarm.

**Plague** deals roughly 300 damage over time to every unit in its area but cannot kill on its own. It ignores shields and damages hit points directly, which is why Protoss endures it better than Terran.

Defilers recover energy quickly with Consume, so killing or chasing Defilers off is as important as dodging their spells.

## High Templar

Each High Templar needs 75 energy per Storm, so a fully charged Templar (200 energy, 250 with the Khaydarin Amulet upgrade) has two or three Storms. Spent Templar can be merged into Archons. A steady supply of new Templar keeps Storm available through long fights.

## Army Composition

The usual late-game Protoss army is speed Zealots, Archons and High Templar, with Corsairs for air control. Liquipedia's Corsair/Dark Templar page also describes a late-game Corsair/Dark Templar mix used against Ultralisk/Zergling armies.

## Carriers

Carriers are rare in PvZ because Hydralisks are very effective against them, especially under Dark Swarm, which protects the Hydralisks from Interceptors.

## Expansion Wars

Protoss needs to keep expanding to afford its expensive army. Cannons at outlying bases, Storm drops on Drone lines and Corsair hunts on Overlords slow Zerg's economy.

## Common Mistakes

- Fighting under Dark Swarm with Dragoons instead of Storm and melee units.
- Running out of High Templar energy in the middle of a fight.
- Falling behind on bases.
