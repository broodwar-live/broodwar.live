Late-game TvP adds Arbiters and Carriers to the Protoss army. Terran keeps the mech core but needs Science Vessels and enough anti-air to handle them.

## Arbiters

The Arbiter (100 minerals, 350 gas) cloaks nearby friendly units and has two researched spells:

- **Recall** (150 energy) teleports Protoss units in a 5×5 area to the Arbiter after a short delay; it can be stopped by killing the Arbiter during that delay. A Recall into an undefended base can end the game.
- **Stasis Field** (100 energy) freezes units in an area; frozen units cannot move, attack or be attacked, which splits Terran's army.

The Arbiter itself is not cloaked. Science Vessel **EMP Shockwave** sets energy (and shields) to zero in its area, so Terrans often keep Vessels near home to EMP an incoming Arbiter before it can Recall, and send Vessels to the front to deny Stasis.

## Carriers

Carriers (300 HP, 150 shields) launch Interceptors from up to 12 range; the Carrier Capacity upgrade raises the number of Interceptors from 4 to 8. Siege Tanks cannot target them, so Terran answers with Goliaths and Charon Boosters, and EMP is worth using on clumped Carriers because it strips their shields. Liquipedia notes Carriers are mostly seen against Terran on maps that favour them over Arbiters.

## Battlecruisers

In very long games Terran may add Battlecruisers; Yamato Cannon can snipe Arbiters or High Templar from range. This is a niche choice — the Vulture/Tank/Goliath army stays the backbone in most games.

## Expansion Control

With both players on many bases, Spider Mines at expansion approaches and Turrets for vision help defend against Recall and Zealot run-bys, while Terran keeps advancing its tank lines.

## Common Mistakes

- Not making Science Vessels, leaving Recall and Stasis unanswered.
- Letting a Carrier fleet grow without adding Goliaths.
- Keeping the whole army in one clump where a single Stasis Field catches most of it.
