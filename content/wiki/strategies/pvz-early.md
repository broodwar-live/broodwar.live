In modern PvZ, Protoss nearly always fast-expands in order to get a second gas for the tech it needs. The early game is about placing that expansion safely and scouting what Zerg is doing.

## Forge Fast Expand

A typical start: Pylon at the natural at 8 supply (the same Probe then scouts), Forge at 11, then an order that depends on the scouted Zerg build. Examples from Liquipedia:

- vs **9 Pool**: two Cannons at 13, then Nexus, Gateway, Pylon, Assimilator and Cybernetics Core.
- vs **12 Pool**: Nexus at 15 with one Cannon and a Gateway.
- vs **12 Hatch**: Nexus at 15, Gateway at 17, Cannon at 18.

The buildings at the natural must be placed so Zerglings cannot run past them (a "sim-city" wall). The rule of thumb is to build as many Cannons as needed to be safe, but no more: the right number depends on the Zergling count, early Zergling speed and any early Hydralisk Den.

## Follow-ups

Forge FE most often transitions into a Stargate; the first Corsair takes over scouting around the time the Probe dies and may pick off an Overlord or two. Other follow-ups include timing attacks such as +1 Corsair/Speedlot.

## Threats to a Fast Expansion

All-ins can break a Forge FE: Speedling rushes, Hydralisk busts and similar attacks. Scouting the Zerg's gas timing and Zergling count tells Protoss how many Cannons it needs.

## Common Mistakes

- Leaving a gap in the wall that lets Zerglings into the natural.
- Building far more Cannons than the scouted threat requires.
- Losing the scouting Probe early and guessing the Zerg build.
