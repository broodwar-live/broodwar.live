## Overview

TvT is largely a positional matchup built around Siege Tanks. Players fight for good tank positions, use Vultures, Spider Mines, drops and Wraiths around them, and try to out-expand the opponent.

## Key Concepts

### Positional Warfare
- **Siege Tank placement** — whoever holds better siege positions (high ground, chokes, a wide arc) usually wins the fights.
- **Leapfrogging** — advancing a tank line by unsieging some tanks and re-sieging them further forward while others cover them.
- **High ground** — ground units on low ground cannot see up a cliff and need vision (from an air unit, a lifted building or a Comsat scan) to target units on the high ground. Even then, ranged attacks from lower ground have a miss chance of about 47% in the engine (a random roll of 0–255 misses at 119 or below, i.e. 120/256).

### Scouting
Lifted Barracks and Engineering Bays are staples of TvT scouting, because floating buildings have plenty of HP and can see high ground.

## Common Openings

- **1 Fact FE** — Depot, Barracks, Refinery, Factory and a Command Center around 20 supply, with a Vulture or Siege Tank first. Liquipedia calls it a safe, standard TvT opening.
- **14 CC / 13 CC** — the Command Center before the Barracks; the greediest openings. Liquipedia lists build variants named after Flash (14 CC) and Fantasy (13 CC) and notes that proxy Barracks rushes and BBS hard-counter them.
- **2 Port Wraith** — two Starports for early Wraith harassment and scouting; popularised by Flash in 2009.
- **BBS / proxy Barracks** — all-in Marine rushes that punish the greediest openings.

## Mid Game

- **Tank lines and drops** — Dropships with tanks or infantry can break an entrenched line from behind.
- **Vultures and mines** — scouting, harassment and defensive mine fields.
- **Wraiths** — vision and harassment; cloaked Wraiths force Turrets or scans.

## Late Game

- **Battlecruisers** — sometimes used to break ground stalemates; Science Vessels with EMP Shockwave answer them by draining their energy before they can fire Yamato Cannon.
- **Expansion attrition** — the player who controls more bases usually wins long games.

## Associated Players

- **Flash** — popularised 2 Port Wraith in TvT; a 14 CC variant is named after him.
- **Fantasy** — a 13 CC variant is named after him.
