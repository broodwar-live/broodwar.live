## Overview

ZvZ is the fastest and most volatile matchup. Games are often decided by Spawning Pool timing, early Zergling fights and Mutalisk control; most games stay at Lair tech.

## Key Concepts

- **Pool timing** — earlier pools get Zerglings sooner at the cost of economy.
- **Scouting with Overlords** — send the first Overlord toward likely enemy positions; what it sees at the natural and ramp reveals 12 Hatch, 12 Pool or 9 Pool.
- **Mutalisks and Scourge** — a Mutalisk has 120 HP and a Scourge deals 110 damage, so it takes two Scourge to kill a full-health Mutalisk.
- **Sunken and Spore Colonies** — placed so Drones can protect them, they buy time against Zergling floods and Mutalisks.

## Common Openings

- **Overpool** — Overlord at 9, Spawning Pool at 9, Extractor at 9, then six Zerglings. Liquipedia calls it the "perfect" standard ZvZ build: Zerglings in time to defend faster pools, Mutalisks in time to defend all-ins.
- **9 Pool** — Spawning Pool at 9 supply, then Extractor and Overlord; usually followed by Zergling speed and Lair. Strong against 12 Hatch, because the Zerglings arrive long before the opponent's Pool finishes.
- **12 Pool** — a more economic Pool, often followed by a Hatchery.
- **4/5 Pool** — an extreme all-in; Liquipedia notes it is used least against Zerg, since a pool-first opponent can hold it.

## Mid Game

- **Mutalisk fights** — stacked Mutalisks fight as one unit; the side with more Mutalisks or better control usually wins the air.
- **Scourge** — cheap and deadly against Mutalisks, but fragile (25 HP).
- **Spores** — protect Drones while building up Mutalisks.
- **Expansions** — the player with air control can deny the opponent's new Hatcheries.

## Late Game

Longer ZvZ games move toward Hydralisks, Lurkers and occasionally Hive tech; Liquipedia's Plague page notes that Defilers are uncommon in ZvZ but Plague combined with Dark Swarm and upgraded Zerglings can be decisive.
