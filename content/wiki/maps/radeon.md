**Radeon** is a 4-player map on the Space Platform tileset, made by 양창목 (Waldstein) with input from professional players including Light, JyJ, Mind, herO, Soulkey, Bisu, Snow and 910. It is 128x128 with start locations at 1, 5, 7 and 11 o'clock. The map was presented by AMD, which explains its name.

Radeon was used in ASL Season 17 and the 2024 SSL Autumn, and again in ASL Season 19.

Each main has 9 mineral patches and a 5000-gas geyser, and the natural has 7 patches and one geyser. The 6 and 12 o'clock bases have a reduced 3500-gas geyser. Version 1.1 fixed pathing and cannon-rush issues around the 11 and 5 o'clock naturals; version 1.2 improved mining rates.
