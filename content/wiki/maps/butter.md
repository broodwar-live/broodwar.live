**Butter** is a 2-player map on the Desert tileset, made by 정호진 (Hozzi). It is 112x128 with start locations at 1 and 5 o'clock. The name is a nod to the BTS song of the same name.

Butter was introduced in February 2022 for ASL Season 13. Version 2.0c, used in ASL Season 14, changed several resource placements and terrain details, including better-protected geysers at the 11 o'clock expansions and a more isolated 9 o'clock base.
