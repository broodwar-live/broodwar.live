**Sylphid** is a 3-player map on the Jungle World tileset, made by LatiAs (주현서). It is 128x128 with start locations at 4, 8 and 12 o'clock.

Sylphid was used in ASL Season 6 (September-October 2018). Its revision **Neo Sylphid** became a ladder map and was used in ASL Season 9 and KSL Season 4.

The start locations and expansions are arranged roughly along a circle around the center, and the map is almost fully ringed by impassable terrain, which suits drops and Arbiter recalls along the edge. The center has no resources and is largely covered in unbuildable debris, which stops Terran from placing turrets and depots along tank lines there; even the inner expansions have patches of unbuildable ground that make static defence harder.
