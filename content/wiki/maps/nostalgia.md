**Nostalgia** is a 4-player map on the Badlands tileset, made by 변종석 (Rose.of.Dream.). It is 128x128 with start locations at 1, 5, 7 and 11 o'clock.

Nostalgia was used from March 2003 to January 2005, including the 2004 Gillette OSL and the 2004 SKY Proleague. It is one of the best-known "old school" maps.

Nostalgia was the first major map whose natural expansion had no Vespene geyser, which made low-gas styles central, especially in PvZ. The natural is reached over two narrow bridges and has a second entrance that must also be defended.
