**Neo Jade** is the revised version of **Jade**, a 4-player map on the Twilight tileset made by 변종석 (Rose.of.Dream.). It is 128x128 with start locations at 1, 5, 7 and 11 o'clock.

Jade was introduced in late 2011 for the 2011-12 SK Planet Proleague Season 1; Neo Jade replaced it in Proleague Season 2 in 2012. The map was later used in the TeamLiquid Legacy Starleague and BSL.

It resembles standard four-corner maps such as Fighting Spirit and Circuit Breakers, but the mains are on low ground, which pushes players to take their naturals early to avoid being contained below their ramp. A long ridge connects the natural to an easy third base whose geyser holds only 1500 gas, so players wanting a full third gas must expand farther away.
