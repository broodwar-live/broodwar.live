**Vermeer** is a 4-player map on the Jungle World tileset, made by LatiAs (주현서) together with the players Mini, Rush and herO. It is 128x128 with start locations at 1, 5, 7 and 11 o'clock. Like Radeon, it was presented by AMD.

Vermeer was introduced in February 2022 for ASL Season 13. The entrance to each natural cannot be completely walled off.
