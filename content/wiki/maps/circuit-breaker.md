**Circuit Breakers** (often written "Circuit Breaker"; Korean 써킷브레이커) is a 4-player map on the Space Platform tileset, made by Earthattack (김응서). It is 128x128 with start locations at 1, 5, 7 and 11 o'clock.

It was one of the new maps of the 2010-11 Shinhan Bank Proleague and was also used in the 2010-11 PDPop MSL and the 2011 ABC Mart MSL. After the KeSPA era it remained a standard map: it appeared in the VANT36.5 National Starleague, several ASL seasons (1, 2, 3 and 6), KSL Seasons 1-4 and BSL, and together with Fighting Spirit it became a fixture of the StarCraft: Remastered ladder.

The map has 16 bases. Each main (9 mineral patches, one geyser) has a single ramp down to a wallable natural; each natural is joined to a partly cliffed mineral-only third, and the fourth bases sit at 3, 6, 9 and 12 o'clock. The large mains make drops a constant threat, and the high ground in front of the third is an important position. It is regarded as one of the more balanced maps in Brood War history.
