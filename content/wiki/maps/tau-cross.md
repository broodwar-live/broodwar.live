**Tau Cross** is a 3-player map on the Ice tileset, made by 변종석 (Rose.of.Dream.). It is 128x128; Liquipedia gives the start locations as 1, 5 and 9 o'clock (TLPD lists 2, 5 and 10).

Tau Cross debuted in the 2006 SKY Proleague and the 2006 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 2 and was used until September 2007. It returned from January to April 2009 for the 2009 Shinhan Bank Winners League and GOMTV Classic Season 3, and the WCG 2008 Grand Final in Cologne also used it. Later it appeared in BSL and ASL Season 4.

There is no ramp between main and natural; the path between them can be blocked by a single Supply Depot, which opened up cheese options especially against Zerg. The natural's small choke makes fast expansions easy to defend, the naturals are cliffed and vulnerable to tank drops, and the open, buildable center favours flanks and lets Terran build turrets while pushing. Rush distances are slightly longer than usual.
