**Plasma** is a 3-player map on the Badlands tileset, made by Earthattack (김응서). It is 96x128 with start locations at 3, 7 and 11 o'clock.

Plasma was used in the 2008 Incruit OSL (August-November 2008). After years out of use, it was chosen for ASL Season 10 in 2020 and also became a StarCraft: Remastered ladder map.

It is a semi-island map. Each player's main, natural and third sit in one large pocket whose exits to the other pockets and to the center are blocked by neutral Zerg eggs that must be destroyed; mineral patches beside the eggs let workers slip through for scouting. The mains are small with a narrow ramp, the naturals have minerals only, and each player's fourth base lies in a middle pocket. Some considered it Terran-favoured against Zerg.
