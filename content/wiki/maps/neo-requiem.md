**Neo Requiem** is the revised version of **Requiem**, a 4-player map on the Ash World tileset made by 지근영 (DreamReaver) and 변종석 (Rose.of.Dream.). It is 128x128 with start locations at 3, 6, 9 and 12 o'clock.

Requiem was used in 2004-2005, including the 2004 EVER OSL and the 2004-05 IOPS OSL. Neo Requiem was played extensively in 2005, for example in the SKY Proleague, and was brought back from October 2008 to January 2009 for the 2008-09 Shinhan Bank Proleague and GOMTV Classic Season 2.

A large cross of minerals divides the center. The corner bases are not true islands: they can be reached by mining through or slipping past mineral lines. The ramp from the center leads down into each main, which makes breaking out of a contain very hard, and the main choke is wider than on most maps of its time.
