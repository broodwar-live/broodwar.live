**Python** is a 4-player map on the Jungle World tileset, made by Terrance. It is 128x128. Revisions 1.1 to 1.3 followed the original.

Python was used in Korean competition from April 2007 to February 2008, including GOMTV MSL Season 3 and the 2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague, and it became one of the most popular maps on Battle.net.

Its layout is similar to Lost Temple, but it was designed to fix several of that map's imbalances. The island expansions in two corners are blocked by a mineral patch holding only 8 minerals, tanks can hit the mineral-only expansions from behind a ridge, and the 11 and 5 o'clock expansions have very wide chokes. A python is drawn in the center of the map.
