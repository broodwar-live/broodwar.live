**Hitchhiker** is a 2-player map on the Ice tileset, made by 조승연 (Forgotten_). It is 128x96 with start locations at 5 and 11 o'clock.

The map was used from December 2006 to August 2007 in OnGameNet events: the 2006-07 Shinhan Bank OSL Season 3, the 2007 Daum OSL, the OnGameNet Dual Tournaments and the Star Challenge. It returned in 2020 as one of the maps of ASL Season 9.

A narrow valley forms the direct path to the enemy base and can be covered by tanks placed beside it. Many areas are blocked off by neutral buildings (Psi Disruptors, Xel'Naga Temples and Power Generators) that act as walls and chokes. The bases are close together, which made cheese strategies common.
