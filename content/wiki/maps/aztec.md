**Aztec** is a 3-player map on the Jungle World tileset, made by 우상희 (Str18-02). It is 128x128 with start locations at 5, 8 and 12 o'clock.

Aztec was introduced for the 2010-11 Shinhan Bank Proleague and was also played in the 2010-11 Bacchus OSL and the 2011 Winners League, staying in use until mid-2011. Because Terran struggled on the original version, the map was revised (as Neo Aztec) for Round 5 of that Proleague season to improve TvP. It was later picked up again by community leagues such as BSL.

An unusual feature is that the main bases sit on low ground while the natural, third and fourth bases are on high ground.
