**Andromeda** is a 4-player map on the Space Platform tileset, made by Earthattack (김응서). It measures 128x128 and has start locations at 2, 4, 8 and 10 o'clock.

The map entered the Shinhan Bank Proleague in April 2008 and stayed in rotation until the 2009 Winners League ended in March 2009. Over that time it was also used in individual leagues such as the 2008 Incruit OSL and several GOMTV Classic seasons. Later it reappeared in community events, including ASL Season 3 and BSL.

Andromeda is a macro-oriented map: each main has a protected mineral-only expansion between the main and the natural, the center is fairly open, and there are two island expansions. The islands and the safe mineral-only made Corsair/Reaver a popular Protoss approach against Zerg on this map, and Zerg players in turn developed anti-air answers built around Scourge and Mutalisks.
