**Fighting Spirit** (Korean: 투혼) is a 4-player map on the Jungle World tileset, made by 변종석 (Rose.of.Dream.). It is 128x128 with start locations at 1, 5, 7 and 11 o'clock. During pre-season map testing it was called **Silver Wing** (은빛 날개).

The map was introduced for the 2009-10 Shinhan Bank Proleague and in that season was also used in individual leagues such as the EVER OSL 2009, the NATE MSL, the Korean Air OSL 2010 Season 1, the Hana Daetoo Securities MSL and the Bigfile MSL. After the KeSPA era it became the standard map of the community: it has been used in many ASL seasons (including Seasons 1, 4 and 11), in KSL Seasons 1-4 and in BSL, and it is a long-running StarCraft: Remastered ladder map.

The middle expansion has two geysers but is risky to hold, the natural has little room behind its mineral line for anti-air defence, and the third gas base is protected by two narrow ramps. Version 1.3 allowed a 2-Depot, 1-Barracks wall in the main, and version 1.4 adjusted resources at 7 and 11 o'clock.
