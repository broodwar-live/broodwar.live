**Persona** is a 4-player map on the Badlands tileset, made by 조승연 (Forgotten_). TLPD lists it as 128x128 with start locations at 2, 5, 8 and 11 o'clock.

Persona was used only in the 2007 EVER OSL (September-December 2007), and only 14 televised games were played on it.

Its defining feature is 17 neutral Dark Swarms placed across the map, most importantly over the wide ramps leading to the naturals and over the mineral lines of every expansion other than the mains and naturals. The choke between main and natural is small enough to be closed by a single Pylon.
