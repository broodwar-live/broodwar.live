**Roadkill** is a 4-player map on the Badlands tileset, made by the foreign mapmaker Freakling. It is 128x128 with start locations at 3, 6, 9 and 12 o'clock.

Roadkill was added to the iCCup map pack and in 2018 became a ladder map and a map for Korea StarCraft League (KSL) Season 1, the first foreign-made map used in a major Korean tournament. It was also used in KSL Season 2 and BSL.

As on Icarus, the mains sit near the middle of the map's sides rather than in the corners. The mains are on low ground and the naturals on high ground. The center, marked by a giant skeleton doodad, is almost fully buildable. A mineral-only expansion in front of each natural can be attacked from the low ground below, and the third gas bases are in the corners, connected by narrow bridges.
