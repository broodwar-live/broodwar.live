**Eddy** (full name *In the Way of an Eddy*) is a 4-player map on the Twilight tileset, made by the foreign mapmaker Freakling. It is 128x128 with start locations at 1, 5, 7 and 11 o'clock.

Eddy was added to the iCCup map pack in March 2015. In August 2018 it was announced as a ladder map and as a map for Korea StarCraft League (KSL) Season 2, making it the second foreign-made map, after Roadkill, to be used in a major Korean tournament. An updated version returned for KSL Season 3 in 2019. It has also been used in BSL.

The mains are on low ground, so players move up a ramp to their natural. The open center is shaped by four long, twisted radial ridges that give the map its name. The geysers at the bases outside the naturals hold only 3000 gas, and island expansions near the natural require destroying a neutral building first.
