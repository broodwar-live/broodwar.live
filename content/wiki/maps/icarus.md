**Icarus** is a 4-player map on the Space Platform tileset, made by 변종석 (Rose.of.Dream.). It is 128x128. Unlike most 4-player maps, its mains sit near the middle of the map's sides rather than in the corners.

Icarus was one of the new maps of the 2010-11 Shinhan Bank Proleague and was also used in the 2010-11 Bacchus OSL and the 2011 Winners League, from October 2010 to July 2011.
