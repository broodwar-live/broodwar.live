**Grand Line** is a 4-player map on the Desert tileset, made by 우상희 (Str18-02). TLPD lists it as 128x128 with start locations at 1, 5, 7 and 11 o'clock.

Grand Line was added to the 2009-10 Shinhan Bank Proleague in Round 4 together with Polaris Rhapsody, replacing Neo Moon Glaive and New Tornado. A revised **Grand Line SE** was introduced in Round 5. The map was used from April 2010 to January 2011, when La Mancha replaced it in the 2010-11 Proleague.

As on Andromeda, the spawn combination matters a lot, because the paths between different start locations cross different terrain. The center expansions are reduced, with 500-mineral patches and a 2000-gas geyser.
