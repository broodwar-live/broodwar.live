**Neo Moon Glaive** is the revised version of **Moon Glaive**, a 3-player map on the Twilight tileset made by 우상희 (Str18-02). It is 128x128 with start locations at 1, 4 and 8 o'clock.

Moon Glaive was selected for the 2009-10 Shinhan Bank Proleague after pre-season map testing and was updated to Neo Moon Glaive in the second round of that season. It was used from October 2009 to April 2010 (including the 2010 Winners League) and was replaced by Grand Line in Round 4.

The naturals have multiple entrances, which made Protoss fast expansions harder to hold. Three raised "watch tower" areas sit in the map, but their ramps were removed in later versions. Because of the clockwise spawn arrangement, a player's third base is often another start location.
