The most economically greedy TvT opening — Flash's 14 CC puts a command center at the natural on 14 supply, before any barracks. It is a rare opening rather than a standard one: it sacrifices early defense for a significant economic advantage and is only feasible depending on the circumstances and the map.

After the command center, wall the natural with a barracks and supply depot, take gas, and add a factory around 21 supply with a bunker to complete the wall if needed. The critical window is the early game — high SCV hit points and the option of a bunker let you hold a 1 Rax FE bunker rush, but if you survive you are in a significant economic lead and should use it for map control and a faster third base.

13/14 CC is strongest on large maps and 4+ player maps such as God's Garden, Andromeda and Colosseum II, where rush distances give natural safety. It soft-counters 1 Fact FE. It is hard-countered by proxy 5 Rax, proxy 8 Rax, BBS and (map dependent) a double expand. The most common follow-ups are 2 Port Wraith or a factory-based transition into tank/goliath or tank/vulture play.
