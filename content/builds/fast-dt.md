A high-variance PvP build that techs to dark templar off one base, betting that the opponent will not have detection when they arrive. In the mirror, detection comes from observers and from photon cannons — the defender can rely on cannons as well as a Robotics Facility and Observatory. If the opponent has neither in time, DTs are invisible and lethal.

Rush Citadel of Adun and Templar Archives behind a few zealots and dragoons, aiming for the first two DTs around the six-minute mark. Send them into the opponent's base immediately to kill probes, units, tech or pylons — deal maximum damage but do not lose them. Meanwhile add a Forge, take the natural Nexus and protect it with photon cannons, so your economy stays level while your tech is ahead.

Do not use it against a fast expansion already secured with cannons — the DTs arrive too late and your own Nexus is delayed. Dragoon/observer builds off two or three gateways can soft-counter it, while a four-gateway dragoon timing is hard-countered by DTs. If both players go DT, games often become base trades. Maps with difficult pathing like Blue Storm or easily defended naturals like Destination support the build.
