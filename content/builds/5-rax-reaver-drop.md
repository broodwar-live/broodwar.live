One of the most traditional forms of Protoss harassment in PvT: a one-gateway opening into Robotics Facility, Shuttle and Reaver, aimed at the Terran mineral line. It is a rather aggressive opening, not an all-in — it is designed to deal heavy economic damage, and the immediate threat of the Reaver often keeps Terran from attacking early.

A conservative version goes gateway, cybernetics core, 4–5 dragoons with range, Robotics Facility at 200 gas, an Observatory around 34 supply and a Nexus at the natural around 37 supply. When the Robotics Facility finishes, build a Shuttle and Reaver as fast as possible — the sooner the harass begins, the less likely Terran has turrets up. Shuttle micro is key: drop, fire scarabs, and pick the Reaver back up before marines can focus it.

If Terran plays aggressively, the Reaver may have to stay home and defend; against a Terran drop or Wraith opening (its hard counter) the harass is easily shut down, for example by a single Wraith. Do not force a drop into turrets — use the Reaver to support the ground army instead, and expand once or twice depending on the damage dealt. It soft-counters FD Terran and 2 Factory vulture openings.
