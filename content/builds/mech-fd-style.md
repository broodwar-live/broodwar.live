FD — "Fake Double" — is a TvP opening created and popularized by Midas around 2004. It fakes a 2 Factory push: Terran trains four to eight marines, one siege tank and at least two early vultures with spider mines, drives back the early dragoons, and builds a command center at the natural while the attack moves out. On small maps it initially overwhelmed many Protoss players, who adapted within months.

The standard version takes a depot, then barracks and refinery, cuts gas briefly around 88, adds a factory and machine shop, and builds a siege tank and spider mine research before a vulture joins the push; the command center goes down at 400 minerals. Not losing the attack force is paramount — vultures must mine the paths between the bases, since Protoss cannot clear spider mines without observers. [Flash](@/players/flash.md) later developed the "Strong FD" and Flash variations, which fake the first attack and then hit with more vultures, marines and tanks while Protoss techs or expands.

FD works on almost any map and remains a popular way to secure a fast expansion on maps with long rush distances and wide chokes; difficult pathing and narrow chokes, as on Blue Storm or Destination, make it harder. Conservative two-gateway builds, delayed reaver drops and 13 Nexus are safe responses, and Bulldog variations punish a failed attack. Two-base Arbiter and dark templar play can also cause problems.
