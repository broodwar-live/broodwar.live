Named after [Bisu](@/players/bisu.md), who debuted it in the finals of the 2006 GOMTV MSL Season 1, beating reigning champion [sAviOr](@/players/savior.md) 3-0. At the time PvZ seemed dominated by Zerg, and the final is widely regarded as one of the most influential ever, helping Protoss regain strength. The build is a follow-up to a Forge fast expansion: Stargate into Corsairs, which clear overlords to remove Zerg's mobile detection, followed by Dark Templar.

The critical sequence: the first Corsair scouts, then Citadel of Adun at 100 gas and Templar Archives, with Corsairs produced constantly. Do not trade Corsairs for overlords early — they are also your defense against mutalisks; once you have a critical mass, snipe stray overlords. The first two Dark Templar deny a Zerg bust or contain and harass expansions when detection is gone, but should not be lost.

The build requires a single wall that shuts off main and natural, and favors two-player maps. Transition into more gateways for a two-base attack or a safe third base, or toward High Templar and storm. It was eventually countered by 3 Base Spire into 5 Hatch Hydra, and today the +1 Sair/Speedlot ("Neo Bisu Build") is the more modern response.
