An air-control strategy that opens with two starports producing wraiths, popularized in TvT by Flash. The goal is to use wraiths — cloaked as soon as possible — to pick off SCVs, snipe exposed units and kill floating buildings while the opponent scrambles for detection.

Build a barracks and factory, then two starports around 22 supply. Because you make few early marines, a wall may be necessary against 2 Factory vultures. Once the first wraiths are out, build a Control Tower add-on on one starport to research Cloaking Field while the other keeps producing. A few marines and a vulture can move out early to clear enemy marines so they cannot shoot down your wraiths. Never let turrets finish if you can stop them.

Wraiths are not an end-game strategy — after the harass, add factories and use the wraiths for scouting and killing floating buildings while you get tanks. Maps with cliffs behind the mineral line (like Neo Medusa) favor the harass; open mineral lines like Python make it harder. The usual Terran response is goliaths and turrets for detection; if the build is scouted too early, you are in serious trouble.
