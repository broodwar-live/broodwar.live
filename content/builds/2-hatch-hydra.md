An aggressive two-base hydralisk timing attack in ZvP. The idea is to hide the lack of a third hatchery, mass range-upgraded hydralisks from two hatcheries, and snipe the Protoss natural Nexus. This is an all-in build — if the push fails to kill a lot of probes or take down the Nexus, Zerg has effectively lost.

Open with anything from 9 pool speed to 12 hatch, and put down an extractor right after the natural hatchery. The Hydralisk Den does not require Lair — build it at 50 gas, research range at 150 gas, then produce hydralisks non-stop and add speed with the next 150 gas. Kill the scouting probe so Protoss does not notice the missing third hatchery. Move out once you have 12 hydralisks: snipe any cannons that can reach your hydras, then hold position on the Nexus while reinforcements stream in.

Best on maps where the natural Nexus can be sniped by ranged hydras, such as Destination. If Protoss responds with a mass of cannons, back off and macro — Protoss is confined to one base with heavy mineral investment in static defense. Weak against a well-designed Sim-City (e.g. on Medusa) and against 1 Gate Tech, in which case you should adjust your build.
