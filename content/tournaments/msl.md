The MBCGame StarCraft League (MSL) was the individual Brood War league of the Korean cable channel MBCGame and, alongside the OSL, one of the two premier individual tournaments of the KeSPA era. It began in 2002 as the KPGA Tour, took the MSL name in 2003, and ended when MBCGame ceased operations in February 2012; the last edition was the 2011 ABC Mart MSL.

Savior won three MSLs (UZOO 2005 and both Pringles seasons in 2006), and Flash won three of the last four editions. Finals were best-of-five except the You Are The Golf King MSL, which used a best-of-seven final. The Stout MSL score is omitted: Liquipedia records the final as 3–0 with one of those games counted as a bye.
