Mihu won BSL Season 13, beating Dewalt 4–1 in the final on 19 December 2021. The season ran from 30 October to 19 December 2021 with a prize pool of US$5,500.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. Sziky and gypsy lost in the semifinals, and gypsy took third place by beating Sziky.
