Bonyth won BSL Season 15, beating Dewalt 4–2 in the grand final on 31 July 2022. The season ran from 11 June to 31 July 2022 with a prize pool of US$4,000.

The playoffs used a double-elimination bracket with upper and lower halves. Dewalt came through the upper bracket and so began the grand final one game ahead; that game is counted in the score.
