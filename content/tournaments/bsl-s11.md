Bonyth won BSL Season 11, beating TerrOr 4–1 in the final on 3 April 2021. The season ran from 26 February to 3 April 2021 with a prize pool of US$5,000.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. Avi-Love and OyA lost in the semifinals, and OyA took third place by beating Avi-Love.
