The OnGameNet Starleague (OSL) was the individual Brood War league of the Korean cable channel OnGameNet. Its Brood War editions ran from 2000 to 2012, each named after its title sponsor, and together with the MBCGame league (MSL) it formed the pair of top individual tournaments of the KeSPA era.

## Golden Mouse

A player who won three OSL titles received a Golden Mouse trophy. Four players earned one in Brood War:

- **NaDa** — Panasonic 2002, IOPS 2004, Shinhan Bank Season 2 2006
- **July** — Gillette 2004, EVER 2005, EVER 2008
- **Jaedong** — EVER 2007, BATOO 2008, Bacchus 2009
- **Flash** — Bacchus 2008, EVER 2009, Korean Air Season 2 2010

The table lists editions by sponsor name; finals were best-of-five. Some histories also count earlier 1999 events as the first OSL — those are not included here.
