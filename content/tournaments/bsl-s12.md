TerrOr won BSL Season 12, beating Bonyth 4–3 in the final on 30 May 2021. The season ran from 30 April to 30 May 2021 with a prize pool of US$5,000.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. Sziky and trutaCz lost in the semifinals, and trutaCz took third place by beating Sziky.
