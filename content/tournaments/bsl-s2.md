trutaCz won BSL Season 2, beating ZZZero 4–2 in the grand final. Like the first season, it was open only to players from Poland. The season ran from 26 July to 6 October 2013 with a prize pool of PLN 750.

The playoffs used a double-elimination bracket with upper and lower halves.
