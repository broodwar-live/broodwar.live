Mihu won BSL Season 16, beating Bonyth 5–0 in the grand final on 26 March 2023. The season ran from 7 January to 26 March 2023 with a prize pool of US$6,000.

The top six players of a round-robin Round of 8 went into a stepladder playoff: the lowest seeds met first, and each winner moved up to face the next seed. Bonyth, the top seed, waited in the grand final.
