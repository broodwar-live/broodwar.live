Bonyth won BSL Season 10, beating Dewalt 4–2 in the grand final on 8 November 2020. The season ran from 19 September to 8 November 2020 with a prize pool of US$5,000.

The top six players of a round-robin Round of 8 went into a stepladder playoff: the lowest seeds met first, and each winner moved up to face the next seed. Dewalt, the top seed, waited in the grand final.
