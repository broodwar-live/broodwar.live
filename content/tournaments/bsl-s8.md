Bonyth won BSL Season 8, beating g0rynich 4–2 in the final on 16 February 2020. The season ran from 4 January to 16 February 2020 with a prize pool of US$2,000.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. Dandy and TT1 lost in the semifinals, and TT1 took third place by beating Dandy.
