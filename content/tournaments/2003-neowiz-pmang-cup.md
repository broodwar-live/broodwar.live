The Neowiz Pmang Cup was OnGameNet's second Proleague, played with the same format as the KTF EVER Cup: two rounds of best-of-three matches (two 1v1 sets and one 2v2 set, all three always played), then a best-of-five playoff and a best-of-seven final.

Semifinal: SUMA GO beat Hanbit Stars 3–0. In the final on 29 February 2004, SUMA GO beat Toona SG 4–1 to win the title.
