The last MBCGame team league before the Proleague was merged across both channels. Eight teams played a fifteen-week league, after which the top teams met in a stepladder playoff of best-of-seven all-kill matches. Matches were played in the all-kill format: the winner of each set stayed on to face the next player of the losing team, so the set lists below show who knocked out whom in order.

First round: STX SouL beat KTF MagicNs 4–2. Semifinal: Pantech & Curitel Curriors beat STX SouL 4–3. In the final on 6 March 2005, GO beat Pantech & Curitel Curriors 4–1 to win the title.

Liquipedia's season summary describes a double-elimination bracket, but its match records show a league followed by a stepladder playoff; the match records are followed here. Set lists follow Liquipedia's match records. Players without a profile on this site are shown as recorded and are not linked; races are given per set as recorded.
