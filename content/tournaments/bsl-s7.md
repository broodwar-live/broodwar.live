trutaCz won BSL Season 7, beating eOnzErG 4–2 in the final on 3 November 2019. The season ran from 14 September to 3 November 2019 with a prize pool of US$1,000.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. Jaeyun and Bonyth lost in the semifinals, and Bonyth took third place by beating Jaeyun.
