In Round 2 of the 2005 SKY Proleague ten teams played a round robin of best-of-five matches over sixteen weeks. The top four entered a stepladder playoff, and both finalists qualified for the 2005 Grand Final.

First round: Samsung KHAN beat GO 4–3. Semifinal: Samsung KHAN beat KTF MagicNs 4–0. In the final on 21 January 2006, SK Telecom T1 beat Samsung KHAN 4–3 to win the title. iloveoov was named Finals MVP.

Liquipedia records which team won each set of these playoff matches but not the players or maps, so only the match scores are given here. Set lists follow Liquipedia's match records. Players without a profile on this site are shown as recorded and are not linked; races are given per set as recorded.
