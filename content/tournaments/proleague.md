The Proleague was the Korean team league of professional Brood War. Organised by KeSPA and broadcast by OnGameNet (and, from 2005, jointly with MBCGame), it pitted the corporate-sponsored pro teams against each other in matches of several sets: a team match was won by the first side to take the majority of the sets, and in later seasons a series level after the regular sets went to an ace match whose players were named only once it was needed. Early seasons mixed 1v1 and 2v2 sets; from 2009–10 every set was a 1v1 game.

The format changed almost every year. OnGameNet's first editions in 2003 were cups named after their sponsors. The 2004 to 2007 seasons were split into rounds, each with its own final, and a grand final between the round winners decided the overall champion. The 2008 season was a single league with a stepladder playoff, and from 2008–09 the league ran as a long season of five or six rounds with a post-season playoff. SK Planet Proleague 2011–12 Season 2 was played partly in StarCraft II; from 2012–13 the Proleague was a StarCraft II league.
