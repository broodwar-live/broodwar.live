Season 2 of the 2011–12 SK Planet Proleague combined both games. Every team match had a best-of-three Brood War half and a best-of-three StarCraft II half; a team that won both halves took the match, and split halves went to a StarCraft II ace match. Only the Brood War sets are listed here; match scores count the sets won in both games, and the StarCraft II sets are noted under each set list.

First round: SK Telecom T1 beat Team 8 2–0 in team matches. Semifinal: CJ Entus beat SK Telecom T1 2–0 in team matches. In the final on 22 September 2012, CJ Entus beat Samsung KHAN 4–1 to win the title.

Liquipedia records the date of each playoff tie but not of its individual matches; both matches of a tie are shown with that date. Set lists follow Liquipedia's match records. Players without a profile on this site are shown as recorded and are not linked; races are given per set as recorded.
