Mihu won BSL Season 14, beating Dewalt 4–3 in the final on 10 April 2022. The season ran from 28 January to 10 April 2022 with a prize pool of US$5,000.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. Zhanhun and Sziky lost in the semifinals, and Sziky took third place by beating Zhanhun.
