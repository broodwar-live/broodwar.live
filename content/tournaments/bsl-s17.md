Mihu won the season, beating Dewalt in the grand final on 3 December 2023. Mihu reached the grand final from the winners' bracket and so started with a one-game advantage; he took four of the five maps played, for a 5–1 result.

The season ran from 7 October to 3 December 2023 with a prize pool of US$5,500. Two double-elimination group rounds cut 28 players down to eight, who then played a double-elimination playoff. Mihu edged Bonyth 4–3 in the upper final, while Dewalt recovered from an upper-semifinal loss to Bonyth, won three lower-bracket series and swept Bonyth 4–0 in the lower final to reach the grand final.
