Dandy won BSL Season 4, beating kogeT 4–3 in the final on 16 June 2018. The season ran from 17 March to 16 June 2018 with a prize pool of US$1,000.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. Dewalt and eOnzErG lost in the semifinals, and eOnzErG took third place by beating Dewalt.
