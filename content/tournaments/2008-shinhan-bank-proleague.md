The 2008 Shinhan Bank Proleague was a short transitional season between the round-based Proleague of 2004–2007 and the long 2008–09 season. Twelve teams played each other twice in best-of-five matches (1v1, 1v1, 2v2, 1v1 and a 1v1 ace match), and the top four entered a stepladder playoff of best-of-seven matches in which the seventh set was the ace match.

Samsung KHAN won the regular season at 16–6 and waited in the final. OnGameNet SPARKYZ, third on set difference, beat STX SouL 4–1 and then knocked out SK Telecom T1 4–3, with Luxury defeating Bisu in the ace match. In the final on 9 August 2008, Leta gave SPARKYZ the first set, but Samsung KHAN took the next four, and firebathero was named Finals MVP. KTF MagicNs' Flash, who led the regular season in wins, was the season MVP.
