In the 2010–11 Shinhan Bank Proleague both the third and the fourth round were played in the all-kill format, and together they formed the 2011 Shinhan Bank Winners League, whose four best teams met in a stepladder playoff. Matches were played in the all-kill format: the winner of each set stayed on to face the next player of the losing team, so the set lists below show who knocked out whom in order.

First round: SK Telecom T1 beat Hite Entus 4–1. Semifinal: SK Telecom T1 beat Hwaseung OZ 4–3. In the final on 9 April 2011, SK Telecom T1 beat KT Rolster 4–1 to win the title.

Liquipedia labels these playoff matches best of five, but the winners took four sets each; they are shown as best of seven. Set lists follow Liquipedia's match records. Players without a profile on this site are shown as recorded and are not linked; races are given per set as recorded.
