Before the two broadcasters merged their team competitions into a single Proleague in 2005, MBCGame ran its own team league alongside OnGameNet's. The first edition in 2003 used ordinary matches; from the LifeZone edition onward every match was played in the all-kill format, in which the winner of a set stays on until beaten — the format later revived inside the Proleague as the Winners League.
