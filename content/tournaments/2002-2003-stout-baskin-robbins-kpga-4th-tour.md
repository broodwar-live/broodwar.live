NaDa won the 2002-2003 Stout & Baskin Robbins KPGA 4th Tour, defeating ChoJJa 3–2 in the final on 18 January 2003. JinNam and kOs went out in the semifinals.

The playoffs were single elimination from the quarterfinals.
