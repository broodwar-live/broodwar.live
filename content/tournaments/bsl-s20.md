Bonyth won the season, beating Dewalt 5–4 in a best-of-nine grand final on 6 July 2025. Bonyth started with a one-game winners'-bracket advantage; the eight maps played were split 4–4, with Bonyth winning the last three after trailing 1–4 in maps.

The season ran from 26 April to 6 July 2025 with a prize pool of US$3,000. Two group rounds cut 36 players down to a 16-player double-elimination playoff, with eight players starting in the upper bracket and eight in the lower. Bonyth beat Sziky, Dewalt and Hawk to win the upper bracket. Dewalt, after losing to Bonyth in the upper semifinals, won three straight lower-bracket series without dropping a map, including a 4–0 over Hawk in the lower final.
