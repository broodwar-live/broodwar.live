The first round of the 2004 SKY Proleague was the first Proleague sponsored by SKY and the third broadcast by OnGameNet. Eleven teams played a single round robin of best-of-three matches, always in the order 2v2, 1v1, 1v1, and the two teams with the most match wins met in a best-of-seven final on 17 July 2004.

Hanbit Stars topped the table at 8–2 and SK Telecom T1 finished second at 7–3, ahead of Toona SG on set difference. In the final SK Telecom T1 recovered from losing the opening set to lead 3–1, but Hanbit won the last three sets to take the round 4–3. As round winner, Hanbit qualified for the 2004 Grand Final, which it also won. Plus finished last and lost its place for Round 2.
