The 2006 Grand Final was a single best-of-seven match between the winners of the season's two rounds, SK Telecom T1 (Round 1) and MBCGame HERO (Round 2).

In the final on 20 January 2007, MBCGame HERO beat SK Telecom T1 4–3 to win the title.
