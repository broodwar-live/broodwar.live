Dewalt won the season, beating Zhanhun in the grand final on 19 May 2024. Coming from the winners' bracket, Dewalt began the final one game up and won four of the five maps played, for a 5–1 result.

The season ran from 24 March to 19 May 2024 with a prize pool of US$4,000. Group rounds narrowed 28 players to eight for a double-elimination playoff. Dewalt beat Zhanhun 3–2 in the upper semifinals and Mihu 4–3 in the upper final. Zhanhun fought back through the lower bracket, beating Mihu 4–3 in the lower final to set up a second meeting with Dewalt.
