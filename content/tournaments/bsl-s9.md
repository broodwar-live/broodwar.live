Bonyth won BSL Season 9, beating trutaCz 4–0 in the final on 7 June 2020. The season ran from 17 April to 7 June 2020 with a prize pool of US$2,000.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. g0rynich and TerrOr lost in the semifinals, and g0rynich took third place by beating TerrOr.
