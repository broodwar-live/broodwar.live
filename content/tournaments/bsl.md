The Bombastic StarLeague (BSL) is a community-run tournament series organised by ZZZero and the BSL staff to support the StarCraft and StarCraft: Remastered scene outside Korea. Its first season, in early 2013, was open only to players from Poland; the league has since grown into one of the largest online Brood War competitions, with Blizzard joining as a sponsor from Season 5. Besides the main league, BSL runs side events such as a Non-Korean Championship, Nation Wars and LAN tournaments in Poland.

The table lists every completed season of the main league. Seasons 4–9 and 11–14 finished with an eight-player single-elimination playoff, Seasons 10 and 16 with a stepladder, and the others with a double-elimination bracket. In Seasons 3, 15 and 17–22 the player arriving from the winners' bracket started the grand final with a one-game advantage; that game is included in the scores shown.
