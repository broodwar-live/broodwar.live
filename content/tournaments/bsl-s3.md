Dewalt won BSL Season 3, beating TerrOr 4–2 in the grand final on 21 January 2018. Held four years after Season 2, it opened the league to players from outside Korea in general rather than only from Poland. The season ran from 25 October 2017 to 21 January 2018 with a prize pool of US$400.

The playoffs used a double-elimination bracket with upper and lower halves. Dewalt came through the upper bracket and so began the grand final one game ahead; that game is counted in the score. Matches settled by walkover are not shown in the bracket below.
