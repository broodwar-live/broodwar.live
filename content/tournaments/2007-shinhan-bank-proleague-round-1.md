The 2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague was the first sponsored by Shinhan Bank. In Round 1 twelve teams played a round robin, and the top four entered a best-of-seven stepladder playoff whose winner qualified for the 2007 Grand Final.

First round: MBCGame HERO beat STX SouL 4–2. Semifinal: Lecaf OZ beat MBCGame HERO 4–2. In the final on 4 August 2007, Samsung KHAN beat Lecaf OZ 4–0 to win the title. Stork was named Finals MVP.
