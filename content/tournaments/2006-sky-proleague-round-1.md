In Round 1 of the 2006 SKY Proleague eleven teams played a round robin of best-of-five matches (1v1, 1v1, 2v2, 1v1, 1v1). The top four entered a best-of-seven stepladder playoff, and the round winner qualified for the 2006 Grand Final.

First round: MBCGame HERO beat KTF MagicNs 4–0. Semifinal: MBCGame HERO beat CJ Entus 4–2. In the final on 29 July 2006, SK Telecom T1 beat MBCGame HERO 4–1 to win the title. Canata was named Finals MVP.
