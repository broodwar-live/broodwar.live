The KTF EVER Cup was the first Proleague broadcast by OnGameNet. Eight teams played two rounds of best-of-three matches made of two 1v1 sets and one 2v2 set, in which all three sets were played even after a team had won two; the playoffs were a stepladder of best-of-five matches ending in a best-of-seven final.

First round: KTF MagicNs beat GO 3–0. Semifinal: Orion beat KTF MagicNs 3–1. In the final on 30 August 2003, Orion beat Hanbit Stars 4–1 to win the title. sigamari was named Finals MVP.
