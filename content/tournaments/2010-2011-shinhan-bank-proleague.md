The 2010–11 Shinhan Bank Proleague was the first season after the match-fixing scandal. Hite SPARKYZ had merged into CJ Entus and eSTRO had folded, leaving ten teams, so the league switched from best-of-five to best-of-seven matches, all 1v1, and from five rounds to six; rounds 3 and 4 were played in the Winners League format, in which the winner of a set stays on for the next one. The top six teams went on to the post-season.

The post-season ties were best of three matches, except for the final, a single best-of-seven match held at Century Square in Shanghai on 19 August 2011. In the playoff matches the seventh set was the ace match, for which a team could send out a player who had already played. KT Rolster started in the first round and needed the full three matches against both STX SouL and Woongjin Stars before sweeping CJ Entus 2–0. In the final SK Telecom T1 led 3–1 after four sets, but KT won the last three: Flash beat BeSt on Fortress SE, Stats levelled the score against n.Die_soO, and Flash beat BeSt again on Circuit Breaker in the ace match. It was KT's second Proleague title in a row.
