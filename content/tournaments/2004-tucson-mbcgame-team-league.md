Three teams were seeded from the previous season and five came through a qualifier. The eight played a double-elimination bracket of best-of-seven matches, and the winning team received a Hyundai Tucson car. Matches were played in the all-kill format: the winner of each set stayed on to face the next player of the losing team, so the set lists below show who knocked out whom in order.

Upper bracket round 1: SK Telecom T1 beat Hanbit Stars 4–3. Upper bracket round 1: STX SouL beat KOR 4–2. Upper bracket round 1: KTF MagicNs beat Toona SG 4–3. Upper bracket round 1: SUMA GO beat Plus 4–2. Lower bracket round 1: Hanbit Stars beat KOR 4–1. Lower bracket round 1: Plus beat Toona SG 4–3. Upper bracket semifinals: SK Telecom T1 beat STX SouL 4–0. Upper bracket semifinals: SUMA GO beat KTF MagicNs 4–0. Lower bracket round 2: Hanbit Stars beat STX SouL 4–2. Lower bracket round 2: KTF MagicNs beat Plus 4–2. Lower bracket semifinal: KTF MagicNs beat Hanbit Stars 4–2. Upper bracket final: SUMA GO beat SK Telecom T1 4–3. Lower bracket final: SK Telecom T1 beat KTF MagicNs 4–1. In the final on 28 August 2004, SK Telecom T1 beat SUMA GO 4–3 to win the title.

Set lists follow Liquipedia's match records. Players without a profile on this site are shown as recorded and are not linked; races are given per set as recorded.
