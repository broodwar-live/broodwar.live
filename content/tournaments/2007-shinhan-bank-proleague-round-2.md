Round 2 of the 2007 Shinhan Bank Proleague again had twelve teams in a round robin, followed by a best-of-seven stepladder playoff for the top four; the winner met the Round 1 winner in the 2007 Grand Final.

First round: CJ Entus beat OnGameNet SPARKYZ 4–3. Semifinal: CJ Entus beat MBCGame HERO 4–3. In the final on 27 January 2008, Lecaf OZ beat CJ Entus 4–2 to win the title. Anytime was named Finals MVP.
