The third round of the 2009–10 Shinhan Bank Proleague was again played in the all-kill format as the 2010 Shinhan Bank Winners League, with its own stepladder playoff for the top four. Matches were played in the all-kill format: the winner of each set stayed on to face the next player of the losing team, so the set lists below show who knocked out whom in order.

First round: Woongjin Stars beat STX SouL 4–1. Semifinal: MBCGame HERO beat Woongjin Stars 4–3. In the final on 3 April 2010, KT Rolster beat MBCGame HERO 4–3 to win the title.

Set lists follow Liquipedia's match records. Players without a profile on this site are shown as recorded and are not linked; races are given per set as recorded.
