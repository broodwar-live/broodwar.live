Mihu, the Season 17 champion, won again by beating Zhanhun in the grand final on 22 December 2024. Zhanhun had reached the final from the winners' bracket and started one game ahead, but Mihu won five of the six maps played to take the series 5–2.

The season ran from 1 November to 22 December 2024 with a prize pool of US$5,000. Group rounds narrowed 28 players to eight for a double-elimination playoff. Zhanhun won all three of his upper-bracket series, edging Mihu 4–3 in the upper final. Mihu answered by beating Bonyth 4–1 in the lower final. Dandy was disqualified from the playoffs, so Bonyth and Dewalt each advanced past him by walkover; matches settled by walkover are not shown in the bracket below.
