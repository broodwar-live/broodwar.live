eOnzErG won BSL Season 5, beating Bonyth 4–2 in the final on 23 December 2018. The season ran from 9 November to 23 December 2018 with a prize pool of US$1,000.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. trutaCz and kogeT lost in the semifinals, and trutaCz took third place by beating kogeT.
