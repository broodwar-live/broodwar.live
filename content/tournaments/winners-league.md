From 2008–09 to 2010–11 one part of each Shinhan Bank Proleague season was played in the all-kill format, in which the winner of a set stays on to face the next opponent until beaten. These rounds were treated as a separate competition, the Shinhan Bank Winners League, with a stepladder playoff of their own for the top four teams, while their regular matches still counted toward the Proleague standings.
