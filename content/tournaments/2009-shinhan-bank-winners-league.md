The third round of the 2008–09 Shinhan Bank Proleague was played in the all-kill format and is treated as a league of its own, the 2009 Shinhan Bank Winners League, with a separate stepladder playoff for the top four; only the round's regular matches counted toward the Proleague standings. Matches were played in the all-kill format: the winner of each set stayed on to face the next player of the losing team, so the set lists below show who knocked out whom in order.

First round: KTF MagicNs beat SK Telecom T1 4–3. Semifinal: Hwaseung OZ beat KTF MagicNs 4–0. In the final on 28 March 2009, CJ Entus beat Hwaseung OZ 4–3 to win the title.

Set lists follow Liquipedia's match records. Players without a profile on this site are shown as recorded and are not linked; races are given per set as recorded.
