SK Telecom T1 won both rounds of the 2005 SKY Proleague, so the Grand Final field was completed by the three best teams over both rounds: KTF MagicNs, GO and Samsung KHAN. They played a best-of-seven stepladder for the right to face SK Telecom T1 in the final.

First round: GO beat Samsung KHAN 4–0. Semifinal: KTF MagicNs beat GO 4–2. In the final on 25 February 2006, SK Telecom T1 beat KTF MagicNs 4–2 to win the title.
