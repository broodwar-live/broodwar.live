Season 22 opened on 17 August 2026 with a Round of 24 split into six dual-tournament groups. Defending champion Soma, Leta and Light entered at the Round of 16 on their Season 21 results. Flash, the Season 21 runner-up, withdrew with a wrist injury, and SnOw claimed his Round of 16 place through a wild-card qualifier.

In the semifinals Rush beat Light 4–2, and Soulkey lost the first two games to Soma before winning four in a row, ending Soma's bid for a third consecutive title. The best-of-seven final between Rush and Soulkey is scheduled for 17 October 2026 at the Lotte World Ice Rink in Seoul.
