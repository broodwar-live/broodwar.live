trutaCz won BSL Season 6, beating Bonyth 4–1 in the final on 24 March 2019. The season ran from 2 February to 24 March 2019 with a prize pool of US$1,000.

The eight-player playoff was single elimination. Dewalt and DragOn lost in the semifinals, and Dewalt took third place by beating DragOn.
